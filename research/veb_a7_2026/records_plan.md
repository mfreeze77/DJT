# Records acquisition and falsification plan
**Scope:** RF-014 Toronto pathway; reported VEB–A7 secured financing; proposed continuity tests. No protected bank records or suspicious activity reports are presumed public.

## Priority 1 — Toronto: resolve the $15m allegation

**Testable hypothesis:** Some proceeds of the reported VEB-financed 2010 Zaporizhstal stake sale were transferred into Talon/Midland's Toronto project and supported fee payments to a Trump entity.

**Alternative explanations:** The sale proceeds never entered the project; a different capital source funded it; proceeds entered a Shnaider holding company but not the project; project contributions occurred but did not generate any particular Trump fee; fees were earned independently of that contribution.

**Records in order:**
1. Original 2010 Zaporizhstal stake-sale agreement, closing statement, escrow instructions, purchaser beneficial ownership and VEB buyer-financing/security documentation. Establish who bought what, who lent, who received proceeds and currency.
2. Midland/Shnaider audited financial statements, capital accounts, project funding requests, shareholder-loan registers and cash journals for 2009–2012.
3. Talon project bank statements, lender draw requests, project account pledges, intercompany receipts, deposit releases, and general ledger for same period.
4. Executed Trump brand/management/license agreements and amendments, invoices, remittance records, fee schedules, termination settlements, and any revenue-sharing rights.
5. Reconcile both sides of every alleged transfer by transaction date, payer, beneficiary, account, currency, amount, bank reference and purpose. Record negative searches and alternative financing.

**Accessible starting point:** [FTI Talon receiver archive](https://cfcanada.fticonsulting.com/talon/reports.htm), Ontario Superior Court CV-16-11573-00CL. First report para. 16 and Appendix D list a C$400m debenture and multiple project-account/security agreements; para. 17 identifies a junior Midland Resources Holding Limited charge. **Request the executed documents, not only their names.** [First report](https://cfcanada.fticonsulting.com/talon/docs/Receiver%27s%20First%20Report%20%28Security%20Opinion%2C%20Status%20Report%29.pdf).

**Public-record route:** court registry access and public filed exhibits, receiver archive, Ontario land-title registrations and PPSA searches, available insolvency dockets. Nonpublic bank statements and private contracts generally require consent, discovery, or appropriate legal process. Do not seek protected SARs.

**Court/receiver inquiry draft:**

> Re: Ontario Superior Court file CV-16-11573-00CL, Talon International Inc. et al. Please identify public filed exhibits or archived documents containing (a) the October 9, 2007 debenture and related draw schedules, (b) the registered subordinate Midland Resources Holding Limited charge, (c) project-account pledge agreements listed in Appendix D to the Receiver's First Report, (d) the original material-project-agreement assignments, and (e) receipts/disbursements reports and schedules. Please advise how to access nonsealed court filings and any copying fees. This request does not seek sealed or confidential material.

**Proof threshold:** original matching records showing actual cash movement into a named project account and onward fee effect. Do not infer from a loan face value or license existence.

## Priority 2 — A7: verify pledge, lender and actual advances

**Testable hypothesis:** VEB made one or more loans to A7 LLC secured by PSB's stake in A7.

**Source dependency:** FinCEN Federal Register NPRM (Oct. 5, 2026), footnote 23 -> CIR *A7 Abroad* (Oct. 2025), pp. 4–5 -> FrankMedia and Star-Pro reporting. This is a dependent chain, not independent triangulation.

**Records in order:**
1. Dated Russian EGRUL extracts for A7 LLC, PSB, Exim International; registration numbers, addresses, owners, directors, name changes and document dates.
2. February 2025 pledge registration or executed share pledge; identify pledgor, pledgee, secured borrower, secured obligations and enforceability dates.
3. VEB credit agreements, public loan notices, financial statements, loan schedules, collateral notes and amendments.
4. Disbursement proof: borrower financial statements, transaction entries, bank confirmations, and use-of-proceeds disclosures where lawfully obtainable.
5. Exim 75/25 claim: independent dated register extract and documents supporting its claimed operational A7 affiliation. Distinguish formal ownership from job-ad language.

**Public route:** CIR source citations; Russian corporate registries and archival extracts where accessible; sanctions notices and company financial statements; investigative outlet source requests. Do not attempt unauthorized access to banking systems.

**Reporting-source inquiry draft:**

> We are verifying the February 2025 PSB pledge of its A7 LLC interest to VEB.RF and the separately reported VEB.RF stake in Exim International. Could you identify the original registry entry, filing date, legal entity numbers, collateral instrument number, and any publicly releasable documentary basis for the loan and equity figures? Please distinguish pledged credit from funds actually advanced. We will attribute any information and respect confidential-source restrictions.

**Proof threshold:** dated original registry/contract plus independently checkable financial reporting or disbursement record. A pledged stake is not an actual loan balance.

## Priority 3 — Cross-period continuity (only after separate paths are documented)

Build a table of **legal names and registration numbers**, not name similarity. Compare entity numbers, counsel, agents, signatories, beneficial owners, lenders, payment intermediaries, correspondent institutions, account identifiers where lawfully available, and approval dates.

An overlap counts as **transactionally meaningful** only if a specific document explains the role and a dated transaction or instruction connects the parties. VEB as a common institutional name alone is a lead, not evidence of coordinated operations. A7's 2024 creation is a hard chronology boundary.

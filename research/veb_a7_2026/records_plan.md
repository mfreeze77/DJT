## Priority 1 — Toronto: resolve the $15m allegation

**Testable hypothesis:** Some proceeds of the reported VEB-financed 2010 Zaporizhstal stake sale were transferred into Talon/Midland's Toronto project and supported fee payments to a Trump entity.

**Alternative explanations:** The sale proceeds never entered the project; a different capital source funded it; proceeds entered a Shnaider holding company but not the project; project contributions occurred but did not generate any particular Trump fee; fees were earned independently of that contribution.

**Records in order:**
1. Original 2010 Zaporizhstal stake-sale agreement, closing statement, escrow instructions, purchaser beneficial ownership and VEB buyer-financing/security documentation. Establish who bought what, who lent, who received proceeds and currency.
2. Midland/Shnaider audited financial statements, capital accounts, project funding requests, shareholder-loan registers and cash journals for 2009–2012.
3. Talon project bank statements, lender draw requests, project account pledges, intercompany receipts, deposit releases, and general ledger for same period.
4. Executed Trump brand/management/license agreements and amendments, invoices, remittance records, fee schedules, termination settlements, and any revenue-sharing rights.
5. Reconcile both sides of every alleged transfer by transaction date, payer, beneficiary, account, currency, amount, bank reference and purpose. Record negative searches and alternative financing.

**Accessible starting point:** [FTI Talon receiver archive](https://cfcanada.fticonsulting.com/talon/reports.htm), Ontario Superior Court CV-16-11573-00CL. First report para. 16 and Appendix D list a C$400m debenture and multiple project-account/security agreements; para. 17 identifies a junior Midland Resources Holding Limited charge. **Request the executed documents, not only their names.** [First report](https://cfcanada.fticonsulting.com/talon/docs/Receiver%27s%20First%20Report%20%28Security%20Opinion%2C%20Status%20Report%29.pdf).

**Public-record route:** court registry access and public filed exhibits, receiver archive, Ontario land-title registrations and PPSA searches, available insolvency dockets. Nonpublic bank statements and private contracts generally require consent, discovery, or appropriate legal process. Do not seek protected SARs.

**Court/receiver inquiry draft:**

> Re: Ontario Superior Court file CV-16-11573-00CL, Talon International Inc. et al. Please identify public filed exhibits or archived documents containing (a) the October 9, 2007 debenture and related draw schedules, (b) the registered subordinate Midland Resources Holding Limited charge, (c) project-account pledge agreements listed in Appendix D to the Receiver's First Report, (d) the original material-project-agreement assignments, and (e) receipts/disbursements reports and schedules. **Proof threshold:** original matching records showing actual cash movement into a named project account and onward fee effect. Do not infer from a loan face value or license existence.

## 5. Cash provenance and economic dependence are different investigations

**Reviewer analytical distinction, not a new historical finding:** cash tracing asks whether identified proceeds reached a project and funded a specified payment. Economic-dependence analysis asks whether financing sustained a project or contractual right whose value later generated income or buyout consideration.

The second hypothesis can matter without a dollar-for-dollar bank trace, but it needs its own evidence: timing, obligations due, liquidity needs, alternative funding, actual applications of funds and the contractual basis of later value. A later disclosed benefit is not by itself proof that the earlier financing was necessary for that benefit. An ordinary commercial explanation must be tested with the same care as an influence explanation.

Maintain separate findings and confidence assessments for these questions. Do not use the existence of a reported benefit to fill the RF-014 provenance gap, or use the absence of a traced payment to deny the reported benefit. No hypothesis in this review establishes Trump knowledge of VEB origination, political coordination, or continuity with A7.

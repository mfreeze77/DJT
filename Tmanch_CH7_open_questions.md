# Chapter 7 Open Questions and Records Plan
## Priority investigations for January 20, 2025–August 26, 2026

## Method

Each program below begins with a falsifiable question. The purpose is not to collect more articles; it is to obtain the record that would connect—or separate—value, access, motive, and official action.

Priority scale:

- **P0:** Could materially establish or falsify a quid pro quo, foreign-direction, or major hidden-concession hypothesis.
- **P1:** Could materially strengthen the circumstantial mosaic or explain a major policy decision.
- **P2:** Important completeness, attribution, or network work.

**Proof threshold:** A document matching a Russian preexisting demand to a U.S. concession with an identified reciprocal benefit would significantly strengthen the payoff analysis.

# Filing-ready request modules

## White House / NSC Presidential records preservation request language

> Records sufficient to identify all meetings, calls, memoranda, briefing materials, talking points, draft agreements, maps, economic proposals, sanctions proposals, and communications involving Donald J. Trump, Steve Witkoff, Jared Kushner, Marco Rubio, Mike Waltz, Joshua Gruenbaum, Vladimir Putin, Yuri Ushakov, Sergei Lavrov, Kirill Dmitriev, or the Russian Direct Investment Fund concerning Russia, Ukraine, sanctions, economic cooperation, energy, critical minerals, Arctic development, NATO, territorial arrangements, security guarantees, prisoner exchanges, or intelligence-service contacts from January 20, 2025 through August 26, 2026.

Because current Presidential records are generally not immediately accessible through ordinary FOIA, this language is primarily for preservation, congressional requests, litigation discovery where available, and future PRA access.

## Treasury / OFAC FOIA

> Records sufficient to identify licenses, authorizations, waivers, interpretive guidance, deliberative recommendations released in final form, and implementation actions concerning Kirill Dmitriev, RDIF, Rosselkhozbank, Russian agricultural/fertilizer trade, maritime insurance, Russian port access, payment systems, correspondent banking, or U.S.-Russia economic projects from January 20, 2025 through August 26, 2026.

## State Department FOIA

> Final or releasable records, including cables, readouts, memoranda, participant lists, briefing materials, diplomatic notes, draft/final agreements, and communications concerning the Riyadh talks, March 2025 Black Sea talks, Anchorage summit, Geneva negotiations, Witkoff/Dmitriev meetings, and U.S.-Russia economic normalization.

## DOD / Intelligence-support records

> Final directives and releasable records identifying the scope, dates, legal authorities, implementation, and assessed operational effect of pauses, restorations, or limitations on U.S. military assistance and intelligence support to Ukraine from February 28, 2025 through August 26, 2026.

## CIA / Ratcliffe visit

National-security exemptions will likely be extensive. Request only releasable metadata first:

> Records sufficient to identify the dates, locations, U.S. participants, Russian counterpart organizations, general subject categories, and final public-affairs or congressional-notification records concerning CIA Director John Ratcliffe's August 2026 travel to the Russian Federation.

## Primary-text comparison

- [GL 128C, April 14](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/935496/download?inline=) and [GL 128D, October 15](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937236/download?inline=): the expiry moves from October 29, 2026 to April 24, 2027, at 12:01 a.m. EDT. Paragraphs (a)–(c) otherwise retain their coverage and exceptions. The license number, document date and supersession clause change. D replaces C effective October 15, before C's expiry.
- [GL 130A, April 14](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/935501/download?inline=) and [GL 130B, October 15](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937241/download?inline=): the same expiry change applies; paragraphs (a)–(b) otherwise retain their scope and exceptions. Identifiers, dates and the supersession clause change. B replaces A effective October 15, with no expiry gap.

## Scope, beneficiaries and limits

GL 128D continues specified retail transactions involving Lukoil International GmbH and qualifying subsidiaries outside Russia, including covered blocked-account use. It retains the express prohibition on transfers to Russia-located persons/accounts. GL 130B covers Lukoil Neftohim Burgas JSC, Lukoil Bulgaria EOOD, Lukoil Aviation Bulgaria EOOD, Lukoil Bulgaria Bunker EOOD and entities in which those companies own, directly or indirectly, individually or in aggregate, a 50 percent or greater interest; its separate exclusions must not be replaced with 128D's transfer restriction.

Analytical inference: operational continuity can benefit covered operators, customers, employees and counterparties and preserve asset value. Neither instrument establishes actual payments, Russian fiscal receipts, lobbying influence or presidential motive. These extensions do not delist Lukoil. [OFAC FAQ 1225](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1225) identifies retail-consumer protection as the retail authorization's rationale and distinguishes it from divestment authority.

This is not a new diesel authorization under GL 135, a GL 131J divestment extension, or implementation of reported shadow-fleet proposals. The contemporaneous EU action is separately recorded in [J20-2026-020's note](Tmanch_CH7_Oct15_EU_Designations_Source_Note.md); it is not an offset or evidence of coordination. Full page-labelled PDF text was compared; no transaction records were obtained. Missing standalone C/A matrix rows are not evidence that October 15 created these permissions for the first time.

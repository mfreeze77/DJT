# Chapter 7 Primary and Documentary Source Inventory

This inventory separates records proving that an event occurred from records describing motive, reaction, or alleged private conduct.

## Source tiers

- **A — Primary/official:** executive actions, agency records, court filings, legislative votes, U.N./NATO records, Kremlin transcripts, official corporate disclosures.
- **B — High-quality documentary:** Reuters, AP, FT and comparable reporting grounded in named documents, participants, or multiple sources.
- **C — Established analytical/business press:** RBC, Interfax, specialist reporting, expert interpretation.
- **D — Lead/perception only:** Telegram, forums, anonymous or semi-anonymous commentary.

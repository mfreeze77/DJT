## Reuters attribution and response

[Reuters, October 15, Erin Banco and Gram Slattery](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/exclusiveorder-to-limit-starshield-for-ukraine-came-from-inside-white-house-sources-say-4952816): two U.S. officials and a briefed Western official anonymously attributed a Starshield restriction instruction to the White House, transmitted to Pentagon leaders; neither decisionmaker nor implementer was named. A senior Ukrainian official said October 13–14 inquiries received similar attribution.

A senior U.S. official, answering Reuters, said: “No such request was ever made by the president.” Questions about other officials went unanswered. Ukraine's Defence Ministry source reported a remaining 160-kph ceiling October 15; Ukrainian officials described impaired strike and interceptor operations.

## Assessment and restoration conflict

**Analytical inference:** This strengthens deliberate government restriction over unexplained malfunction. The solicited denial is president-specific, leaving broader attribution unrebutted, not admitted. Impairment benefits Russia operationally; personal presidential direction, Russian coordination, attack timing and a diesel quid pro quo remain unproved.

[Axios, October 15](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/15/ukraine-starlink-restored-strikes-russia-trump-musk), citing a Ukrainian official, reported full Starlink/Starshield restoration. The discrepancy remains unresolved.

Do not silently replace either dated account with a claim of verified full restoration. System configuration, unit-level tests and timestamped change logs are needed to reconcile differences in service, movement threshold and observation time. A news-publication timestamp is not the time a technical change occurred.

## Signed Senate letter: request, not investigative finding

The [October 15 signed letter](https://www.foreign.senate.gov/download/letter-to-dodig-re-starlink-over-ukraine_-final-signed) asks DoD Inspector General Platte B. Moring to investigate. Its nine signatories are Jeanne Shaheen, Thom Tillis, Richard Durbin, Susan Collins, Jack Reed, John Cornyn, Christopher Coons, Lisa Murkowski and Richard Blumenthal.

Pages 1–2 distinguish satellite restrictions from warning/intelligence disruptions. Question 5 covers direction, authorization or implementation by SpaceX, DoD **or other U.S. government entities**, plus contractual compliance and DoD response. Use this signed text rather than a narrower press-release transcription. The letter requests preservation of communications, contracts and technical records, with written investigation status and an initial briefing by **November 1, 2026**. That is not a completed-investigation deadline. No opening or findings are verified here.

# Russian-Linked Financial Flows — Source Inventory
## What each record proves and what it does not prove

## Source-control rule

The `Visual_Timeline` yearly JSON files organize the research but do not independently prove the underlying events. Dollar amounts enter a conclusion only when supported by:

- a primary property, court, regulatory, tax-derived, contract, or corporate record;
- an official government finding;
- or high-quality documentary reporting that identifies records and methodology.

# November 6 FinCEN MEXC order: issuance and effective-date boundary

**J20-2026-091 / CH7-MEXC-9714-001.** Unscored institutional action, separate from the eight-entity OFAC action recorded as J20-2026-090. No financial flow is added.

**MEXC-9714-S002:** [Treasury's November 6 release](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/) announces issuance of a FinCEN order under section 9714(a). Treasury and FinCEN materials describe one action, not independent corroboration of underlying conduct.

**MEXC-9714-S001:** The [original pre-publication order](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf), cover and operative sections VI.A–E, provides for covered financial institutions to stop sending or receiving funds, including convertible virtual currency, to or from defined non-US MEXC entities and successors. It expressly excludes US-located branches, subsidiaries and offices from that defined target. The terms begin **30 days after Federal Register publication**, with no cessation date. FinCEN reserves authority to impose conditions or grant exemptions; existing BSA and OFAC obligations remain separate.

The acquired PDF says Federal Register placement/publication is pending and retains an effective-date placeholder. **MEXC-9714-S003:** The [FinCEN special-measures index](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-and-regulations/special-measures) links the same PDF under November 6; its column label does not establish Federal Register publication or immediate operation. **MEXC-9714-S004:** A [November 7 Federal Register API query for MEXC](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions%5Bterm%5D=MEXC&per_page=20) returned three older SEC notices, no matching order. This is a bounded search result, not proof of universal absence. No exact effective date is assigned.

# November 12 Skyline corporate-action disclosures

Acquired for the November 14 watch. Two SEC-hosted Form 6-K reports dated November 12, 2026 describe separate issuer actions. Their EDGAR acceptance timestamps were not independently checked; report and announcement dates are not claimed as filing-acceptance times. ## SKYLINE-NOV12-S001: issuer-reported ticker change

In [Form 6-K, accession 0001213900-26-118491](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026118491/ea0311820-6k_skyline.htm), Skyline Builders Group Holding Limited (CIK 2031009) announces a change from **KAZR to CRMN**, effective at the opening of trading November 12. It says shareholder action is not required and its CUSIP remains unchanged. This is an issuer-reported effective date, not independently verified exchange trading or a change of ownership.

## SKYLINE-NOV12-S002: nonbinding proposed combination

A separate [Form 6-K, accession 0001213900-26-118516](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026118516/ea0312035-6k_skyline.htm) reports that Skyline announced entry into a **nonbinding term sheet** for a proposed combination with SuperCritical Materials. The issuer describes SuperCritical as developing infrastructure to extract uranium and other critical metals from seawater in the United States. This is an attributed business description, not independent validation of commercial feasibility.

The related [Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026118516/ea031203501ex99-1.htm) describes a prospective definitive share-exchange agreement, shareholder approval and regulatory/exchange steps. These remain future stages, not a completed acquisition, approved transaction, operating project or new disbursement. The filing and exhibit are one disclosure family, not independent confirmations.

## November 11 final act: legal dates, exact reference and limited remit

**EUMAM-NOV9-S003:** The [official final PDF](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/57b98e4d-dd70-11f1-8501-01aa75ed71a1.0006.01/DOC_1) and [matching official XHTML](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/57b98e4d-dd70-11f1-8501-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1) are two formats of **Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2508**, CELEX **32026D2508**, amending Decision (CFSP) 2022/1968. The Official Journal header dates publication **November 11**. The act is adopted **November 9**, and its Article 2 enters it into force on adoption while expressly providing application from **November 16**. These are three distinct stages; as of November 11 the act is in force but its stated application date is future.

Article 1(7), replacing parent Article 14, extends the mandate through **November 15, 2028**. Article 1(5)(b), inserting parent Article 11(7), specifies **EUR 292.6 million** for the mission's **common costs during November 16, 2026–November 15, 2028**. This refines the earlier rounded nearly-EUR-300-million report. It is a financial reference, not actual spending, a Ukrainian receipt, weapons delivered or a net new cash increment. The Article 51(2) reference percentages of zero for commitments/payments are a legal budget clause, not evidence of observed zero expenditure.

The relationship to **054 / CH7-EPF-ADOPTION-001** and its **EUR 230 million** remains unresolved. Neither amount is added to the other or to the earlier EPF figures. The [noncash reference observation](Tmanch_CH7_Nov9_EUMAM_Financial_Reference_Observation.json) preserves the exact legal amount while leaving actual spending/receipt and overlap unknown and excluding it from financial-flow totals.

Article 1(1) adds battlefield-lessons collection/analysis/dissemination and requested defence-reform advisory/educational work, initially including professional military education. The replaced territorial clause permits activity in Ukraine **exclusively for direct coordination and those two task categories**, alongside operation in member states. This is more specific than a coordination-only summary and does not authorize a blanket in-Ukraine combat-training deployment or establish actual presence. The earlier press-release description remains acquisition history, superseded only by this precise final remit.

Article 1(3) requires coordination with member-state bilateral efforts and partners including the US, and cooperation with NATO structures while respecting institutional autonomy. It requires a local presence, but is not evidence that personnel have deployed or the presence has opened. Article 1(4) leaves mission/personnel status in Ukraine to an EU–Ukraine agreement; no concluded agreement was acquired. The common-cost provisions include training-centre partnership cofinancing subject to EPF Committee criteria, not verified grants or payments.

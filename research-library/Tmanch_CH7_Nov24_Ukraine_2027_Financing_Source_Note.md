# Ukraine's 2027 external financing: forecast, facilities and cash

Status checked November 25, 2026. This note backs **J20-2026-127**, distinct from the 2026 cash receipt in [J20-2026-125](Tmanch_CH7_Nov20_EU_Ukraine_Facility_Source_Note.md) and the [PURL framework in J20-2026-120](Tmanch_CH7_Nov_Ukraine_Aid_Fiscal_Stages_Source_Note.md).

| Stage | Amount | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| External financing need | €115bn | Zelenskyy reiterated this estimate on November 24 at the [Congress of Local and Regional Authorities](https://www.president.gov.ua/news/zvernennya-prezidenta-do-uchasnikiv-zasidannya-kongresu-misc-107469). He cited about €90bn in expected EU support and a remaining €25bn to find. [Reuters coverage](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ukraine-needs-125-billion-external-support-2027-zelenskiy-says-2026-11-24/) gives the euro figures despite the dollar headline. |
| Existing Ukraine Support Loan, 2027 portion | Up to €45bn | [Regulation (EU) 2026/467](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/467) establishes the €90bn 2026–27 envelope; [Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/919](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2026/919/oj) covers the first €45bn in 2026. Final [Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2623](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202602623) was adopted November 23, 2026 and entered into force on publication November 25; Article 2 applies the act from adoption. Inserted Article 2a makes the ceiling accessible January 1 to December 31, 2027: €6.65bn Ukraine Facility loans, €6.65bn macro-financial assistance and €31.7bn for defence industrial capacity. Inserted Article 3a sets indicative macro-financial instalments of €2bn, €3bn and €1.65bn, all inside that €6.65bn. This is legal availability, not a payment. |
| Separate proposed Coordinated Support instrument | Up to €45bn | The Commission proposed it November 18 ([COM(2026) 636, 2026/0363(COD)](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16080-2026-INIT/en/pdf); [Commission summary](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf)). Up to €25bn military and €20bn budget support, financed through EU market borrowing backed by EU budget headroom. Ukraine would owe principal once it receives reparations. It is a separate legislative proposal, not the existing loan's 2027 balance. No adoption or payment was verified. |

Counting only the authorized existing-loan balance leaves €70bn against the president's estimate. The €25bn residual assumes the additional instrument is adopted and both windows provide the full €45bn. Neither number is a new cash movement. Annex II separately estimates a baseline residual of about €27bn budget plus €40.9bn defence after the existing-loan support. These are scenario estimates, not additional authorizations or payments; defence needs can change with fulfillment of partner commitments. The proposed windfall mechanism would allow future earnings from immobilized Russian sovereign assets to service new eligible loans after existing G7-backed loans are repaid; this is not seizure or spending of the Russian principal. The proposed up-to-€5bn third-country guaranteed channel is conditional and not added to either financing total.

Ukraine's budget and defence capacity are the intended beneficiaries. No 2027 disbursement, vendor receipt, U.S. transfer or quantified Russian cost was verified. Track the separate instrument and guarantee amendment, 2027 implementation and eligibility, and EU disbursement and Ukrainian receipt records. The November 26–27 [European Council agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/26-27/) lists the proposed top-up for discussion, not adoption. Do not combine this 2027 ceiling with the proposed tenth 2026 Ukraine Facility instalment (J20-2026-133) or the separate European Peace Facility authorization (J20-2026-134).

**Legislative gates, status checked November 25.** [Parliament file 2026/0363(COD)](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2026%2F0363%28COD%29) tracks COM(2026)636, the proposed lending instrument under ordinary legislative procedure. [2026/0364(APP)](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2026%2F0364%28APP%29) tracks COM(2026)637, the separate proposed EU budget-guarantee change in the 2021–27 framework under the consent procedure. Both were preparatory in Parliament, notwithstanding [Zelenskyy's stated expectation](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/zvernennya-prezidenta-do-uchasnikiv-zasidannya-kongresu-misc-107469) that the extra €45 billion was agreed. Summit political language is not legislative adoption or cash. Separately, [COM(2026)636 Article 27](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16080-2026-INIT/en/pdf#page=56) would add up to €5 billion of guarantee-backed Ukraine Facility loans through June 30, 2028, conditional on qualifying third-country guarantees entering into force and the Commission receiving required provisioning; loans would be capped by covered principal. No such signed guarantee or payment was found. Do not deduct the €5 billion from the 2027 gap or count it as committed.

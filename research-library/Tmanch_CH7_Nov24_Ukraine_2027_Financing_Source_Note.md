# Ukraine's 2027 external financing: forecast, facilities and cash

Status checked November 24, 2026. This note backs **J20-2026-127**, distinct from the 2026 cash receipt in [J20-2026-125](Tmanch_CH7_Nov20_EU_Ukraine_Facility_Source_Note.md) and the [PURL framework in J20-2026-120](Tmanch_CH7_Nov_Ukraine_Aid_Fiscal_Stages_Source_Note.md).

| Stage | Amount | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| External financing need | €115bn | Zelenskyy reiterated this estimate on November 24 at the [Congress of Local and Regional Authorities](https://www.president.gov.ua/news/zvernennya-prezidenta-do-uchasnikiv-zasidannya-kongresu-misc-107469). He cited about €90bn in expected EU support and a remaining €25bn to find. [Reuters coverage](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ukraine-needs-125-billion-external-support-2027-zelenskiy-says-2026-11-24/) gives the euro figures despite the dollar headline. |
| Existing Ukraine Support Loan, 2027 portion | Up to €45bn | [Regulation (EU) 2026/467](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/467) establishes the €90bn 2026–27 envelope; [Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/919](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2026/919/oj) covers the first €45bn in 2026. [COM(2026) 606, draft Article 2a](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/f1ea4425-d475-11f1-9458-01aa75ed71a1.0001.03/DOC_1) proposed the second €45bn for 2027: €31.7bn for defence industry and €13.3bn for budget assistance, split €6.65bn each between Ukraine Facility loans and macro-financial assistance. Coreper cleared the implementing decision on November 18 and the Council listed it for adoption November 23 as non-legislative A-item 2 ([ST 16072/26](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16072-2026-INIT/en/pdf); decision text ST 15371/26 + ADD 1). The agenda does not prove formal adoption; a final adopted act or minutes were not verified in this check. |
| Separate proposed Coordinated Support instrument | Up to €45bn | The Commission proposed it November 18 ([COM(2026) 636, 2026/0363(COD)](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16080-2026-INIT/en/pdf); [Commission summary](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2454/IP_26_2454_EN.pdf)). Up to €25bn military and €20bn budget support, financed through EU market borrowing backed by EU budget headroom. Ukraine would owe principal once it receives reparations. It is a separate legislative proposal, not the existing loan's 2027 balance. No adoption or payment was verified. |

Counting only the planned existing-loan balance leaves €70bn against the president's estimate. The €25bn residual assumes the additional instrument is adopted and both windows provide the full €45bn. Neither number is a new cash movement. The proposed windfall mechanism would allow future earnings from immobilized Russian sovereign assets to service new eligible loans after existing G7-backed loans are repaid; this is not seizure or spending of the Russian principal. The proposed up-to-€5bn third-country guaranteed channel is conditional and not added to either financing total.

Ukraine's budget and defence capacity are the intended beneficiaries. No 2027 disbursement, vendor receipt, U.S. transfer or quantified Russian cost was verified. Check the published decision/minutes, the new instrument's adoption, and separate EU disbursement and Ukrainian receipt records. The November 26–27 [European Council agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/24/special-european-council-of-26-27-november-2026-invitation-letter-by-president-antonio-costa-to-the-members-of-the-european-council/) is a checkpoint, not evidence of an outcome.

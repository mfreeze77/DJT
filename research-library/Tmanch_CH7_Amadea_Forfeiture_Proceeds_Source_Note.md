# Amadea: federal sale date, restricted proceeds and pending petition

**J20-2026-122. Status as of November 24, 2026.** The *Amadea* (IMO 1012531) was sold for **$187 million on October 10, 2025**, according to [DOJ's audited FY2025 financial statements, Note 22, printed p. 56](https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/documents/reports/2026-02/26-013.pdf#page=62). The note says net proceeds remain in the Seized Asset Deposit Fund until the appeals process is fully resolved and the transaction is for FY2026 accounting.

The [USMS year-end release](https://www.usmarshals.gov/news/press-release/end-of-year-review-us-marshals-arrest-over-73000-fugitives-in-2025-strengthening) describes the September 2025 sealed-bid auction and an undisclosed buyer. The [auction rules](https://amadea.s3.amazonaws.com/Auction_Documents/Auction%20Rules.pdf) specify highest sealed bid, bidder registration and escrow, followed by USMS acceptance and closing. September bidding and October completed sale are distinct events. The later [July–September 2026 Operation Atlantic Resolve report, PDF p. 14 and endnote 31](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=14) instead places the same $187 million sale in the quarter and separately reports **nearly $40 million in DOJ outlays**, with funding impact pending. Use the audited completion date. No evidence of a second sale or an accounting reconciliation allowing the reported costs to be subtracted from gross as a verified net figure.

The [November 10, 2026 Coast Guard eCOFR status report](https://www.uscg.mil/Portals/0/NPFC/COFR/COFR%20Vsl%20Status%20Report.pdf?ver=Dpn2FMHC14fkhrEMBS-htA%3D%3D) lists **Beyond Holdings Group Ltd** for L1012531 from November 12, 2025 to November 12, 2028. This is a financial-responsibility entry, not an auction bill of sale or disclosure of ultimate ownership. The [LEI registry](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/254900Y1E7ZT46Z0P637) identifies BVI registration 2173316 and former legal name AHS Four Company Limited; its accounting-parent fields do not establish a beneficial owner.

The government alleged that sanctioned Russian businessman Suleiman Kerimov beneficially owned the vessel. The [Second Circuit's June 1, 2026 judgment](https://ww3.ca2.uscourts.gov/decisions/OPN/25-869_opn.pdf) affirmed the district court's ruling that Eduard Khudainatov and Millemarin lacked constitutional standing to contest forfeiture, not a separate appellate finding of all alleged misconduct. It recounts a distinct €225 million payment under the 2021 transaction; do not combine that historical transfer with the 2025 auction price. The [Supreme Court docket, No. 26-586](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/26-586.html) shows an October 29 certiorari petition and November 17 waiver of the United States' initial response, without a decision as of November 24.

The buyer obtained the vessel; the gross amount recorded by DOJ is not unrestricted federal revenue or a transfer to Ukraine. [Section 1708 as enacted](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-117publ328/html/PLAW-117publ328.htm) is a discretionary authority, not a direction that proceeds from this sale be paid. No transfer, net amount available, or effect on Ukraine recorded. Check the Supreme Court docket for disposition and then the DOJ/State audit or transfer record for any actual amount or recipient.

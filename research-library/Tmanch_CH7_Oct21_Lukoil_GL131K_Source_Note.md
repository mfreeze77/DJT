## Version change and legal limits

Paragraph (e) replaces J effective October 21. Paragraphs (a) and (b) now expire **November 21, 2026 at 12:01 a.m. EST (05:01 UTC; November 20 at 11:01 p.m. CST)**, instead of October 22 at 12:01 a.m. EDT. The replacement occurs before J expires, without a gap. Text comparison found version/date/signature changes and note punctuation, not a substantive expansion of authorized activities.

Negotiations and contingent contracts remain distinct from permission to perform a sale. Paragraph (c) retains specified blocked-account use for paragraph (b) activity; paragraph (d) retains other exclusions, including transfers to persons or accounts located in Russia. This is not delisting or unrestricted unblocking.

FAQ 1224 continues to describe expected conditions for any future sale authorization: severance from Lukoil, U.S.-jurisdiction blocking of amounts owed until sanctions lift, no upfront windfall, and review of further material disposals. These are agency expectations, not an identified buyer's approved payment terms. FAQ 1225 describes ordinary-course maintenance and consistent pre-existing activities; its explanation is not evidence that a particular payment occurred.

## Cumulative assessment and joins

**J20-2026-032 / GL_131K** is one successor action, not two concessions. Preserve historical **J20-2026-028 / GL_131J**, **CH7-LIG-004**, and the [October 18 source note](Tmanch_CH7_Oct18_Lukoil_Divestment_Source_Note.md). Existing named-company and transaction joins remain there; K supplies no new buyer, perimeter schedule, price, creditor allocation or settlement.

**Benefit assessment: mixed, inferred rather than measured.** Continued maintenance can preserve value in Russian-owned foreign assets and support counterparties/workers; the same controlled window can facilitate divestment to nonblocked buyers while limiting Russia-bound proceeds, consistent with the stated U.S. pressure objective. Extension prolongs the pathway without demonstrating successful separation or realized Russian receipts. Neither zero effect nor a completed financial windfall follows from the instrument alone.

This issuance alone establishes no Trump-family receipt, personal presidential direction or quid pro quo. Same-day publication does not establish a link to the Hubbard negotiations or the separate sanctions-restraint statement in J20-2026-031. Specific decision records would be required for that join. No financial-flow or payoff amount is added.

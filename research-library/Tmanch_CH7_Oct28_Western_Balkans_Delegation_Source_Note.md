# October 28 presidential delegation: Western Balkans sanctions authorities

**J20-2026-076 / CH7-WB-DELEGATION-001.** The [signed memorandum dated October 28](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-02/pdf/2026-22386.pdf) was published November 2 at **91 FR 69203–69204**, document **2026-22386**. It delegates existing functions under FY2026 NDAA §8335, codified at [22 U.S.C. §10704](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t22/s10704/), rather than establishing a new sanctions programme.

**WB-DEL-S001–002:** Treasury, in consultation with State, receives §8335(a) functions. State identifies sanctionable foreign persons, and relevant agency heads implement sanctions within their responsibilities. Treasury also receives blocking, implementation and rulemaking authorities, plus conditional termination authority in consultation with State; State receives the conditional waiver authority. These are delegations of the statute's powers, not evidence that a particular power has been exercised.

The statute covers the Western Balkans and includes property-blocking and entry restrictions. Termination remains subject to statutory certifications. A waiver requires a national-security determination and advance congressional notice, for renewable periods of no more than 180 days. The memorandum names no sanctioned person, removes no listing, grants no waiver and supplies no licensing or asset-release result. Do not join it to Russia, Iran or Ukraine sanctions merely because Treasury implements those programmes too.

**Mechanism and cumulative assessment:** this is a verified presidential administrative allocation under a congressional sanctions framework. It establishes agency responsibility, not new measured pressure or relief. National benefit is **uncertain** pending implementation; no Russian gain, private beneficiary, political exchange, payment or enforcement outcome is established. Keep it unscored and separate from sanctions additions/removals and financial totals. Implementation records, later designations, waivers and termination decisions could change the assessment; none was acquired for this entry.

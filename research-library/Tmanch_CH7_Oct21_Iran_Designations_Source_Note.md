# October 21, 2026: Iran designations and bounded wind-down permissions

## Verified action and scope

OFAC's October 21 notice records five individual and four entity additions under EO13902, plus nine vessels identified as blocked property. The entities are Karnakis and Karnakis, Roxette Maritime Shipping and Trading S.A., Amigoz Singapore PTE. Ltd., and Whales Shipping Line LLC. This records the U.S. government's legal action, not independent adjudication of the underlying allegations. [OFAC notice](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261021) · [Treasury announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0664/).

[GL GG](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937266/download?inline) authorizes necessary wind-down involving Karnakis and its 50%-or-more-owned entities, with limited official-capacity participation of blocked individuals. [GL HH](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937271/download?inline) authorizes enumerated financial wind-down, safety/environmental activities and qualifying cargo offloading involving Roxette and its seven annexed cargo vessels, not every vessel designated that day. Both require blocked-account treatment for payments to blocked persons and expire November 20, 2026 at 00:01 EST (05:01 UTC).

HH's annex lists ANEMOS (9205952), GLORIS (9449510), PRIDE (9487407), DESTINY (9385491), FLAMINGO 1 (9474199), HARMONY (9449522), and PRINCE (9548316). Its cargo authorization is limited to non-Iranian cargo loaded on or before October 21. Authorized docking/anchoring/departure and cargo delivery/offloading exclude ports in Iran or Russia, or controlled by their governments. New commercial contracts are not generally authorized. The two Whales vessels are not in HH's annex. The Russia-port exclusion is a legal condition, not evidence of a common Russian shipping network.

# Supporting transcript research

A source index for interviews and other recorded commentary. Use the original
video and exact moment links, then verify the underlying claims separately.
This lane is supporting research; an entry does not promote a claim to the living
ledger or count a dependent report as independent confirmation.

## Send a transcript for intake

Supply the YouTube link and copied raw transcript with timestamps. The full raw
and cleaned versions belong in the private workbench. The private intake guide,
shared with the owner separately, provides the template, deterministic parser,
revision history and validation.

The public lane keeps selected source metadata, original summaries, short
necessary excerpts when appropriate, and timestamp links. Full-transcript
republishing permission has not been established. It is not inferred from a
source being viewable online or from a user supplying a copy. There is no automatic
private-to-public export.

## Evidence controls

- Separate a source's assertions from independently verified facts, allegations,
  commentary, forecasts and unresolved leads.
- Preserve exact source/video identity, publication and recording-date distinctions,
  acquisition provenance and correction history. An unknown recording date does
  not become the upload date.
- Link claims/events by immutable repository revision, path and local ID. A
  topical match is context, not corroboration or approval of either proposition.
- Treat clips, reposts, transcript copies and reporting dependent on the same
  interview as one source family. Investigate the lineage of other reports before
  assigning independence.
- Correct transcription only with a documented review. Do not silently repair
  numbers, quotes, attribution or dates. Cue starts are not verified audio ends.

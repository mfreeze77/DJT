# November Ukraine Facility: Council approval to confirmed state-budget receipt

**J20-2026-125.** The [European Commission](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-disburses-eur79-billion-strengthen-ukraines-financial-stability-and-advance-reform-2026-11-20_en) reports a net **€7,859,441,144 disbursement on November 20, 2026**, and [Ukraine's Ministry of Finance](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine_received_record_eur_8_billion_from_the_eu_under_ukraine_facility-6032) independently confirms that day's receipt by its state budget, rounding the amount to €8 billion. The Commission calls this the ninth Facility disbursement. Its roughly **€5.1 billion Ukraine Support Loan component is included**, not another payment.

[Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2596](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2026-81818) was adopted November 16 and published November 23, with application from adoption. Article 1 sets **€8,040,665,425 before prefinancing clearance**, spanning portions of the fourth, seventh, eighth and ninth instalments. The **€181,224,281** difference between that gross amount and the exact payment equals the change in cumulative loan prefinancing cleared: €1,455,253,522 after eight instalments in [Council ST 14861/26, recital 5](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-14861-2026-INIT/en/pdf), versus €1,636,477,803 after nine in [COM(2026) 642 final, recital 5](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16195-2026-INIT/en/pdf). This is a reconciliation across records, not a second outlay.

Book the net once as EU-originated financing received by Ukraine's state budget. This supports public administration and liquidity but does not demonstrate a specific end expenditure. It is separate from November 5 macro-financial assistance and defence aid (J20-2026-085), the November 12 equipment mobilisation (J20-2026-104), and the Cabinet's proposed PURL mechanism (J20-2026-120). No U.S. cash or Russia-benefit score attributed.

**Next tranche, separate and not paid.** The November 20 [COM(2026) 642 final proposal](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16195-2026-INIT/en/pdf) recommends €2,946,297,070 gross against Ukraine's €4,134,971,466 request, covering portions of instalments seven through ten. [Its assessment annex](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/a74679f2-e4c5-11f1-b745-01aa75ed71a1.0001.03/DOC_2) finds step 6.3 partly fulfilled (independent majorities on 12 of 15 required state enterprise supervisory boards) and step 10.9 fulfilled (a November 10 roadmap for gradual gas and electricity market liberalisation after martial law, including vulnerable-consumer protections). Check a final adopted Council act and separately a dated actual transfer or receipt; do not count this proposal in cash totals.

# Tajikistan Rome Statute withdrawal notice and Putin visit

**J20-2026-118** records Tajikistan's own action, not another U.S. act or a legal consequence attributed to the Shenzhen declaration (**J20-2026-116**).

- The [UN depositary notice C.N.506.2026](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/CN/2026/CN.506.2026-Eng.pdf) records the withdrawal notification on November 20, 2026 and sets November 20, 2027 as the effective date. Tajikistan remains a party until then. [Article 127(2)](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/99_corr/13.htm) preserves obligations arising while a party, cooperation in qualifying investigations and proceedings that began before the effective date, and consideration of matters already before the Court. Filing did not discharge Tajikistan's duties on the day of the visit or cancel the ICC warrant.
- The [Tajik presidency](https://president.tj/event/news/58589) records Rahmon meeting Putin in Dushanbe on November 20 local time, the same calendar date as the depositary action; the precise order of the meeting and the filing is not established. The [EU's November 22 statement](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/tajikistan-statement-spokesperson-visit-vladimir-putin-tajikistan-0_en) describes a November 19–20 visit and says Tajikistan again failed to comply with its obligations. No new ICC determination for the November 2026 visit was verified. The [Court's earlier finding ICC-01/22-143 and Assembly follow-up](https://asp.icc-cpi.int/press-releases/PR-20260507) concern the October 2025 visit and arrest request; do not assign that finding to the later visit.
- The July 24, 2026 Russia–China bilateral declaration, annexed to the October 21 letter circulated as [UN A/81/451, pp. 2–3](https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/n26/271/09/pdf/n2627109.pdf#page=2), sets out a more specific position on immunity and cooperation, including arrest and surrender and qualified withholding of assistance involving each other's nationals. It is a submission by Russia and China, not a UN decision. [Shenzhen clause 3](Tmanch_CH7_Nov23_Shenzhen_EU22_Source_Note.md#shenzhen-j20-2026-116) shares general consent and immunity language but contains no equivalent mutual-assistance clause or commitment that the U.S. adopt the July position. The earlier bilateral text establishes a pre-existing policy track; the earlier Tajik visit shows enforcement friction predated Shenzhen. The dates alone do not establish coordination or cause.
- **Effect:** Putin was able to make another official visit without arrest, according to the EU's account. Withdrawal would further narrow the Court's membership prospectively, subject to surviving obligations. This is a concrete enforcement setback, not a change to the warrant or a demonstrated benefit caused by the United States. No policy score is assigned. Check the [Assembly's December 5 non-cooperation plenary, agenda item 21](https://asp.icc-cpi.int/sites/default/files/asp_docs/ICC-ASP-25-1Add.1.Rev1-ENG.pdf#page=10) and published session outcomes for a specifically identified new referral or action; the agenda itself does not announce one.

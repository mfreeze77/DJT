# October 14 EU defence-loan disbursement

## Source and scope

This note supports **J20-2026-017** in [J20-Pres.md](J20-Pres.md) and its [structured companion](Tmanch_CH7_J20_event_ledger.csv).

Sources: [official EU release, October 14](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/ukraine/commission-disburses-eur286-billion-ukraine-drones-missiles-and-urgent-battlefield-needs_en); [Commission timeline](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/eu-solidarity-ukraine_en). Reviewed October 14 through indexed official-page text; direct retrieval of the release failed.

The release identifies drones, air defence, missiles, radars, electronic warfare and ammunition as intended uses. This tranche sits within the existing loan, not on top of its envelope. The separate [October 7 €1.24 billion payment](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/ukraine/commission-disburses-eur124-billion-ukraine-drones-and-missiles_en) is comparison context only; no new cumulative total is constructed.

## Attribution and beneficiary limits

Financing availability benefits Ukraine's procurement capacity; quantities, receipt, deployment and battlefield effects require contracts and delivery records. It does not establish replacement of U.S. intelligence or satellite capabilities.

[J20-2026-016](J20-Pres.md) records contemporaneous Ukrainian needs. This note does not establish that its appeal caused this tranche. Attribute the financing action to the EU. Numerical benefit/cost scores remain unassigned.

## Source-controlled legal action and chronology

- **CAN-IRAN-NOV10-S001:** The [November 10 Global Affairs Canada announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/11/minister-anand-announces-reimposition-of-sanctions-against-iranian-entities.html) describes reimposition of sanctions on **11 Iranian entities** that it says were delisted in 2016. The announcement date is not the legal start.
- **CAN-IRAN-NOV10-S002:** The [original amendment text](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/iran_regulations-reglements12.aspx?lang=eng) adds **Schedule 1 Part 1 entries 242–252**. It provides that it applies according to its terms before Canada Gazette publication and comes into force on registration. Its November 6 page-modification date is not assigned as registration or effectiveness.
- **CAN-IRAN-NOV10-S003:** The [official GAC chronology](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/iran-regulations-reglementations.aspx?lang=eng) identifies the amendment as **SOR/2026-234** and records **November 5, 2026 entry into force**. The chronology supplies that date; the separately registered or gazetted artifact was not acquired.
- **CAN-IRAN-NOV10-S004:** The [November 10 backgrounder](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/11/canada-reimposes-sanctions-against-11-iranian-entities.html) describes financial/property transaction prohibitions applying to persons in Canada and Canadians abroad. These are legal restrictions, not measurements of money frozen or transfers interrupted. The government pages are one official source family, not independent corroboration of alleged conduct.

This Canadian autonomous-sanctions amendment is distinct from a US designation, the prior UN snapback and **097’s Russia SOR/2026-233**. Do not add cumulative historical sanctions counts to these eleven, count the 2016 delisting as another present action, or create another event when Gazette publication later becomes available.

# Ukraine winter cash: UNICEF transfer, Treasury allocation and first batch

Checked November 25, 2026. Source note for **J20-2026-139**. Distinguish the donor-to-account transfer, internal allocation and batch financing; they are not additive.

| Stage | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Donor-to-government account | [UNICEF situation report no. 62, January–September 2026, PDF pp. 1 and 6](https://www.unicef.org/ukraine/en/media/60146/file/Ukraine%20Humanitarian%20Situation%20Report%20No.%2062%20-%20January-September%202026.pdf.pdf#page=6) says UNICEF provided **US$45 million** to the Ukrainian Ministry of Social Policy, Family and Unity's Humanitarian and Resilience Account. UNICEF expected to support 104,000 frontline households with one-time UAH 19,400 payments. This target is not a paid count. No exact transfer date or original-country breakdown for the contribution is specified. |
| Internal government allocation | [Cabinet Resolution 1084 of September 3](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/npas/deiaki-pytannia-nadannia-dopomohy-naselenniu-protiahom-opaliuvalnoho-s1084030926), [text §§1–2 and payment procedure](https://docs.dtkt.ua/doc/1246.2228.0), directs **UAH 2,242,169,600** from the ministry's pooled National Bank humanitarian account to the Treasury special fund for program 2501530 and separately redirects UAH 5,563,999 of unused prior funding. The account channel can pool UNICEF and Ukrainian Red Cross Society money. The allocation is not another donor payment, and there is no exact reconciliation to UNICEF's reported $45 million. |
| First Pension Fund batch | The [Pension Fund](https://www.pfu.gov.ua/2181457-profinansovano-pershi-vyplaty-zymovoyi-pidtrymky/) says it financed the initial **1,424 recipients** on September 29 at **UAH 19,400** each, or **UAH 27,625,600**. The [ministry](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/pershi-vyplaty-19-400-hrn-za-prohramoiu-zymova-pidtrymka-vzhe-profinansovano) corroborates the batch and reports over UAH 27.6 million allocated. These records do not show final household settlement or donor-level attribution for that batch. |
| Later stage | The [ministry’s November 25 update](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/news/zymova-pidtrymka-vyplaty-tryvaiut-zariadni-stantsii-rodynam-dostavliat-do-kintsia-roku-denys-uliutin) reports payments progressing gradually and talks for additional donor financing, with no consolidated paid count. The [Pension Fund’s guidance](https://www.pfu.gov.ua/kh/262977-zymova-pidtrymka-zayavu-podaly-koly-otrymayemo-koshty-use-pro-vyplaty/) describes November 30 as its channel's deadline; the separate partner-administered channel pays in waves during the heating season as financing allows. |

The intended beneficiaries are vulnerable households in frontline communities. The Cabinet's broader [program launch estimate](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/zymova-pidtrymka-ponad-380-tysiach-rodyn-na-pryfrontovykh-terytoriiakh-otrymaiut-po-19-400-hryven) of more than 380,000 households is distinct from UNICEF's 104,000 target. A [November 16 Kharkiv briefing](https://suspilne.media/kharkiv/1453634-na-harkivsini-14-tisac-ludej-uze-otrimali-po-194-tisaci-griven-zimovoi-pidtrimki-sinegubov/) reported approximately 14,000 people had received support locally; its count is not a national household total and is not combined with the initial batch. Separate portable power stations reported procured and in transit on November 25 are not cash or verified household deliveries. The next reconciliation needs aggregate recipient settlements and donor allocations, not another program target.

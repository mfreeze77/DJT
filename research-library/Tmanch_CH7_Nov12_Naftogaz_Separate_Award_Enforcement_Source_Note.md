# Naftogaz: separate Gazprom and Russian Federation awards

Checked November 25, 2026. Sources for **J20-2026-137** and **J20-2026-138**. Not public assistance and not collected proceeds. Do not combine these awards or count either as a realized Russian payment.

## Gazprom: commercial gas-transit award

The June 16, 2025 Swiss-seated ICC award, case 27245/GL/DTI, orders Gazprom PJSC to pay Naftogaz. The [AIFC May 15 first-instance order](https://court.aifc.kz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AIFC-Court-Case-No.-2-of-2026-Judgment-and-Order_ENG-1.pdf#page=2) identifies $1,134,843,398 as the principal; interest is separately specified. It was provisional recognition and enforcement subject to Gazprom's opportunity to contest jurisdiction, not collection. The [November 12 appellate judgment](https://court.aifc.kz/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/AIFC-Court-Case-No.-35-of-2026-AIFC-Court-Case-No.-40-of-2026-Judgment-ENG.pdf#page=3), paragraphs 7–8 and 57–63, records that on July 7 the first-instance court set the order aside and explains why the appeal failed. The court's reason was the limits of AIFC enforcement jurisdiction. It did not annul the ICC award itself. No payment proof, settlement, other final execution result, or Naftogaz receipt was verified for this award. Do not confuse it with other Naftogaz–Gazprom awards.

## Russian Federation: Crimea investment-property award

The [April 12, 2023 final award, PCA 2017-16, operative orders](https://www.italaw.com/sites/default/files/case-html-files/180074_Naftogaz-and-others-v-Russia_Final-Award_2023-04-12.html) awarded **$4,222,875,858.81 original principal** plus separately calculated interest to Naftogaz and five affiliated companies. The debtor is the Russian Federation, not Gazprom. On September 22, 2026 the [Hague Court of Appeal](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/pdf/decision/en-njsc-naftogaz-of-ukraine-pjsc-state-joint-stock-company-chornomornaftogaz-pjsc-ukrgasvydobuvannya-and-others-v-the-russian-federation-judgment-of-the-hague-court-of-appeal-tuesday-22nd-september-2026) ([alternate court-document index](https://www.italaw.com/cases/documents/65317)), case 200.336.746/01, paragraphs 6.75–6.80 and section 7, preliminarily concluded that parts of the final award should be set aside because the assets fall outside temporal treaty jurisdiction. It requested party submissions to delimit and quantify those portions, and reserved its disposition. The surviving principal was not verified.

The [Governor of Svalbard](https://www.sysselmesteren.no/en/news/2026/09/russian-ship-seized-in-svalbard/) independently confirms the September 2 arrest of the Russian vessel *Professor Molchanov* at Barentsburg after the August 31 district court order. This protected potential enforcement, not a vessel sale or proceeds. In the United States, [Judge Bates's October 26 opinion and order](https://www.italaw.com/sites/default/files/case-documents/ca/N4381/italaw19600351%20-%20Naftogaz%20and%20others%20v.%20Russia%20-%20Memorandum%20Opinion%20and%20Order%20of%20the%20United%20States%20District%20Court%20for%20the%20District%20of%20Columbia%20-%20October%2026%2C%202026.pdf) ([index](https://www.italaw.com/cases/documents/65319)), D.D.C. No. 1:23-cv-01828-JDB, denied Russia's jurisdictional motion to dismiss. This lets the recognition petition proceed; it did not enter a money judgment on the award or order payment. The record checked does not establish a final recalculated principal, execution proceeds, or any cash disbursed to the claimants.

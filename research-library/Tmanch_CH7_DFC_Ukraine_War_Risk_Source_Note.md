# DFC Ukraine war-risk insurance: payment, exposure and separate facilities

## J20-2026-157: one reported cash settlement; country and peril inferred

The [FY2026 DFC insurance claims experience report](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/FINAL-Insurance%20Claims%20Experience%20to%20Date%20-%20Sept%202026.pdf#page=3), pp. 3–5 and 7, shows one FY2026 claim and **$139,260 cash settled**. Compare the [prior report](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/FINAL-Insurance%20Claims%20Experience%20to%20Date%20-%20Sept.%202025.pdf#page=7): its cover is dated September 2025 but the underlying tables are explicitly as of September 30, 2024. The [FY2026 audited financial statements, Note 14](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/DFC%20FY%202026%20Annual%20Management%20Report%20-%20508-Compliant.pdf) report FY2025 claims paid as zero and FY2026 as $139,000 rounded to thousands. These are different presentations of one DFC payment, not two.

| Cumulative measure | FY2024 table | FY2026 table | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ukraine settlements | 2 / $62,700,000 | 3 / $62,839,260 | 1 / $139,260 |
| Political-violence settlements | 63 / $82,562,784 | 64 / $82,702,044 | 1 / $139,260 |
| Ukraine recoveries | $4,505,417 | $4,505,417 | Unchanged |

The matching count and dollar changes and zero FY2025 cash payments strongly support one Ukraine-related political-violence insurance payout. DFC does not identify the insured, policy, exact date, or bank recipient, and the country label does not identify the residence or nationality of the payee. The $62.839 million is a cumulative Ukraine total, not FY2026 cash.

Note 14 reports **$52.771 million unpaid claims** and **$65.886 million actuarial liabilities** program-wide at September 30, 2026. DFC accounts for poorer insurance results mainly in connection with Ukraine, but the reserve amounts are not disclosed by country or policy. DFC reports the insurance program used neither appropriated funds to pay claims nor Treasury borrowing in FY2025 or FY2026. The settlement compensated an insured loss and is not a U.S. grant, Vodafone loan draw, Russian payment, or evidence of a particular Kniazha policy.

## J20-2026-158: named Kniazha counterparty, not verified cash

The [DFC FY2025 Q4 congressional report](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/FY25%20Q4%20508%20Compliant%20Appropriators_REDACTED.pdf) lists project **9000116846** under new insurance issuance on September 30, 2025, at **$25 million maximum contingent liability**. [Vienna Insurance Group and Aon](https://group.vig/en/investor-relations/ir-news/ir-meldungen/kniazha-vienna-insurance-group-secures-dfc-backed-war-risk-insurance-facility-with-support-of-aon/) report the facility came into force February 1, 2026. The [DFC project summary](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/9000116846.pdf) gives an **up to $100 million underlying policy portfolio**, not separate agency exposure, and identifies eligible Ukrainian businesses and households. [Kniazha’s products](https://kniazha.ua/about/news/prohramy-strakhuvannia-maina-vid-voiennykh-ryzykiv-dlia-fizychnykh-osib-ta-biznesu) show market availability; no policy-level claim settlement, actual DFC remittance to Kniazha, or connection to J20-2026-157 was verified. The effect evidenced here is risk-sharing capacity for underwriting, not a payment to the insurer or to end policyholders.

## Distinct domestic and multilateral channels

Ukraine's proposed UAH 31 billion state war-risk fund is separately tracked under [J20-2026-156](Tmanch_CH7_Nov24_Ukraine_2027_Financing_Source_Note.md#domestic-war-risk-fund-proposal). The [separate EBRD/Aon facility](https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd--eu-and-aon-expand-war-risk-insurance-facility-to-meet-evol.html) expanded from €110 million to €150 million; EBRD reports over €500 million in coverage on nearly 10,000 policies and no call on its guarantee by MS Amlin at the time of that release. These are capacity and coverage, not EBRD claims paid. The [NASU survey](https://nasu.com.ua/en/war-risk-insurance-market-overview-for-9-months-2026/) describes market-sample payouts, not independently allocated to DFC, EBRD or Kniazha.

# Kyiv municipal liquidity: EBRD loan and U.S.-funded grant receipts

## EBRD project 55503: municipal budget receipt

The [EBRD project card](https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/55503.html) and [November 19, 2024 EU delegation statement](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/ukraine/ebrd-european-union-and-united-states-help-kyiv-prepare-winter_en) describe a **€50 million EBRD emergency-liquidity loan** to Kyiv and a separate **€5 million U.S.-funded grant** routed through the EBRD Crisis Response Special Fund. The EU supplied first-loss cover on 25% of the loan; that is a contingent guarantee, not another disbursement and not an identified guarantee payment.

The Kyiv Finance Department's [signed 2025 annual report](https://kyivcity.gov.ua/kyiv_ta_miska_vlada/struktura_150/vikonavchiy_organ_kivsko_misko_radi_kivska_miska_derzhavna_administratsiya/departamenti_ta_upravlinnya/departament_finansiv/zvit_pro_robotu_departamentu_finansiv_u_2025_rotsi.pdf) records receipt in the city budget on **December 27, 2024** under the November 19 €50 million loan, with the funds directed to support municipal heating utility Kyivteploenergo. It records receipt of the full **€5 million grant in March 2025** in the city's budget. The [2024 annual report](https://kyivcity.gov.ua/kyiv_ta_miska_vlada/struktura_150/vikonavchiy_organ_kivsko_misko_radi_kivska_miska_derzhavna_administratsiya/departamenti_ta_upravlinnya/departament_finansiv/zvit_df_pro_robotu_u_2024_rotsi.pdf) corroborates the December loan receipt. The 2025 report separately records **UAH 77,293,200 in interest paid** by the city on the EBRD loan in 2025; this is debt service, not support received or repayment of principal. The city reports were inspected in a research pass; the 2025 PDF returned HTTP 403 in a subsequent text retrieval. No municipal bank statement was obtained.

The municipality is the immediate borrower and cash recipient; Kyivteploenergo's continued heating and electricity operations are the named purpose. No exact March value date, contractor settlement, completed repairs, directly traceable U.S. Treasury wire to Kyiv, or EU guarantee call was verified. Do not double-count the loan, its EU backing, or the U.S. contribution and its EBRD-routed grant. No measured Russian effect is inferred.

## Separate 2026 facilities: ceilings and lender status

- [Kyiv Emergency Liquidity II, project 57497](https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/57497.html): separate **up to €50 million** for the city and Kyivteploenergo, with EU first-loss cover up to 25% of the committed amount. [June 18 Kyiv Council decision 696/11163](https://old.kmr.gov.ua/uk/content/rishennya-kyyivskoyi-miskoyi-rady-12721) authorized borrowing. The bank marks it **Disbursing**. That is the bank's stage; no amount or value date for a city receipt was reconciled, and no repeat U.S. grant is identified. The bank's project card and board report cover show July 22 and July 21, respectively; no need to treat those as two approvals.
- [Ternopil Emergency Liquidity, project 57901](https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/57901.html): the bank marks the **up to €9 million** facility Approved. The planned €6 million first tranche is to be committed at signing; €3 million remains uncommitted. No signing, disbursement or heating/transport operator receipt is verified.

Municipal financing here is separate from [Naftogaz's EBRD gas grant and supplier payments](Tmanch_CH7_Nov18_Naftogaz_Gas_Financing_Source_Note.md), the [Ukraine Energy Support Fund](Tmanch_CH7_Nov24_Ukraine_Energy_Support_Fund_Source_Note.md), and the [HOME III housing receipt](Tmanch_CH7_Sep30_CEB_HOME_III_Source_Note.md).

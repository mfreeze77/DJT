# Norway–Ukraine: confirmed air-defense delivery and separate budget and audit stages

**J20-2026-128, November 24, 2026.** The [Norwegian Ministry of Defence](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/styrker-det-norsk-ukrainske-sikkerhetssamarbeidet/id3194577/) said Norway had acquired and donated several DESNA air-defense systems over the preceding months and they were already in use in Ukraine. No precise count, handover dates, purchase price or audited effectiveness figures were given. This is verified in-kind assistance benefiting Ukraine, not a pledge and not evidence of a Patriot transfer under [J20-2026-074](Tmanch_CH7_Oct30_Patriot_Sale_Source_Note.md). The same bilateral visit included a signed letter of intent for Norway to test a Ukrainian defense software platform; that is an agreement to test, not proof of implementation.

**2027 proposal.** Norway's [Finance Ministry](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/finansminister-jens-stoltenberg-motte-ukrainas-forsvarsminister-yeyhenii-khmara/id3194746/) still describes **NOK 85 billion** as proposed. Its [budget summary](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/regjeringen-videreforer-sterk-stotte-til-ukraina/id3174281/) splits it into NOK 70 billion military and NOK 15 billion civilian/humanitarian, with NOK 50 billion within the military component planned for international initiatives and procurement from Ukrainian industry. The [Ukrainian ministry's at-least-$7-billion headline](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/norway-support-for-ukraine-2027-7-billion) describes the same annual bilateral support, not an additional commitment. The [Storting timetable](https://www.stortinget.no/no/saker-og-publikasjoner/statsbudsjettet/statsbudsjettet-2027/arbeidsplan/) lists December 7 framework treatment, December 11 portfolio committee report and December 18 portfolio plenary treatment. None of these November records shows a new 2027 outlay or a cash offset to the [EU/Ukraine financing gap, J20-2026-127](Tmanch_CH7_Nov24_Ukraine_2027_Financing_Source_Note.md).

**Separate audit comparison.** In its [November 24 treatment of the two auditor reports](https://stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Saker/Sak/?p=200319), Parliament examined both aid delivery and internal controls. The [committee report, §§2.2–2.9](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Innstillinger/Stortinget/2026-2027/inns-202627-051s/?all=true) cites 23 examined measures (about one third of military aid appropriated by the end of 2025) with 6–886 days from implementation decision to Ukrainian handover and 46–988 days total for closed measures. Delays varied; auditors did not find systemwide Norwegian administrative delays. The separate internal-control review found insufficient monitoring and called for recipients to document equipment receipt; NOK 41.7 billion is the broad grant scope, not a loss, fraud or undelivered-aid estimate. These results add a same-war comparison for the [existing U.S./NATO obligation and outlay stages](Tmanch_CH7_Nov_Ukraine_Aid_Fiscal_Stages_Source_Note.md). No aggregate is moved between proposed, funded, handed over, or in-use buckets; no score assigned.

## What the company says

MOL states that OFAC granted **MOL Plc. and MOL Serbia Investment Kft.** an additional licence connected with a possible ownership interest in **NIS a.d. Novi Sad**. It describes permission for specified transactions needed to transfer **Russian-issued securities to MOL Serbia Investment Kft.**, and related activities including certain required Russian governmental approvals. The stated validity runs until **February 28, 2027**.

The issuer expressly says this does **not** authorize completion of the transaction that would divest Russian ownership and control in NIS. Separate OFAC authorization would be needed for completion. This affirmative restriction is as important as the preparatory permission.

The record does not identify the securities, their issuer, ISIN, current holder, amount, valuation, consideration, transfer route or final disposition. Do not silently substitute NIS shares for “Russian-issued securities,” or treat the description as proof of an asset swap, cash substitution, Russian-government approval already received, actual acquisition financing or payment to Russia.

# November 19, 2026: Lukoil GL 131L and Berlin Ukraine support

## GL 131L

**J20-2026-114 and GL_131L** follow [J20-2026-032 / GL 131K](J20-Pres.md), [J20-2026-028 / GL 131J](Tmanch_CH7_Oct18_Lukoil_Divestment_Source_Note.md), and the earlier designation and accommodation sequence J20-040/041. They are not changes to the separate retail/Bulgaria licenses under J20-2026-019.

- [Operative signed General License 131L](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937386/download?inline=), paragraphs (a)–(e), effective November 19. Compare the [OFAC issuance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261119) and [FAQ 1224](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1224).
- **Paragraph (a)** covers specified negotiations and contingent contracts for LIG or an entity in which LIG owns at least 50% directly or indirectly, individually or in aggregate; expires at **12:01 a.m. EST December 19, 2026 (05:01 UTC)**. Sale performance needs distinct OFAC approval.
- **Paragraphs (b)/(c)** cover specified maintenance and wind-down until **12:01 a.m. EST February 20, 2027 (05:01 UTC)**; blocked-account use is limited to otherwise authorized (b) transactions. **Paragraph (d)** excludes transfers to any Russia-located person or account and retains other prohibitions subject to its terms. No transaction-specific application is inferred from a minority holding alone.
- **Who benefits:** mixed at the level of permitted options. Covered foreign operations, workers and host-country counterparties gain additional continuity, and nonblocked buyers more time. Preserving operating value may indirectly benefit LIG, while keeping closing controls and the Russia-located transfer exclusion limits the inference that Moscow gains proceeds. No actual transfer, closing, delisting, change to Russian revenue or intent is established.

**Financial follow-up, not a new policy event:** [Burgas refinery shareholder debt and trade setoff](Tmanch_CH7_Burgas_Litasco_Finance_Source_Note.md) documents the year-end amount drawn, the repayment demand and separate noncash trade netting. It identifies what to verify at any refinancing or authorized sale without presuming a closing or payment.

## November 25: consortium announces definitive conditional swap

**Later stage of J20-2026-114, not a second license or a closing.** The [consortium's announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261125144720/en/) ([full wire text](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72538179/american-led-consortium-announces-agreement-to-acquire-lukoil-internationals-global-energy-portfolio?lang=en&publisherId=58763726)) names Eldridge, the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation (DFC), UAE-based Catena Investment Holding and Xtellus Partners, with Citi as lead financial adviser. It says the consortium entered a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of LIG in exchange for restricted shares of PJSC Lukoil and other Russian companies, subject to OFAC and other regulatory approvals, due diligence and closing conditions. It describes no direct cash payment to Russia under this structure and a prospective DFC equity interest with no upfront taxpayer cost. The interest size and economic and contingent exposures are undisclosed; the signed agreement and an independent DFC commitment record were not obtained.

**Custody is the key unresolved term.** The release describes restricted custody for five years or until sanctions lift, without identifying the custodian or the precise release triggers. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/boehly-signs-us-backed-share-swap-deal-buy-lukoil-global-assets-2026-11-25/) attributes UAE escrow to a person familiar with the deal and an approximately $10 billion securities valuation to Boehly and a DFC official. The value is an attributed estimate, not contract face, a payment or booked proceeds. Reuters separately describes a DFC profit share after private investors' capital recovery and agreed return, not an independently verified financial obligation. [OFAC FAQ 1224](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1224) says Treasury expects severance of LIG's ties with Lukoil, amounts owed to stay blocked under U.S. jurisdiction until sanctions lift, and no upfront value or windfall, including via a swap. The public custody descriptions leave unresolved whether the actual terms would meet those expectations. GL 131L permits contingent contracting, not completion. No transaction-specific closing approval, completed sale, custody deposit, cash payment, share transfer or Russian access is verified.

**DFC authority is disputed, not adjudicated.** House Foreign Affairs Ranking Member [Gregory Meeks called for a halt](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/2026/11/meeks-calls-on-admin-to-immediately-halt-dfc-role-in-lukoil-deal/). [22 U.S.C. § 9612(e)(1)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-142/t22/s9612/) bars DFC support for projects in foreign countries of concern; [§ 9601(3)(F)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-142/t22/s9601/) includes Russia. The target portfolio is outside Russia; the seller is Russian and the proposed consideration is Russian securities. Application to this transaction requires the actual structure and a competent legal determination. The [prior DFC–IHC partnership](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-and-ihc-forge-new-investment-partnership-drive-strategic-capital) is separate. Reuters reports earlier participants IHC and the Al-Khayyat brothers left; the current release does not identify Catena's ultimate owners or show a Trump- or Kushner-family financial stake or payment.

**Potential beneficiaries, not realized flows:** the consortium and DFC would gain equity and potential operating returns if it closes; host-country operations and customers could gain continuity. Lukoil could gain restricted consideration with possible future value if released. No Russian government receipt, investor proceeds, or U.S. taxpayer return is shown. Watch the operative approval, custody jurisdiction and release triggers, DFC stake and obligations, and settlement records. Do not combine these with [J20-2026-123's separate Lukoil dividend](Tmanch_CH7_Nov23_Lukoil_Dividend_Source_Note.md) or book a flow in the ledger.

### Xtellus intermediary comparator: completed MTS sale, not Lukoil settlement

[Xtellus Capital's April 15, 2026 announcement](https://xtelluscapital.com/2026/04/15/xtellus-capital-facilitates-the-termination-and-divestment-of-depositary-receipt-programs-for-pjsc-mobile-telesystems-mts-and-pjsc-novorossiysk-commercial-sea-port-ncsp/) says it facilitated sales of underlying shares in JPMorgan's terminated MTS and Novorossiysk Commercial Sea Port depositary-receipt programs. [JPMorgan's April 13 final MTS notice](https://api.markitdigital.com/jpmadr-public/v1/cms/document?cmsId=8043843bdfc5456fafcbf1f694cc0f6a) independently confirms the MTS sale to an unnamed non-sanctioned purchaser under transaction-specific OFAC authorization. It reports a price of 30% of the December 29, 2025 Moscow close, gross $1.640704 and a final $1.582695 net cash distribution rate per receipt surrendered. JPMorgan reports no Russian exit tax or National Settlement Depository fee for that sale. [MTS's holder notice](https://ir.mts.ru/en/news_and_events/cancellation_of_adr_program) points holders to the depositary. The rate is not aggregate proceeds or evidence of specific redemptions, and the notice does not identify Xtellus's fee. Do not apply the MTS terms to Novorossiysk.

JPMorgan describes MTS as outside the applicable OFAC blocked categories. The completed MTS transaction gives one actual example of an exit route and a notice against which to test the reported Lukoil purchases. It does not establish a Lukoil purchase, discount, seller, intermediary mandate, or holder payout. This sale concerned Russian MTS equity receipts, not the [Vodafone Ukraine acquisition history or DFC refinancing](Tmanch_CH7_Nov_Vodafone_Ukraine_DFC_Source_Note.md).

**Ownership versus commercial history:** The broker-dealer's [2018 SEC-filed statement, Notes 1 and 8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/vprr/1900/19007893.pdf) records that VTB Capital IB Holding Limited sold its equity to existing officers and internal management in August 2018. Separately, the report describes brokerage and service agreements with VTB Capital PLC made as part of that transaction; it does not establish their continuation today. [FINRA BrokerCheck](https://files.brokercheck.finra.org/firm/firm_156048.pdf) lists Xtellus Partners LLC as managing direct owner of 75% or more of Xtellus Capital Partners Inc., and Paul Marshall Swigart in the 50% to under 75% indirect ownership band through that LLC. These percentages concern the broker-dealer, not the Lukoil consortium. No current VTB ownership, role in this swap or payment is established. No ledger flow is added.

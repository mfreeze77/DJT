# November 19, 2026: Lukoil GL 131L and Berlin Ukraine support

## GL 131L

**J20-2026-114 and GL_131L** follow [J20-2026-032 / GL 131K](J20-Pres.md), [J20-2026-028 / GL 131J](Tmanch_CH7_Oct18_Lukoil_Divestment_Source_Note.md), and the earlier designation and accommodation sequence J20-040/041. They are not changes to the separate retail/Bulgaria licenses under J20-2026-019.

- [Operative signed General License 131L](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937386/download?inline=), paragraphs (a)–(e), effective November 19. Compare the [OFAC issuance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261119) and [FAQ 1224](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1224).
- **Paragraph (a)** covers specified negotiations and contingent contracts for LIG or an entity in which LIG owns at least 50% directly or indirectly, individually or in aggregate; expires at **12:01 a.m. EST December 19, 2026 (05:01 UTC)**. Sale performance needs distinct OFAC approval.
- **Paragraphs (b)/(c)** cover specified maintenance and wind-down until **12:01 a.m. EST February 20, 2027 (05:01 UTC)**; blocked-account use is limited to otherwise authorized (b) transactions. **Paragraph (d)** excludes transfers to any Russia-located person or account and retains other prohibitions subject to its terms. No transaction-specific application is inferred from a minority holding alone.
- **Who benefits:** mixed at the level of permitted options. Covered foreign operations, workers and host-country counterparties gain additional continuity, and nonblocked buyers more time. Preserving operating value may indirectly benefit LIG, while keeping closing controls and the Russia-located transfer exclusion limits the inference that Moscow gains proceeds. No actual transfer, closing, delisting, change to Russian revenue or intent is established.

## Berlin

**J20-2026-115** records allied coordination, not a discrete U.S. transfer or guaranteed protection. It sits alongside, not inside, the equipment mobilization reported under [J20-2026-104](Tmanch_CH7_Nov12_EU_Winter_Source_Note.md), [J20-2026-102](Tmanch_CH7_Nov10_Denmark_Source_Note.md), and the separate prior PURL record (legacy J20-032).

- [Germany/France/UK/Ukraine joint statement](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/spilna-zayava-lideriv-nimechchini-franciyi-velikoyi-britaniy-107409) and [German federal government's coalition statement](https://www.bundesregierung.de/resource/blob/992814/2461956/9c822e93b93aba0d75cba1ca91c20025/2026-11-19-cow-data.pdf?download=1) document coordination, possible ceasefire monitoring and reassurance mechanisms and sustained assistance.
- The **EUR 70 billion** military assistance pledge for 2026 and at least equivalent 2027 support were set out in the [July 8 NATO Ankara Summit Declaration](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2026/07/08/the-ankara-summit-declaration). Do not add November reaffirmation to July's commitments, add it to named national suballocations, or treat it as spending. Do not conflate with a similarly sized 2027 financial shortfall described separately by the [German federal government](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-en/news/coalition-of-willing-berlin-2461990).
- The [UK prime minister's Berlin remarks](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/pm-speech-at-coalition-of-the-willing-in-berlin-19-november-2026) report U.S. representatives' participation and a UK commitment of 1,000 long-range drones; this is not delivery, and possible overlap with other British commitments is not resolved.
- **Who benefits:** Ukraine and allies gain a reaffirmed negotiating position and prospective deterrence mechanisms. U.S. participation is affirmative coordination evidence, but not a U.S. signature on the four-leader statement or evidence that any proposed guarantee already binds the U.S. Neither an implemented ceasefire, signed guarantee, deployed force, delivered kit nor a measured Russian cost follows from the statement.

Both entries are unscored. No prior event or estimate is rescored, and neither enters any historical concession/financial sum. Earlier independently scoped proposed event identifiers 109–113 are not reused.

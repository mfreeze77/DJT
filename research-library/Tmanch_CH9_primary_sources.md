## Source-control rules

1. ProPublica establishes the reported payment chain; the couple's later statement materially corroborates the core gift/hospitality event.
2. "Putin-linked" is supported by Kremlev's public relationship and state honor; it does not mean intelligence officer.
3. The IBA's interests are documented separately from motive for the wedding gift.
4. No quid pro quo is claimed without communication or transaction evidence linking value to a specific official act.
5. The refinery event is analyzed as a policy tradeoff with a real energy-market rationale and a reciprocity question.

# Vodafone Ukraine: DFC refinancing, acquisition-debt lineage and settlement status

**As of November 24, 2026.** The [September 16 DFC Board resolution BDR(26)20](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/BDR%2826%2920%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) authorizes up to $350 million in principal, plus interest, of financing to PrJSC VF Ukraine for Eurobond obligations and associated shareholder loans. The [Board announcement](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-announces-more-8-billion-investments-fuel-american-economic-and-national) names the NEQSOL subsidiary. The [public project summary](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/Board%20PIS%20-%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) describes a direct loan that would refinance debt and allow Vodafone Ukraine to redirect **its own cash** to at least $350 million of trusted-vendor equipment over the first five years. Its up-to-$700-million project cost is not U.S. financing, a subsidy or actual procurement. The [Operation Atlantic Resolve quarterly report, printed p. 11 and note 30](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=13) lists the telecom loan as a commitment, not a cash outlay. This is separate from military aid accounts and PURL/JUMPSTART.

## Which debt and who gets paid

| Date and stage | Documented amount and direction |
|---|---|
| December 3, 2019: sale closed | MTS sold its Ukraine holding vehicle Preludium B.V. through its Luxembourg subsidiary to the Bakcell/NEQSOL-controlled purchaser. MTS **received $645 million cash**; the $12 million deferred and $32 million fair-value contingent amounts were distinct at closing. The $734 million headline included about $84 million of potential earn-out rather than cash at close. [MTS closing release filed at SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000110465919069362/a19-24415_16k.htm) · [MTS Note 10 sale accounting](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000104746920002528/R17.htm) |
| February 2020: acquisition bridge refinanced | VFU Funding PLC originally issued **$500 million** of loan participation notes and on-lent the proceeds to PrJSC VF Ukraine, which used funds to refinance the **$464 million bridge** that Telco Solutions and Investments had obtained to acquire Preludium from MTS. These are different financing stages, not additive transfers to MTS. [VF Ukraine financial statements, Note 1](https://content.vodafone.ua/vf-strapi-prod/interim_condensed_consolidated_fs_as_of_30_june_2025_signed_a65b1c2e40.pdf) · [UK issuer accounts, pp. 3–5](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873/filing-history/MzUwNzU1MjYxMmFkaXF6a2N4/document) |
| November 23, 2026: reported financing agreement; proposed settlement | [Interfax quoting the issuer's exchange disclosure](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1240876.html) reports an entered financing agreement and intent to repay the underlying loan and redeem the remaining **$273.66 million face** at par plus accrued and unpaid interest before February 11, 2027. The underlying and note legs are one linked payoff, not two separate uses of $273.66 million. The press coverage is not a settlement statement; the November 23 exchange filing itself was not recovered. The separate recent partial tender at 98% is not the planned full redemption. |

Under the loan participation structure, future funds would go from DFC to Vodafone Ukraine (borrower), through repayment of the underlying loan to VFU Funding PLC (issuer), and from there to holders entitled at settlement. Reg S ISIN **XS2114201622**; Rule 144A **US918212AA96**. The [issuer's note page](https://www.vodafone.ua/company/investors/miscellaneous-disclosures/eurobonds) gives the instrument identifiers, $500 million original face and $300 million outstanding in its April 2025 update, not November 2026 outstanding. [Virtus Stone Harbor Emerging Markets Income Fund's SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501103/000094040026028940/xslFormNPORT-P_X01/primary_doc.xml) shows $560,418.08 of Rule 144A principal on May 29, 2026. [Transamerica Emerging Markets Debt’s SEC schedule, p. 5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/787623/000141036826100245/NPORT_I3CN_39069784_0726.htm) separately shows **$192,058 face** and **$190,864 reported value** of VF Ukraine through VFU Funding PLC as of July 31, 2026 (9.625% notes, schedule rounded to 9.63%, due February 11, 2027). These are separate dated portfolio snapshots, not a current holder list, ownership at settlement, or proof of receipt.

The financing could benefit Vodafone Ukraine by replacing near-term debt, current noteholders through early repayment, and approved vendors if procurement occurs. MTS is the **historical seller and recipient**; no evidence here identifies MTS as a current holder or payee, or connects these obligations to the Deutsche Bank leak, a Russian beneficiary in the present financing, or a Trump family payment. No score assigned. No verified draw amount, redemption date, final settlement, or underlying shareholder-loan payment. Look for the issuer's drawdown, redemption/cancellation and actual settlement notices under both ISINs and independently verify equipment orders or spend. Do not infer any use or receipt from the authorization ceiling minus outstanding note principal.

## Historical cash reconciliation and debt servicing

- **Sale proceeds to MTS, 2019–21: about $701.4 million identifiable cash.** Start with the approximately $645 million received at close, add $40.1 million received in September 2020 ($28.1 million closing adjustment and $12 million deferred consideration), and add $16.304 million of 2021 earn-out cash. The latter is the March 2021 installment reported as RUB 1.234 billion by MTS, not a second payment. The approximately $689 million acquisition-date accounting measure included deferred and fair-valued consideration, and the $734 million announced headline included a potential earn-out. Neither is cash received. [MTS 2020 Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000110465920126202/tm2036226d1_6k.htm) · [MTS 2022 Form 20-F, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000110465923046029/mbt-20221231x20f.htm) · [NEQSOL audited Note 25, printed p. 60](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09102634/filing-history/MzUwMjEzOTc4N2FkaXF6a2N4/document#page=96).
- **Noncash settlement, October 3, 2023.** By 2022 MTS had fully provided against the remaining receivable (RUB 1.867 billion at year-end 2021). NEQSOL reports a subsequent setoff against MTS's obligation to provide Vodafone Ukraine with billing-system source code and a $23 million accounting gain. Do not add that gain or the adjacent Audubon earn-out payments to the Vodafone cash total. Separately, MTS assigned UAH 2.182 billion of old Vodafone dividend claims net of withholding, via the seller to the purchaser at closing; assignment is not receipt. [VFU listing particulars, printed p. 3](https://ise-prodnr-eu-west-1-data-integration.s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/legacy/ListingParticulars_f719ea7a-dc7a-42e2-82ab-e1ba1809330d.PDF#page=17).
- **MTS shareholder distribution, February 2020.** MTS confirmed RUB 26,478,309,511.50 paid at RUB 13.25 per ordinary share. The approval considered both nine-month 2019 results and the Vodafone Ukraine sale. Then-majority owner Sistema corroborates the aggregate. No holder-level allocation or segregated sale proceeds were verified. The payout is a distinct downstream flow and is not additive to the MTS sale-cash total. [MTS Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000110465920021029/a20-8139_16k.htm) · [Sistema Q1 release, p. 6](https://sistema.com/upload/iblock/14a/14ad6f6d85be711eac7c5dafc3f8caf3.pdf#page=6).
- **Bridge financing and later bond servicing.** The [original use of proceeds](https://ise-prodnr-eu-west-1-data-integration.s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/legacy/ListingParticulars_f719ea7a-dc7a-42e2-82ab-e1ba1809330d.PDF#page=85) names Raiffeisen Bank International AG as bridge agent; it does not allocate payoffs by bank. [VF Ukraine's audited 2023 accounts, Notes 1 and 17](https://content.vodafone.ua/vf-strapi-prod/konsolidovana_finansova_zvitnist_stanom_na_i_za_rik_shho_zakincivsya_31_grudnya_2023_roku_ta_zvit_nezaleznogo_auditor_131790487e.pdf#page=18) date the UAH 11.569196 billion interest-free advance to Telco Solutions and Investments to February 10, 2020. It is a bridge-refinancing leg, not additional seller proceeds. [NEQSOL audited Note 43, printed p. 88](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09102634/filing-history/MzUwMjEzOTc4N2FkaXF6a2N4/document#page=124) reports $99.880 million face redeemed and canceled on February 6, 2025, $9 million in fees, and $12 million and $14 million interest paid in February and August. It records a notice obligation upon relevant authorization or payment-control changes for the holder par-redemption option. None is a 2026 DFC draw, redemption, or proof that the required currency permissions are in place for the reported plan.

MTS's historical receipts and the proposed DFC-funded settlement have distinct payees and dates. In the same group's separate titanium transaction, [NEQSOL reported](https://www.neqsolholding.com/news-and-media/neqsol-holding-finalizes-payment-for-ukraines-umcc/) payment for UMCC, and [audited Note 43, printed p. 89](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09102634/filing-history/MzUwMjEzOTc4N2FkaXF6a2N4/document#page=125) specifies UAH 3.938351 billion paid to Ukraine's state budget and the June 11, 2025 transfer of the 100% stake to Cemin Ukraine. This is an ownership-group overlap, not evidence of interproject cash, DFC financing or a U.S.–Ukraine Reconstruction Investment Fund contribution.

# Vodafone Ukraine: DFC refinancing, acquisition-debt lineage and settlement status

**As of November 24, 2026.** The [September 16 DFC Board resolution BDR(26)20](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/BDR%2826%2920%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) authorizes up to $350 million in principal, plus interest, of financing to PrJSC VF Ukraine for Eurobond obligations and associated shareholder loans. The [Board announcement](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-announces-more-8-billion-investments-fuel-american-economic-and-national) names the NEQSOL subsidiary. The [public project summary](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/Board%20PIS%20-%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) describes a direct loan that would refinance debt and allow Vodafone Ukraine to redirect **its own cash** to at least $350 million of trusted-vendor equipment over the first five years. Its up-to-$700-million project cost is not U.S. financing, a subsidy or actual procurement. The [Operation Atlantic Resolve quarterly report, printed p. 11 and note 30](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=13) lists the telecom loan as a commitment, not a cash outlay. This is separate from military aid accounts and PURL/JUMPSTART.

## Which debt and who gets paid

| Date and stage | Documented amount and direction |
|---|---|
| December 3, 2019: sale closed | MTS sold its Ukraine holding vehicle Preludium B.V. through its Luxembourg subsidiary to the Bakcell/NEQSOL-controlled purchaser. MTS **received $645 million cash**; the $12 million deferred and $32 million fair-value contingent amounts were distinct at closing. The $734 million headline included about $84 million of potential earn-out rather than cash at close. [MTS closing release filed at SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000110465919069362/a19-24415_16k.htm) · [MTS Note 10 sale accounting](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1115837/000104746920002528/R17.htm) |
| February 2020: acquisition bridge refinanced | VFU Funding PLC originally issued **$500 million** of loan participation notes and on-lent the proceeds to PrJSC VF Ukraine, which used funds to refinance the **$464 million bridge** that Telco Solutions and Investments had obtained to acquire Preludium from MTS. These are different financing stages, not additive transfers to MTS. [VF Ukraine financial statements, Note 1](https://content.vodafone.ua/vf-strapi-prod/interim_condensed_consolidated_fs_as_of_30_june_2025_signed_a65b1c2e40.pdf) · [UK issuer accounts, pp. 3–5](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873/filing-history/MzUwNzU1MjYxMmFkaXF6a2N4/document) |
| November 23, 2026: reported financing agreement; proposed settlement | [Interfax quoting the issuer's exchange disclosure](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1240876.html) reports an entered financing agreement and intent to repay the underlying loan and redeem the remaining **$273.66 million face** at par plus accrued and unpaid interest before February 11, 2027. The underlying and note legs are one linked payoff, not two separate uses of $273.66 million. The press coverage is not a settlement statement; the November 23 exchange filing itself was not recovered. The separate recent partial tender at 98% is not the planned full redemption. |

Under the loan participation structure, future funds would go from DFC to Vodafone Ukraine (borrower), through repayment of the underlying loan to VFU Funding PLC (issuer), and from there to holders entitled at settlement. Reg S ISIN **XS2114201622**; Rule 144A **US918212AA96**. The [issuer's note page](https://www.vodafone.ua/company/investors/miscellaneous-disclosures/eurobonds) gives the instrument identifiers, $500 million original face and $300 million outstanding in its April 2025 update, not November 2026 outstanding. [Virtus Stone Harbor Emerging Markets Income Fund's SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501103/000094040026028940/xslFormNPORT-P_X01/primary_doc.xml) shows $560,418.08 of Rule 144A principal on May 29, 2026; that dated holding is not evidence of ownership at settlement or receipt.

The financing could benefit Vodafone Ukraine by replacing near-term debt, current noteholders through early repayment, and approved vendors if procurement occurs. MTS is the **historical seller and recipient**; no evidence here identifies MTS as a current holder or payee, or connects these obligations to the Deutsche Bank leak, a Russian beneficiary in the present financing, or a Trump family payment. No score assigned. No verified draw amount, redemption date, final settlement, or underlying shareholder-loan payment. Look for the issuer's drawdown, redemption/cancellation and actual settlement notices under both ISINs and independently verify equipment orders or spend. Do not infer any use or receipt from the authorization ceiling minus outstanding note principal.

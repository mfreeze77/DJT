# Vodafone Ukraine: DFC refinancing, acquisition-debt lineage and settlement status

**As of November 25, 2026.** The [September 16 DFC Board resolution BDR(26)20](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/BDR%2826%2920%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) authorizes up to $350 million in principal, plus interest, of financing to PrJSC VF Ukraine for Eurobond obligations and associated shareholder loans. The [Board announcement](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-announces-more-8-billion-investments-fuel-american-economic-and-national) names the NEQSOL subsidiary. The [public project summary](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/Board%20PIS%20-%20Vodafone%20Ukraine_508.pdf) describes a direct loan that would refinance debt and allow Vodafone Ukraine to redirect **its own cash** to at least $350 million of trusted-vendor equipment over the first five years. Its up-to-$700-million project cost is not U.S. financing, a subsidy or actual procurement. The [Operation Atlantic Resolve quarterly report, printed p. 11 and note 30](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=13) lists the telecom loan as a commitment, not a cash outlay. This is separate from military aid accounts and PURL/JUMPSTART.

The financing could benefit Vodafone Ukraine by replacing near-term debt, current noteholders through early repayment, and approved vendors if procurement occurs. MTS is the **historical seller and recipient**; no evidence here identifies MTS as a current holder or payee, or connects these obligations to the Deutsche Bank leak, a Russian beneficiary in the present financing, or a Trump family payment. No score assigned. No verified draw amount, redemption date, final settlement, or underlying shareholder-loan payment. Look for the issuer's drawdown, redemption/cancellation and actual settlement notices under both ISINs and independently verify equipment orders or spend. Do not infer any use or receipt from the authorization ceiling minus outstanding note principal.

## Historical cash reconciliation and debt servicing

VFU Funding’s [audited 2024 issuer cash-flow statement, printed p. 24](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873/filing-history/MzUwNzU1MjYxMmFkaXF6a2N4/document) lists **$24,792,560 of interest received and the same amount paid in each of 2023 and 2024**. This illustrates the historical pass-through; the inward and outward legs are not additive. None of these interest flows is evidence of a 2026 DFC draw or current payee.

**Historical February 6, 2025 redemption: issuer-confirmed cash and use.** [VFU Funding’s FY2024 accounts, Notes 20–21, printed p. 42](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873/filing-history/MzUwNzU1MjYxMmFkaXF6a2N4/document#page=43) confirm receipt of the $99,880,000 prepayment from VF Ukraine and use for partial note redemption. Count the receipt and use as one pass-through, not an additional amount to the same redemption reported by NEQSOL. The issuer separately confirms that it received borrower funds for the consent fee and paid $20 per $1,000 of principal that day. The holder resolution passed February 5; the amendments extended maturity to February 11, 2027, reset the coupon to 9.625%, and subordinated the partial-prepayment loan and certain additional shareholder loans used to fund future tenders. This is not proof of a current shareholder-loan payoff.

The issuer reports $119,265,066 in subsequent repayment **or cancellation**, with resulting underlying loan principal of $280,614,934. The first figure includes the historical redemption and is not a second cash amount; the second is a historical debt balance, not the later reported approximately $273.66 million note balance. The difference does not establish a payment. The accounts were approved February 23, 2026 and [filed March 2](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873/filing-history); [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12381873) lists FY2025 accounts due December 30, 2026. For the present DFC-backed plan, the equivalent evidence would be dated funding and matching issuer redemption/settlement evidence for XS2114201622 or US918212AA96. Neither was verified.

- **Sale proceeds to MTS, 2019–21: about $701.4 million identifiable cash.** Start with the approximately $645 million received at close, add $40.1 million received in September 2020 ($28.1 million closing adjustment and $12 million deferred consideration), and add $16.304 million of 2021 earn-out cash. The latter is the March 2021 installment reported as RUB 1.234 billion by MTS, not a second payment. The approximately $689 million acquisition-date accounting measure included deferred and fair-valued consideration, and the $734 million announced headline included a potential earn-out. ## Related-party servicing and pre-DFC investment

[VF Ukraine's 2025 interim statements, Notes 15 and 20, printed pp. 19 and 24](https://content.vodafone.ua/vf-strapi-prod/interim_condensed_consolidated_fs_as_of_30_june_2025_signed_a65b1c2e40.pdf#page=22) identify **Telco Investments B.V.** as the immediate parent that advanced **$49.6 million** in February 2025, used to partially redeem the notes and still outstanding when the interim statements were prepared. A separate June 2025 parent facility, denominated in USD up to the equivalent of UAH 660 million, had **$2 million drawn**. Both agreements carried 10% interest and 2028 maturity. The same report records a distinct July **$10 million** facility from an unnamed related party, with **$6.3 million received** into a foreign account for note repayment and **fully repaid by August 27, 2025**. That draw is not another $6.4 million of note face: the latter is the amount canceled in July–August, and the source does not allocate this draw to specific notes. These are historical records; the named parent is a candidate counterparty to check, not a verified 2026 DFC payee. Require updated principal, continued eligibility, and actual settlement or receipt before booking any shareholder-loan payment.

[NEQSOL's Note 43, printed p. 88](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09102634/filing-history/MzUwMjEzOTc4N2FkaXF6a2N4/document#page=124) records a broader **$19 million note face canceled July–November 2025**, not additive to the July–August subset. It separately records **€4 million paid September–November 2025** toward VF Ukraine's estimated **€65 million share** of the joint Black Sea cable [Kardesa](https://www.vodafone.com/news/newsroom/technology/vodafone-group-and-vodafone-ukraine-to-build-new-submarine-cable-system-across-the-black-sea). That is group-reported pre-DFC expenditure, not a new DFC draw or vendor payment under the 2026 loan. The filing does not name the payee for those installments.

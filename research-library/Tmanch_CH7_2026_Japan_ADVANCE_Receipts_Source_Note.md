**Reconciliation:** Together these two recipient-confirmed transfers are $464.5 million, including $364.5 million in IBRD lending with Japanese credit backing and $100 million in IDA SPUR lending. This is a subtotal of these two notices only, not all Japan-backed World Bank disbursements. IDA SPUR is a separate World Bank channel; this source does not assign its $100 million component to Japan. The [World Bank mechanism brief](https://www.worldbank.org/en/brief/2026/04/13/world-bank-group-donor-financing-mechanisms-for-supporting-ukraine) describes ADVANCE as IBRD credit enhancement funded by deposited promissory notes, with $12.5 billion of Japanese notes and $10.6 billion in signed IBRD loans at the brief's reporting basis. Neither portfolio amount is an additional recipient receipt or a guarantee payout. The [Canada-backed PEACE transfer](Tmanch_CH7_Sep22_PEACE_Canadian_Guarantee_Source_Note.md) is a distinct disbursement, not a source or component of the transfers tracked here.

Ukraine's state-budget general fund is the immediate recipient. The loan programs support education and health reform; the performance indicators describe what permitted the draw, not an audited onward allocation of that cash to any school, hospital, supplier, or other payee. Lender-side transaction and instrument reconciliation, exact value dates, any guarantee call or Japanese cash payout, and matching downstream cash or delivery were not verified. No effect on Russian finances or link to separate corporate financing is inferred.

## November 26: SURGE and RISE (J20-2026-147)

The [Finance Ministry confirms](https://www.mof.gov.ua/uk/news/ukraine_receives_usd_450_million_through_world_bank_programs_with_japans_support-6041) USD 450 million reached the state-budget general fund: USD 225 million under SURGE for fiscal governance and reconstruction and USD 225 million under RISE for private-sector support. The ministry identifies USD 415 million of Japan-backed IBRD lending and USD 35 million of IDA credit. [The prime minister’s statement, as reported by UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/ukraine-has-received-dollar450-million-from-the-world-bank-to-support-businesses-and-reconstruction), places all USD 35 million of IDA credit and USD 190 million of Japan-backed IBRD lending in RISE, leaving USD 225 million of Japan-backed IBRD lending for SURGE.

SURGE is a draw under the second additional-financing agreement (IBRD K0230), not another USD 584.25 million receipt. [World Bank Finances One](https://financesone.worldbank.org/countries/ukraine) dates approval to June 26; the ministry dates signing to June 29 and effectiveness to July 13. A cumulative lender loan balance does not establish the value date of this draw. RISE indicators were validated, per the ministry; they do not identify onward payments to enterprises or individual cash beneficiaries.

The three releases together document USD 914.5 million in World Bank budget receipts: USD 779.5 million in Japan-backed IBRD loans and USD 135 million in IDA financing. That is a subtotal for these specified receipts, not a portfolio total; components, cumulative program amounts, ceilings, and any Japanese guarantee exposure are not additional cash. No Japanese guarantee call or separate Japanese payment was verified.

## November 26 agreements, not receipts

In a [separate signing notice](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/news/ukraina-i-svitovyi-bank-pidpysaly-uhody-pro-finansuvannia-prohram-rise-ta-reach), Ukraine reported three agreements totaling **$676 million**: one **$500 million IBRD loan for RISE third additional financing**, and REACH housing agreements for a **$106 million IBRD loan** and **$70 million grant** (IBRD and IDA involved). The [RISE appraisal](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100126064068796/pdf/P505616-3f4c7583-e230-4997-8445-99b371ff3432.pdf#page=5) describes Japan-backed credit enhancement, $25 million capitalized and $475 million linked to qualifying additional business lending, all within the $500 million. No guarantee call or new loan withdrawal is established. The $225 million RISE receipt in J20-2026-147 included $190 million Japan-backed IBRD and $35 million IDA per the [Cabinet receipt notice](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/ukraina-otrymala-450-mln-vid-svitovoho-banku-ta-pid-harantii-iaponii-na-pidtrymku-biznesu-i-vidbudovy-serhii-koretskyi). Matching program names and dates do not establish that an individual withdrawal came from the newly signed loan.

[REACH (P517177)](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P517177) is a prospective mortgage program with [Ukrfinzhytlo](https://ukrfinzhytlo.in.ua/language/en/reach-project/); participating banks originate loans and the project supports down payments and interest subsidies. This is separate from [CEB HOME III](Tmanch_CH7_Sep30_CEB_HOME_III_Source_Note.md) compensation. Track financing IDs, effectiveness, withdrawals and first recipient-level evidence. Neither the $500 million nor the $176 million belongs in the cash total.

# September and October World Bank budget receipts: LEARN and THRIVE

Historical receipts backfilled November 26, 2026. Event dates are the dates by which Ukraine's Finance Ministry reported receipt, not independently confirmed bank value dates.

| [October 28: THRIVE](https://www.mof.gov.ua/en/news/ukraina_otrimala_4045_mln_dolariv_ssha_vid_iaponii_ta_svitovogo_banku_v_mezhakh_proiektu_thrive-5990) | $404.5 million | Ukraine's state-budget general fund received $304.5 million in Japan-backed ADVANCE financing from IBRD and $100 million in IDA lending under SPUR. **Reconciliation:** Together these two recipient-confirmed transfers are $464.5 million, including $364.5 million in IBRD lending with Japanese credit backing and $100 million in IDA SPUR lending. This is a subtotal of these two notices only, not all Japan-backed World Bank disbursements. IDA SPUR is a separate World Bank channel; this source does not assign its $100 million component to Japan. The [World Bank mechanism brief](https://www.worldbank.org/en/brief/2026/04/13/world-bank-group-donor-financing-mechanisms-for-supporting-ukraine) describes ADVANCE as IBRD credit enhancement funded by deposited promissory notes, with $12.5 billion of Japanese notes and $10.6 billion in signed IBRD loans at the brief's reporting basis. Neither portfolio amount is an additional recipient receipt or a guarantee payout. The [Canada-backed PEACE transfer](Tmanch_CH7_Sep22_PEACE_Canadian_Guarantee_Source_Note.md) is a distinct disbursement, not a source or component of these two transfers.

Ukraine's state-budget general fund is the immediate recipient. The loan programs support education and health reform; the performance indicators describe what permitted the draw, not an audited onward allocation of that cash to any school, hospital, supplier, or other payee. Lender-side transaction and instrument reconciliation, exact value dates, any guarantee call or Japanese cash payout, and matching downstream cash or delivery were not verified. No effect on Russian finances or link to separate corporate financing is inferred.

# Hungary–Ukraine: civilian goods, hospital equipment and separate budget authority

**J20-2026-129, November 24, 2026.** [Budapest reported](https://kormany.hu/hirek/karpataljan-talalkozott-magyar-peter-es-volodimir-zelenszkij) that Prime Minister Péter Magyar brought civilian donations valued at approximately **HUF 1 billion** to Ukraine, including medicines, patient monitors, energy equipment and vehicles. This is the donor’s account of in-kind aid, not a verified cash transfer or a recipient-by-recipient receipt.

The [official gazette, no. 178, pp. 6380–6382](https://magyarkozlony.hu/hivatalos-lapok/QipCpmwj2Gu0utVoHdzj6afed8ff263ce/dokumentumok/8d12e3aac17e3a2d25c9b4996596f1ffd53475be/letoltes#page=2) records three different decisions:

- **1382 and 1384:** one **HUF 1,001,050,000** central emergency-reserve reallocation to the Bethlen Gábor Fund for the Zrínyi Ilona Social Development Program (HUF 1 billion for the program and HUF 1.05 million for administration). The decision directs immediate reallocation; the account and return of unused funds are due March 31, 2027. Do not count 1382 and 1384 separately or count either as downstream grant payment.
- **1383:** transfer of ten patient monitors without charge to Linner Bertalan Central District Hospital in Berehove. Monitors also appear in the broader consignment description. No independent hospital acceptance record for those ten units or separate valuation was found. Do not add their value separately.

**Who benefits and what is not established.** These goods directly support Ukrainian civilian services, a distinct channel from [Norway’s air-defense delivery](Tmanch_CH7_Nov24_Norway_Ukraine_Delivery_Source_Note.md) and the [EU 2027 funding proposal](Tmanch_CH7_Nov24_Ukraine_2027_Financing_Source_Note.md). Budapest’s readout reports support for humanitarian cooperation and opposition to tying Ukraine financing to the EU’s seven-year budget; this is a stated position, not by itself a recorded veto. [Kyiv’s readout](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/volodimir-zelenskij-i-peter-madyar-obgovorili-energetichnu-s-107481) records energy discussions; no signed fuel supply deal, product origin, price, volume or payment verified. No aggregate aid cash score or new delivery valuation assigned.

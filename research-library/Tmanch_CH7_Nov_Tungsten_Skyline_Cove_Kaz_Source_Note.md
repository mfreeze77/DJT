# Kazakhstan tungsten: Skyline/Cove Kaz restructuring and potential distribution

**J20-2026-119** records an ended corporate merger and a separate contingent exchange/distribution. It does not record a U.S. government decision, a family payment, or a Russia-policy score.

- The [November 2 Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026114713/ea0310448-6k_skyline.htm) and [signed termination agreement](https://ir.skylinebuildersgroupholding.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001213900-26-114713/ea031044801ex10-1.htm) identify November 1 as the effective termination of the April business combination. A same-day binding term sheet contemplates conversion of Cove Kaz into Kaz Resources Inc. and a separate direct listing. It contemplates Skyline receiving **63,994,955 restricted Class A Cove Kaz shares**, roughly 26% ahead of listing and before a contemplated employee plan, in exchange for redemption of Skyline's existing Kaz Resources LLC equity and cancellation of all amounts due on its loan. Skyline and Cove Kaz plan to register a pro rata distribution to Skyline shareholders. The [November 12 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026118516/ea031203501ex99-1.htm) forecasts a Q4 2026 record date and places the distribution before the expected Q1 2027 completion of a separate proposed transaction; that is a planning sequence, not a declared record or payment date. Skyline's [November 12 ticker change to CRMN](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026118491/ea0311820-6k_skyline.htm) is not Cove Kaz's direct listing.
- Skyline's [20-F/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026092307/ea0302498-20fa1_skyline.htm) reports **$45 million advanced in private loan principal**, $23.1 million April 22 and $21.9 million June 10. This is actual reported Skyline-to-Cove Kaz financing, distinct from Skyline's older $20 million equity subscription and from proposed conversion of the amounts due. The amended facility subsumed the earlier tranche; don't add it again. The [6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026114713/ea0310448-6k_skyline.htm) and [Cove Kaz issuer release](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/cove-kaz-capital-group-announces-termination-business-combination-agreement-skyline) separately describe a proposed $32 million placement with American Ventures LLC, four million shares or equivalent units at $8, subject to diligence, documentation and closing. The issuer didn't identify the series. Do not equate the placement with the loan, assume it closed, or use its price to book a distribution or proceeds.
- The [November 17 Schedule 13G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026121533/primary_doc.xml) reports American Ventures LLC, Series XIX SKBL directly held 1,675,788 **Skyline** Class A shares, 6.36% using the November 17 class denominator of 26,355,502. Shared authority reported by affiliates is the same block, not additive. [Dominari's 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000143774926035967/domh20260930_10q.htm) reports its Series XIX investment and separately its 90% interest in the management entities; its [proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390026116888/ea0309773-04.htm) reports Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump each beneficially owned 5.55% of Dominari common stock and describes advisory relationships. These document exposure to Dominari and a possible indirect route if the vehicle qualifies and receives value, not a direct Cove Kaz stake for either individual, a quantified personal distribution, or improper influence. Do not multiply class, fund, manager and issuer percentages: the class eligibility, fund economics and record-date holdings have not been established.
- [Tau-Ken Samruk](https://tks.kz/en/severniy-katpar-llp/) confirms a 70/30 ownership split in the Kazakh project vehicle. Two company materials give [April 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026084658/ea0300275-425_skyline.htm) and [April 30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026090743/ea030151601ex99-1.htm) for the transaction; retain the date difference pending a closing instrument. This equity acquisition is not the Skyline share distribution. The [DFC primary announcement](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-highlights-landmark-critical-minerals-investments-strengthen-us-national) describes letters of interest; the [sponsor's filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2031009/000121390026087848/ea0301400-425_skyline.htm) describes indicative EXIM and DFC support. A letter of interest is not a federal credit approval, executed loan commitment or disbursement. No modeled value or private investment is federal money.

**What changed:** private financing and equity exposure can persist without the canceled merger or federal financing. Cove Kaz received the reported private advances; Skyline investors have a possible distribution route if the new structure closes. An eventual supply benefit for U.S. customers remains prospective. Check CRMN under CIK 2031009 and separate Kaz Resources Inc. listing filings for an exact record date, eligible classes, definitive terms, actual shares delivered and recipient economics; check federal financing on its own record. No completed distribution, federal disbursement or family proceeds verified.

## Reuters attribution and response

[Reuters, October 15, Erin Banco and Gram Slattery](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/exclusiveorder-to-limit-starshield-for-ukraine-came-from-inside-white-house-sources-say-4952816): two U.S. officials and a briefed Western official anonymously attributed a Starshield restriction instruction to the White House, transmitted to Pentagon leaders; neither decisionmaker nor implementer was named. A senior Ukrainian official said October 13–14 inquiries received similar attribution.

Ukraine's Defence Ministry source reported a remaining 160-kph ceiling October 15; Ukrainian officials described impaired strike and interceptor operations.

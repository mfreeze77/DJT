# October 16, 2026: Zelenskyy's European Council address

[Official English address](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/zvernennya-prezidenta-ukrayini-do-uchasnikiv-zasidannya-yevr-106921), dated October 16, 15:51 (timezone unspecified), retrieved that day through the presidency news index after direct retrieval failed.

Zelenskyy predicted diesel-related Russian war funding without sufficient relief from Hormuz disruption. He sought at least €70 billion additional support for 2027, a winter package of 300 Patriot interceptors, and an EU-controlled custodian for frozen Russian assets.

These are forecasts and requests, not payments, commitments or deliveries. Conditional benefits are Ukrainian/allied financing, air defence and custody leverage; Russian revenue remains an attributed forecast. The satellite passage identifies neither Starshield nor an order's author.

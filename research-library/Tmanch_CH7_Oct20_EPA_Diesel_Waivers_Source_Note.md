# October 20 EPA diesel renewals, effective October 25

**J20-2026-051/052; CH7-EPA-DIESEL-001/002.** Signed legal actions, not measured output or price relief. Acquired October 25.

## Verified scope

- **EPA-DIESEL-S001 — Texas:** [October 20 signed letter to Governor Abbott](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/fuel-waiver-txled-ulsd-2026-1020.pdf), pp. 1–4. EPA, with DOE concurrence, renews Clean Air Act relief from TXLED and specified diesel-sulfur requirements effective **October 25–November 13**. The operative ceiling is **up to 500 ppm** (p. 4); earlier passages describe eligible engines using less-than-500 wording. Eligibility covers specified older engines, including pre-2007 highway and pre-Tier-4 nonroad engines, not every diesel vehicle. Texas manufacturers retain the specified batch reporting.
- **EPA-DIESEL-S002 — Arctic Alaska:** [October 20 signed letter to Governor Dunleavy](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/fuel-waiver-alaska-arctic-2026-1020.pdf), pp. 1–5. Effective **October 25–November 13**, permits **up to 5,000 ppm** for eligible older engines fueled and operated above the Arctic Circle, including pre-1994 highway and pre-Tier-3 nonroad engines. The letter defines its geographic term as Alaska north of the 66th parallel. Local topping-refinery supply is the stated mechanism. Except for warning-bearing product-transfer documentation, the letter waives other diesel-sulfur reporting requirements under Part 1090.

Both letters exclude engines with particulate filters or catalytic controls, require separation from ULSD and warnings, and preserve unrelated applicable requirements. **November 13 is not an absolute end to all qualifying sales:** documented qualifying fuel introduced into pipelines or certified at manufacturing/import facilities before expiry may be sold until the identified volume is depleted. Other state/local restrictions may require separate action.

**EPA-DIESEL-S003:** The inspected [EPA fuel-waiver index](https://www.epa.gov/gasoline-standards/fuel-waivers) calls these renewals and lists the October 5 Texas predecessor through October 24 and September 29 Arctic predecessor effective October 5–24. Predecessor PDFs were not independently acquired in this pass. Do not describe October 25 as the first use of this relief.

# Naftogaz natural gas financing: supplier payments, advances and contracts

Checked November 26, 2026. Evidence for **J20-2026-136**. Distinct from the Energy Community Ukraine Energy Support Fund and J20-2026-131.

| Instrument and stage | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Norway grant through the EBRD: agreement limit | The [Naftogaz unaudited H1 2026 consolidated financial statements, PDF pp. 19 and 21](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=21) report that the February 13 supplement to the August 26, 2025 agreement increased the limit by €85.17 million (UAH 4.363 billion in the company’s reporting) to €165.73 million. The ceiling is not cumulative verified supplier cash. |
| EBRD crisis response grant: Q1 pay-through | [Notes 880000.7 and 880000.18, PDF pp. 35 and 51](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=35) report UAH 2.553 billion paid directly by the EBRD to suppliers of gas purchased by Naftogaz. Naftogaz recognized grant income alongside the cost of gas sold. This is supplier cash, not a separate deposit to Naftogaz or profit. |
| EBRD crisis response grant: Q2 advances | [Notes 880000.10 and 880000.13, PDF pp. 43 and 46](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=43) report the remaining UAH 1.810 billion as advances to gas suppliers, with associated grant funding deferred. The two reported amounts sum to the UAH 4.363 billion equivalent of the February increase. Do not add that equivalent again or infer vendor names, individual payments, or later advance liquidation. |
| EIB gas reserve loan | The [EIB signing announcement](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2026-034-eib-provides-an-additional-eur50-million-to-naftogaz-to-keep-ukraine-s-energy-system-resilient-through-winter) and Naftogaz accounts describe the January increase; [Note 880000.15, PDF p. 48](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=48) reports an additional €50 million raised in February and gas purchased under the loan injected into storage. This raises the original €300 million facility to €350 million. The company also translates the €50 million increment to UAH 2.553 billion, numerically the same as the separate Q1 grant payment, not the same flow. |
| EBRD emergency gas revolving loans: limits, repayment and restructuring | [Naftogaz accounts, Note 880000.15, PDF p. 48](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=48) identify the €770 million in the [November 26 Finance Ministry readout](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/over_eur_11_billion_in_ebrd_financing_since_the_start_of_the_full-scale_invasion_sergii_marchenko_discussed_further_support_for_ukraine_with_matteo_patrone-6039) as the combined limit of the revolving credit lines agreed in 2025. Naftogaz reports repaying €270 million to the EBRD in February–March 2026 and a UAH 25.579 billion balance on the European Commission-guaranteed EBRD loan at June 30. [PDF p. 19](https://www.naftogaz.com/short/ec692928#page=19) gives €499.9 million in outstanding principal on the August 12, 2025 loan at June 30 and reports a July restructuring extending maturity to June 5, 2029. The UAH balance and EUR principal are not additive. The repayment is cash to the lender, not incoming financing or a supplier payment. The facility limit does not establish dated gross draws or supplier allocations. |
| Netherlands EBRD grant | The [November 26 Finance Ministry readout](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/over_eur_11_billion_in_ebrd_financing_since_the_start_of_the_full-scale_invasion_sergii_marchenko_discussed_further_support_for_ukraine_with_matteo_patrone-6039) dates signing of the €23 million grant to November 6; [Naftogaz announced it November 10](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/news/naftogaz-secures-23-million-dutch-grant-for-gas-purchases). The [EBRD announcement](https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2026/ebrd-and-netherlands-support-naftogaz-with--23-million-grant.html) confirms the grant is for peak-demand gas. No disbursement or vendor settlement was verified. Naftogaz’s [procurement notices](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/ebrd_funds_gas_procurement) show prequalification, not payment to named suppliers. |
| ORLEN commercial gas contract | [Naftogaz](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/news/naftogaz-group-and-orlen-sign-new-contract-for-the-supply-of-three-us-lng-cargoes) and [ORLEN](https://www.orlen.pl/en/about-the-company/media/press-releases/current/2026/november-2026/orlen-to-supply-more-gas-to-ukraine-new-contract-with-naftogaz) announced the November 18 contract for U.S. LNG-derived gas from three partial cargoes in Q1 2027. Separately, [Naftogaz reported delivery](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/news/naftogaz-zabezpechyv-pershu-postavku-amerykanskogo-lng-v-ukrainu-v-2026-rotsi-mayzhe-100-mln-kub-m) of almost 100 million cubic metres in February 2026 with ORLEN. Neither disclosure assigns any cargo to the EBRD grant or EIB draw. |

Naftogaz and U.S. EXIM agreed in June to [develop a mechanism](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/news/naftogaz-ta-exim-bank-ssha-rozpochynayut-robotu-nad-mekhanizmom-finansuvannya-na-300-mln) of up to $300 million for U.S. equipment, works and services. That is not a verified EXIM authorization or draw, and no link to the LNG contracts or named supplier receipts was established. The disclosed economic beneficiaries are Ukraine’s gas reserve and consumers, and the unnamed suppliers receiving payments or advances. ORLEN is a named commercial counterparty, not a verified grant payee.

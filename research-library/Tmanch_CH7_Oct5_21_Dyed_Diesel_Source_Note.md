# Domestic dyed-diesel relief: executive direction and IRS implementation

Reviewed October 29, 2026. J20-2026-067 / CH7-DYED-DIESEL-001. Newly verified historical actions, not new October 29 orders. Unscored pending comparative review.

## Inspected originals and scope

- **DD-S001:** [October 5 White House order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/emergency-tax-relief-on-diesel-fuel/) directs conditional deferral to the extent legally authorized, penalty relief and guidance. Covered language is October 5–December 31. Section 4 explores forgiveness, including legislation; it does not itself eliminate liability. Presidential direction is documented, unlike an inferred personal order from an agency press release. Transportation enforcement remains distinct.
- **DD-S002:** [IRS IR-2026-122, October 14](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-provides-dyed-diesel-fuel-relief-related-to-excise-tax-following-president-trumps-executive-order) announces nationwide penalty and collection/enforcement relief for highway dyed-diesel uses/sales October 1–December 31, including suspension of federal on-road dyed-fuel inspections. Further tax-postponement guidance and exploration of forgiveness remain prospective. Preserve the IRS October 1 versus order October 5 date distinction without inferring an adjudicated violation. State requirements remain separate.
- **DD-S003:** [IRS IR-2026-125, October 21](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/temporary-reporting-and-recordkeeping-relief-for-certain-fuel-uses) limits reporting/recordkeeping relief to failures solely attributable to section 4041(a) liability for covered dyed-diesel uses October 1–December 31. It covers specified Form 720 obligations and does not require later reconstruction. Other liabilities and independently applicable exemption, credit and refund records remain required. This is neither general destruction permission nor removal of sanctions/customs documentation.
- **DD-S004:** [October 23 IRS FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/dyed-diesel-frequently-asked-questions) preserves unrelated records and state rules, excludes clear diesel and dyed kerosene, and includes dyed biodiesel mixtures. The already-imposed LUST tax is not imposed again; a blanket rate-times-volume savings calculation would be unsound. A residual-dye grace period is considered, not implemented by this text.

All four original HTML records were acquired and inspected; hashes appear in [source manifest](Tmanch_CH7_Oct29_Evening_Source_Manifest.json). Agency republications are the same source family. No taxpayer take-up, forgone receipts, final forgiveness or realized net savings is established.

# Ukraine Energy Support Fund: U.S. grant notice, deposits and recipient delivery stages

**J20-2026-131.** Keep the administrative notice and the cash outcome separate. The [Energy Community Secretariat](https://www.energy-community.org/news/Energy-Community-News/2025/03/12.html) reported receiving a USAID termination notice on February 27, 2025 for the **$75 million** grant arrangement ($25 million original plus $50 million extension pledged). The administrator's [year-end 2024 table](https://www.energy-community.org/dam/jcr%3A9590e7cb-5276-4714-950a-9e991e7aa8b2/2024_Annual_Report_UESF.pdf#page=12) records **€22,984,279 deposited**. Its [June 30, 2025 table](https://www.energy-community.org/dam/jcr%3A3bf59282-d25b-466a-bbd8-1c2257af5966/25422_EC_UESF_v02_MM.2025-10-21-12-54-41.pdf#page=9) shows **€71,246,827 deposited**, an increase of **€48,262,548** between snapshots. The [Special IG March 2026 report, p. 42 and n. 243](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q2_mar2026_final_508.pdf) separately cites State for **$75 million contributed**. Do not add the U.S. and euro reports as separate money. Neither fund table dates the individual wires within January–June 2025; there is no evidence here to distinguish a transfer before the notice, a reversal, or another administrative path. The notice did not translate into a $75 million shortfall versus the fund's reported cumulative U.S. receipts.

The same U.S. deposited total appears in the [June 30, 2026 table](https://www.energy-community.org/dam/jcr%3A0e8ad6cb-117a-4f1d-b9bd-080fac96cbb2/26304_EC_Half-Year_v02_JM_LQ.2026-10-13-12-25-13.pdf#page=10); the November dashboard's U.S. **signed** column is within €1 and is not a newer U.S.-only deposit ledger. No 2026 U.S. increase verified. The IG's separately terminated USAID **Energy Security Project** had about $250 million remaining, and the implementer reported about $37 million unpaid, at March 31, 2026. Neither is a UESF balance or settlement. The [DFC reconstruction investment fund](Tmanch_CH7_Nov_URIF_Seed_Cash_Source_Note.md) is different again.

**Fund-wide comparison.** On November 24 the [fund administrator](https://www.energy-community.org/Ukraine/Fund/overview.html) reported **€2,577,206,986 pledged**, **€2,485,718,171 signed** and **€2,480,170,063 deposited**, up **€539,241,272** over June 30. The €97,036,923 pledge-to-deposit gap is not undelivered equipment. On November 6 the [administrator](https://www.energy-community.org/news/Energy-Community-News/2026/11/6.html) separately reported **€812 million of equipment received** by more than 60 Ukrainian energy companies since inception. The fund pays suppliers on Ukrainian companies' behalf. These different dates and stages cannot be added or used to infer a donor-specific delivery or receipt into Ukraine's general budget. The result benefits Ukraine's civilian energy resilience, distinct from [EU state-budget cash](Tmanch_CH7_Nov20_EU_Ukraine_Facility_Source_Note.md) and from Norway and Hungary in-kind reporting.

**Separate Azerbaijani channel.** Azerbaijan's [energy ministry](https://minenergy.gov.az/en/xeberler-arxivi/00904) reported dispatch on October 7, 2026 of AZN 2.5 million in electrical equipment. Ukraine's energy ministry reported October 29 receipt valued at over $1.47 million, [as quoted by Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.ua/rubric-vidbudova/4179335-ukraina-otrimala-vid-azerbajdzanu-partiu-energoobladnanna-majze-na-15-miljona.html), with nine transformers and 186 km of cable. Timing, equipment and reported value point to the same consignment; don't add the two valuations or include them in the UESF deposits. Separately, [Naftogaz](https://www.naftogaz.com/en/news/ukrayina-vpershe-importuye-azerbaydzhanskyy-haz-transbalkanskym-marshrutom) reported a July 2025 SOCAR Energy Ukraine test purchase, while a [November 2026 Ukrainian readout](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/denys-shmyhal-ta-parviz-shakhbazov-obhovoryly-spivpratsiu-v-enerhetytsi-dyversyfikatsiiu-postachannia-hazu-ta-rozvytok-lohistychnykh-marshrutiv) describes regular supply and longer-term contracting as future work. No price or settlement verified; this is neither a donation nor evidence of a cross-project payment.

### Original U.S. grant milestones

The administrator's [April 25, 2023 announcement](https://www.energy-community.org/news/Energy-Community-News/2023/04/25b.html) identified the initial $25 million pledge; its [November 20, 2023 announcement](https://www.energy-community.org/news/Energy-Community-News/2023/11/20.html) reported signing the original grant. The administrator described the $50 million addition as pledged in 2024. Neither the announcement, signing nor extension dates establish a wire date; the tables still do not resolve whether the later balance increase occurred before or after February 27, 2025.

## Procurement beneficiaries and supplier payments, January–June 2026

The fund's [half-year report, Figure 7](https://www.energy-community.org/dam/jcr%3A0e8ad6cb-117a-4f1d-b9bd-080fac96cbb2/26304_EC_Half-Year_v02_JM_LQ.2026-10-13-12-25-13.pdf#page=15) records **about €42 million in newly signed purchase agreements with U.S.-registered suppliers**, 20% of €212.2 million in new agreements by value (328 agreements across all suppliers). It shows Ukraine €121 million (57%), Germany €20 million (10%), Denmark €9 million (4%), Romania €6 million (3%) and UK €4 million (2%). These figures are rounded country categories. They describe commercial contract value by supplier registration, not production origin, net income, or cash received by suppliers in any one country.

The fund reports **€94.6 million actually paid across 731 transactions** in the half-year and **€782.9 million cumulatively paid** since inception as of June 30 across all suppliers (p. 15; €94.6 million is part of, not additional to, €782.9 million). The [fund rules](https://www.energy-community.org/Ukraine/Fund/overview.html) describe direct payment to suppliers on behalf of eligible Ukrainian energy companies. The report separately records **€720.9 million of cumulative reported deliveries** through June (p. 16), distinct from cash disbursed. It does not trace specific supplier payments to the U.S. grant, show how much of the €42 million U.S. contract value had been paid, or disclose supplier profit. Keep donors, procurement counterparties and Ukrainian equipment recipients distinct; do not add these amounts to each other or to donor deposits.

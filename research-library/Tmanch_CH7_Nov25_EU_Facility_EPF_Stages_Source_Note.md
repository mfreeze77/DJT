# November 20–25, 2026: two separate stages of EU Ukraine support

## Ukraine Facility: Commission proposal, not adopted or paid

[COM(2026) 642 final, 2026/0368(NLE), Articles 1–2 and recitals](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16195-2026-INIT/en/pdf) proposes **€2,946,297,070 gross before clearing prefinancing** against a November 5 request of **€4,134,971,466**. Components are €294,408,294 (seventh), €262,804,869 (eighth), €224,939,512 (ninth) and €2,164,144,395 (tenth). The first three total €782,152,675 and are included, not new top-ups on top of the gross. Article 1(2)'s €590,221,197 mitigates the amount suspended; it is not an independent payment.

The [Council working party agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/mpo/2026/11/att-ahwp-on-reconstruction-and-support-for-ukraine-ahwp-resua-(373241)/) lists the file on November 25 at 14:30 Brussels, 13:30 UTC. No Council adoption, Commission disbursement or Ukrainian Treasury receipt verified at review. The proposal is distinct from the [prior €7,859,441,144 November 20 disbursement](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-disburses-eur79-billion-strengthen-ukraines-financial-stability-and-advance-reform-2026-11-20_en) and the separate proposed €45 billion instrument for 2027. The €40.315 billion prior Pillar I disbursement figure in the proposal is cumulative, not a new transfer.

The [assessment annex](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16195-2026-ADD-1/en/pdf) credits adoption of the gas and electricity roadmap (10.9), not implementation of its post-martial-law pricing changes. Four tenth-stage conditions do not meet the satisfaction threshold. Read the detailed assessment rather than the executive bullet for step 4.18: it concerns payment-instrument offenses (Law 5038-IX); step 4.16 concerns transfer and crypto-asset information and remains unsatisfied. Step 10.21 was previously credited for mitigation only and should not be tallied twice as a newly completed reform.

## European Peace Facility: enacted authorization, not delivery

The [Council's November 20 announcement](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/20/european-peace-facility-council-mobilises-additional-1-billion-in-new-military-aid-to-ukraine/) explicitly ties this €1 billion to the previously recorded September 25 €6.6 billion political agreement (J20-2026-024/054). [Decision 2026/2618](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1789220222589&uri=CELEX%3A32026D2618) and [Decision 2026/2620](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1789220222589&uri=CELEX%3A32026D2620), adopted and effective November 20 and published November 23, establish reference amounts of €100 million and €900 million. Article 2 authorizes initial contribution calls of up to €67 million and €600 million, respectively, within approved budgets; those ceilings are part of, not in addition to, the reference amounts and are not receipts. No collection, vendor settlement or delivery established. The Council's €11.8 billion EPF summary incorporates this €1 billion, and its separately stated historical €3.5 billion earnings channel is not a source attribution to these measures.

For the October 26 €4.9 billion event, the [Official Journal results](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/search.html?AU_CODED=CONSIL&lang=&or+DNGroup=CM%3Dp3r-9203-1slc-08b1-abf7&orDNGroup=CM%3D36813_0840c6_9684&qid=1788083210328&sortOne=DD&sortOrder=desc&type=advanced) identify adopted Decisions 2026/2362–2369, published October 27. No October cash or delivery was verified in this update.

# Binance–USD1: advertised balance-retention incentives

Inspected October 20, 2026. Companion **CH7-UAE-USD1-002** supplements CH7-UAE-USD1-001 and CH7-PAY-UAE-002; it does not alter either transaction total.

## Dated primary record

Binance's [announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/9745ace0eb7a42d396e22a711f907616), **USD1-INC-S001**, was published October 1 and records amendments October 15 and 20. The live original displays October 20 11:00 without a timezone.

It advertises **150 million WLFI tokens** and **up to 2.5 million USD1**, not a $150 million payment. WLFI accrual runs October 2–30; qualifying USD1 enrollment October 16–30 precedes conditional December 11 and January 8, 2027 payouts. USD1 incentives reward new/increased balances, subject to a first-50,000-USD1 balance cap, retention requirements and oversubscription adjustments. USD1 rewards aggregate subaccounts under master accounts; WLFI distributions use corresponding subaccounts. United States, Russia and Iran residents are among excluded participants. These are advertised rules, not verified enforcement or transfers. Binance is the announcing operator; contributor funding shares are not identified.

## Increment and beneficiary assessment

This adds a concrete ongoing commercial-distribution mechanism to the existing MGX/USD1/Binance relationship. It is not another MGX investment, a new presidential action, or demonstrated personal income. Do not add promotional ceilings to the $2 billion investment or to realized financial totals.

Potential direct private benefits are exchange usage and USD1 adoption/retained balances; qualifying users may receive rewards. **National-benefit classification: uncertain.** No Russian benefit or governmental exchange is established. A commercial incentive explanation has affirmative documentary support here; it does not answer whether the separate official-treatment sequence involved conflicts or influence. Existing CH7-BIN-SEC-001 and CH7-BIN-CZ-001/002 retain their separate evidence and limitations. One exchange announcement is one source family, not independent proof of its own implementation.

Discriminating evidence remains actual reward-account transfers, aggregate uptake, source-of-funds allocation and independently attributable distributions. Token issuance, reserve value, user rewards and family income are different measures.

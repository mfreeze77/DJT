# October 24 Ukrainian sanctions: enacted legal measures, implementation not measured

**J20-2026-049 / CH7-UA-SANCTIONS-001** and **J20-2026-050 / CH7-UA-SANCTIONS-002**. Reviewed October 25, 2026. These are Ukrainian legal actions, not Trump decisions or new U.S./EU designations.

## Controlling originals and identities

- **UA-SAN-S001:** [Decree 1058/2026](https://www.president.gov.ua/documents/10582026-62173), dated October 24, implements the October 22 NSDC decision. Its original [Annex 1](https://www.president.gov.ua/storage/j-files-storage/01/52/13/c46b29cc7512d0e595e42b27713fd811_1792834145.docx) contains 32 individual entries; [Annex 2](https://www.president.gov.ua/storage/j-files-storage/01/52/17/8b38aea6987491b88099d1063136c3f4_1792834181.docx) contains 35 legal entities. Annex 2 item **4** identifies UC RUSAL TH JSC / United Company RUSAL – Trading House, OGRN **1028700588168**, INN **5519006211**; item **27** identifies RUSAL Taishet Aluminium Smelter LLC, OGRN **1063815015494**, INN **3815011264**.
- **UA-SAN-S002:** [Decree 1059/2026](https://www.president.gov.ua/documents/10592026-62177), also October 24 implementing an October 22 decision. Original [Annex 1](https://www.president.gov.ua/storage/j-files-storage/01/52/05/a07026f1437049f9245f91d855aa18cd_1792834091.docx) contains 51 individual entries; [Annex 2](https://www.president.gov.ua/storage/j-files-storage/01/52/09/70c39daf50c344e5e3738fab1ce7de7e_1792834119.docx) contains 44 legal entities. Do not describe all targets as Russian: Annex 2 includes Chinese/Hong Kong entities.
- **UA-SAN-S003:** [Presidency's October 24 explanatory release](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/prezident-uviv-u-diyu-sankciyi-proti-subyektiv-ta-osib-yaki-107021), published 12:28, characterizes the packages as targeting missile and other defense-production supply chains. That rationale is attributed to the government, not an independently adjudicated finding about each target. Its narrative order differs from decree-number order; the annexes control the mapping above.

Both decrees take effect from publication. The attached decisions direct the Cabinet and Security Service, together with the National Bank and awards commission, to implement and monitor the measures; the Foreign Ministry must inform the EU, United States and other states and seek analogous restrictions. That request does **not** establish their adoption abroad.

All four original DOCX annexes were acquired and their table entries inspected. The measures include asset blocking, full trade restrictions, capital-outflow restrictions and other specified measures. The duration cells distinguish indefinite and ten-year terms; this is not a blanket claim that every measure lasts ten years. The texts establish legal restrictions within Ukraine's sanctions framework, not identified assets actually frozen, completed interdictions, global enforcement or reduced Russian output. No asset/trade-value total is added. [Original-byte acquisition manifest](Tmanch_CH7_Oct25_Policy_Source_Manifest.json).

**Who benefits: United States/allies, prospectively and indirectly**, through Ukraine's legal exclusion of designated supply-chain actors and potential wider coordination; Russia's defense-production access is the intended adverse target. The established near-term result is the legal action and implementation mandate. Actual freezing, disrupted supply, battlefield effects and long-term resilience remain unmeasured.

These measures strengthen the evidence for active Ukrainian/allied resistance alongside the already recorded U.S. relief and support-dependency pattern. They are affirmative evidence of a countervailing policy, not merely a possible benign explanation. Conversely, independent Ukrainian action does not negate repeated Russian benefits from different U.S. decisions or establish that Washington supplied an equivalent remedy. The supported cumulative account remains mixed across actors and mechanisms, not uniformly Russia-favoring policy. No motive, payment, control or quid pro quo is inferred.

Open records: publication/implementation history, registry matches and amendments, actual frozen assets and interrupted transactions, partner adoption, substitution/evasion and production results. Counts above are per-annex legal entries, not a deduplicated global target or financial-flow total.

# Separate PEACE receipts: Sweden-backed IBRD loan and Norway-funded grant

## J20-2026-159: €236 million received, with Sweden as guarantor

Ukraine [reported signing on May 29](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/minfin-ta-svitovyi-bank-pidpysaly-uhodu-pro-kredyt-na-236-mln-ievro-pid-harantiiu-uriadu-shvetsii) and [reported receipt June 10, 2026](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine_received_236_million_in_loan_financing_guaranteed_by_sweden_under_the_world_bank_project-5770). IBRD supplied the loan cash; Sweden provided a guarantee. The [Swedish budget proposal and agreement annex](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/proposition/extra-andringsbudget-for-2026-stod-till-ukraina_hd03143/html/) identify €236 million as the facility's guaranteed principal, excluding interest, and €280.7 million plus interest as an authority ceiling including earlier guarantees. The agreement envisages eligible public service and social expenditure categories. Do not add the ceiling to the loan or attribute a Swedish payment absent a guarantee call and settlement. Exact loan identifier, payer bank value date and onward payments not established here.

## J20-2026-160: $200 million in grant cash from Norway through URTF

On March 18, 2026, [Ukraine's Finance Ministry reported](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/ukraina-zaluchyla-200-mln-hrantovoho-finansuvannia-na-biudzhetnu-pidtrymku-za-rakhunok-vnesku-norvehii-u-mezhakh-proektu-svitovoho-banku) that **$200 million** from Norway had already been credited to the general budget fund via the World Bank Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction and Reform Trust Fund (URTF), under PEACE. The [World Bank URTF annual report](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099713103262663347/pdf/IDU-8f5a661b-317c-4cbb-ab7e-0a9c95156336.pdf) describes a matching-size early-2026 PEACE grant as planned; this corroborates program context but does not identify the grant instrument or another cash receipt. The donor contribution and budget credit are steps in the same funding stream. Do not add this to Norway's cumulative PEACE assistance or a World Bank aggregate without checking inclusion. Exact grant ID, fund-to-budget value dates and end-uses remain unverified.

Both receipts support civilian budget continuity, but the instruments put different risks on the donors. Keep them separate from the [September IBRD loan backed by Canada](Tmanch_CH7_Sep22_PEACE_Canadian_Guarantee_Source_Note.md), Japan-backed programs and Norway's civilian energy fund contributions. These are multilateral transactions, not evidence of a new U.S. financing or direct Russian effect.

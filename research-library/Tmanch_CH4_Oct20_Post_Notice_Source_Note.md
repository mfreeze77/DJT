# Sater: personal notice followed by a directly negotiated relationship

Prepared October 20, 2026. This is a targeted supplement to [CH4-TRUMP-SATER-KNOWLEDGE-006](Tmanch_CH4_claim_ledger.csv), not a claim that Trump knew Sater's complete criminal or cooperation history from the beginning. New companion claim: **CH4-TRUMP-SATER-POSTNOTICE-041**.

## Documented sequence

1. **Notice by the December 19–20, 2007 deposition.** In the O'Brien litigation transcript, pp. 410–413 (physical PDF p. 104), Trump is asked about assault and securities-fraud convictions, identifies Felix Sater, and describes calling Bayrock's owner after the newspaper story. He says Bayrock represented Sater as an employee rather than a principal, describes limited interactions, and says he is considering the relationship and waiting to see Bayrock's response. This is Trump's own recorded response to a specific warning, not an inference from shared office space. It establishes notice by this date, not knowledge of every sealed detail or the date of first knowledge. [PS4-031](https://media-cdn.factba.se/pdf/trump-deposition-obrien-20071219-20071220.pdf)

2. **Later personal arrangement, according to Sater's congressional testimony.** On December 20, 2017, Sater testified that he had a Trump Organization email address as a senior adviser, not an employee. He described the role as continuing real-estate deal work, said he negotiated it directly with Trump, placed it approximately at the end of 2009/beginning of 2010, and said it lasted under a year. His explanation for ending it concerned the economics of taking a substantial deal interest while so close to the organization. These are his answers, not a committee finding that every detail was independently corroborated. [PS4-032, pp. 118–119](https://intelligence.house.gov/uploadedfiles/felix_sater_testimony_dec_20_2017.pdf)

3. **Company-side corroboration and alternative explanation.** AP reported Alan Garten acknowledging a six-month 2010 deal-prospecting arrangement and Trump's offer of office space after the December 2007 disclosure. Garten said there was no formal employment agreement or completed deal, and characterized the renewed opportunity in commercial/rehabilitative terms. AP also reported Garten saying Trump did not then know details of Sater's government cooperation. This supports the relationship's existence from the other side while preserving its explanation and limits. [PS4-033](https://www.ksl.com/article/37620475)

4. **Compensation response.** Garten later expressly denied payment during the 2010 arrangement. [PS4-034](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/former-mafia-linked-figure-describes-association-with-trump/2016/05/17/cec6c2c6-16d3-11e6-aa55-670cabef46e0_story.html) The response is attributed, not independently verified accounting. Do not infer a payment from unanswered questions in an earlier article.

## What this adds to the cumulative record

**The supported inference is a knowing decision to permit renewed access after notice, with personal participation in the later arrangement supported by Sater's testimony.** A blanket explanation that Trump simply did not know of the warning no longer accounts for this later period. Uncertainty about earlier knowledge cannot be carried forward unchanged past documented notice.

The competing commercial explanation has affirmative attributed support: the company described deal prospecting and rehabilitation. That explanation accounts for the business purpose but does not erase the choice to resume a relationship after a serious due-diligence warning. Conversely, access plus notice does not establish participation in Sater's offenses, Russian direction, or a government quid pro quo. Personal decision responsibility and proof of a particular crime are different questions.

This joins the existing Bayrock history and claims CH4-SATER-RICO-004, CH4-SATER-COOPERATION-005 and CH4-TRUMP-SATER-KNOWLEDGE-006. It is one connected notice/continuation sequence, not several independent influence operations. AP and the later response draw on the same company lawyer; they are not two independent witnesses. The testimony and deposition contribute distinct speaker/date evidence.

## Benefits, mechanisms and limits

- **Direct near-term private benefit:** organizational access and a platform for Sater's deal sourcing; potential commercial opportunities for Trump's organization. Realized deal proceeds are not established by these sources.
- **United States/allies, Russia, both/mixed or uncertain:** **uncertain** for national-benefit attribution. This record supports a counterparty-screening and access mechanism, not a demonstrated state beneficiary.
- **Longer-term inference:** willingness to retain or renew access despite warning is relevant to evaluating cumulative susceptibility. Subsequent state influence requires additional evidence rather than automatic attribution.
- **Next discriminating records:** dated approvals, correspondence, credentials/office provisioning, prospect lists, expenses and payment records. Do not require an employment contract as the only proof of a relationship.

# TMTG–TAE financing: corporate funding and unresolved contract-summary differences

Reviewed October 19, 2026. Companions: [typed observations](research/tae_financing_2026/transactions.csv) and [source manifest](research/tae_financing_2026/source_manifest.json). This is source-controlled corporate context, with no new J20 event, score, or addition to historical financial totals.

## Parties and cash direction

Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. (TMTG), a Florida corporation, is the holder/lender; TAE Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation, is the issuer/borrower. The December 18, 2025 merger agreement also includes TMTG's wholly owned T Media Sub, Inc. The contemplated merger would leave TAE as TMTG's subsidiary. [TAE-S09, Item 1.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125046056/ef20061577_8k.htm).

TAE's audited Note 9 reports **$200 million funded December 19, 2025**. [TAE-S04, F-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#notes_annual). TMTG's 2025 cash-flow statement records the corresponding $200 million convertible-note investment outflow. Those are two observations of the same advance. Aggregate corporate cash flows do not identify the particular wire's ultimate capital sources. [TAE-S05, cash-flow statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036126007174/ef20062365_10k.htm).

The September 30 8-K describes **up to $100 million additional draw capacity**, with the window ending November 29, 2026 or earlier merger-agreement termination. Capacity does not establish a draw. [TAE-S03, Item 1.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/djt20260930_8k.htm). The reviewed records do not establish a later second-tranche payment.

## September 30 voting rights and interest

The amended unsecured note grants pre-conversion voting units calculated as:

`floor(0.25 × (outstanding principal + unpaid accrued interest) / Original Issuance Price of the then-most senior equity security)`

Series 12's reference price is $50. Using the reported $200 million principal and $10,969,863.01 interest once gives **1,054,849 illustrative units**. The factor is not 25% ownership or a verified percentage of voting power. Rights apply to combined common/preferred votes and quorum, with separate-class and Requisite Holder votes excluded. [TAE-S02, §§3(c), 3(i)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/ex_1020868.htm).

## Discrepancies to preserve

- **Financing trigger:** The September 30 8-K describes automatic conversion on qualifying financing. [TAE-S03, Item 1.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/djt20260930_8k.htm). Both signed notes expressly exclude financing itself as a conversion/prepayment trigger. The earlier clause instead links later conversion to the financing's securities and price. [TAE-S01, §3(a)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125046056/ef20061577_ex10-1.htm); [TAE-S02, §§3(a), 3(i)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/ex_1020868.htm). TAE's audited note also contains automatic-conversion summary language. [TAE-S04, F-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#notes_annual). No legal resolution or actual conversion is inferred.
- **Termination timing:** The 8-K compresses the termination branches. The signed notes specify conversion immediately before scheduled maturity, with the mandatory/elective branch depending on the termination reason. [TAE-S03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/djt20260930_8k.htm); [TAE-S01, §§3(c)–(e)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125046056/ef20061577_ex10-1.htm).
- **Maturity date:** The S-4 says December 19, 2027 on pp.119 and 133, but December 18, 2027 in audited Note 9's F-16 table. Neither date is silently selected. [TAE-S04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#notes_annual).
- **PIK presentation:** The original note permits interest on capitalized interest. [TAE-S01, §1(b)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125046056/ef20061577_ex10-1.htm). The amended preamble bars capitalization while §1(b) retains principal-increase wording; it expressly bars interest-on-interest. Its $10,969,863.01 unpaid interest balance matches simple 7% accrual for 286 days from December 18. This arithmetic does not resolve presentation or the earlier accrual treatment. [TAE-S02, preamble and §1(b)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/ex_1020868.htm).

## Exposure, beneficiaries and closing status

The S-4 lists the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust's **114,750,000 TMTG shares, approximately 41%**, as of September 15, 2026, with Trump sole beneficiary and Trump Jr. sole trustee. Separately, it reports a **$176,019 salary-reduction bonus paid to TAE CEO Michl Binderbauer in December 2025** following the employment agreement's financing trigger. That definition differs from the note's. [TAE-S04, pp.140, 143, 150–151](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#principal).

Analytical classification: TAE receives corporate liquidity; TMTG receives contractual return/conversion/voting rights and bears investment risk. Shareholder exposure does not establish a personal Trump receipt. The bonus is separately disclosed compensation, without tracing of particular loan proceeds. The reviewed records do not trace the $200 million TMTG advance to a Russian or sovereign capital source. Separately, the UKAEA investment documented below is UK public-body equity in the TAE group; it does not trace the origin of that advance or establish a personal Trump receipt. A governmental bargain or realized net national benefit is not established.

The merger's HSR waiting period expired **February 19, 2026**. [TAE-S04, p.235](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#regulatory). Other contractual conditions include shareholder approvals, registration effectiveness, listing and specified certifications, subject to the agreement's waiver terms. Expiry alone does not establish closing or satisfaction of all conditions. [TAE-S06, §§7.1–7.3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125046056/ef20061577_ex2-1.htm). The October 2 delaying amendment adds Rule 473 language; it is not an effectiveness or completion notice. [TAE-S07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031872/djt20261002_delam.htm). The October 19 inspected submissions index listed October 2 as its latest filing; that bounded search does not prove absence of an unfiled event. [TAE-S08](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001849635.json).

## October 19 extension: UKAEA equity in TAE Beam

UKAEA's audited accounts record a **£5.6 million investment** in TAE Beam (UK) Ltd by March 31, 2026, with **13.5% ownership** and £135 nominal share value; TAE holds the other **86.5%**. UKAEA reports significant influence over certain board decisions and accounts for the stake as an associate. The investment addition and closing carrying value are the same £5.6 million, not two payments. [TAE-S10, Note 14.3, printed p.160 / PDF p.83](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a563f942f6185941a9a649c/uk-atomic-energy-authority-annual-report-and-accounts-2025-2026.pdf#page=83).

TAE's S-4 p.119 describes UKAEA's **$7.5 million cash contribution** and TAE's **$47.9 million contribution**, principally an in-kind IP license. Audited annual Note 2 instead describes the respective cash and intangible amounts as commitments, with each party also granting IP licenses; F-8 separately reports $7.5 million net proceeds from Beam share sales during the year ended March 31. These source verbs remain distinct. [TAE-S04, p.119 and annual F-7–F-8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm#notes_annual).

**Counting control:** £5.6 million and rounded $7.5 million are counterpart reports of one equity investment. Neither TAE's intangible contribution nor nominal share capital is another comparable cash receipt. The annual F-5 aggregate $7.475 million noncontrolling-interest proceeds line is consistent with the rounded disclosure, not an extra receipt. No exact exchange rate, bank-transfer date, recipient account, onward transfer or unrestricted parent-company cash receipt is established. These accounting disclosures do not trace the earlier TMTG advance.

## October 19 follow-up: corporate sources and uses behind the advance

The annual and September-quarter reports narrow the context to accumulated corporate liquidity and fourth-quarter asset turnover. They do **not** identify the December 19 originating account, wire remitter, investment-settlement dates or any investor's earmarked dollars. In particular, **$250 million of Q4 short-term-investment sales is not a traced source of the $200 million TAE advance**. TAE-T01/T02 remain the same advance, without a new receipt entry. [TAE-S05, annual F-7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036126007174/ef20062365_10k.htm); [TAE-S17, nine-month cash flow p.5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125040977/ef20054981_10q.htm).

### Q4 bridge, derived rather than separately reported

The following USD-million values are annual cash-flow amounts less nine-month amounts. Cash includes equivalents **and restricted cash**; a consolidated balance does not establish unrestricted cash in TMTG's paying account.

| Cash bridge | USD million |
|---|---:|
| September 30 opening balance | 501.9115 |
| Q4 operating net inflow: 14.7581 − 2.6388 | +12.1193 |
| Q4 investing net flow: −2,267.2830 − (−1,973.1082) | −294.1748 |
| Q4 financing net flow: 2,248.1769 − 2,302.1448 | −53.9679 |
| December 31 closing balance | 165.8881 |

The bridge closes exactly at published precision. Within Q4 investing, short-term-investment sales were 325 − 75 = **250**, short-term-investment purchases 23.5060 − 18.8744 = **4.6316**, and equity-security purchases 932.2958 − 592.7564 = **339.5394**, alongside the existing **200** TAE outflow. These components overlap the net investing line and must not be added to it. The annual accounting policy describes short-term investments primarily as Treasury-collateralized repurchase arrangements in which TMTG lends to a seller. The accounting description does not establish a new TMTG borrowing or identify the TAE wire bank. [TAE-S05, F-7 and Note 2]; [TAE-S17, p.5].

PIPE and note-issuance proceeds are unchanged between the two cash-flow statements, so there is no disclosed Q4 increment in those lines. The annual report states there were no 2025 SEPA share sales. Q4 financing is a net outflow, despite small warrant and noncontrolling-interest receipts. A **$100 unexplained reporting difference** in cash offering costs is retained mechanically in the subtraction, not classified as new proceeds: Q3's cash-flow table says $34,399.3 thousand, while its Note 14 and the annual report say $34,399.2 thousand. No rounding or reclassification explanation is assumed. [TAE-S05, F-7, Note 13 and pp.99–100](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036126007174/ef20062365_10k.htm); [TAE-S17, p.5 and Note 14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125040977/ef20054981_10q.htm).

### May financing package: gross, face and cash are different measures

The closing report identifies 55,857,181 shares at $25.72, or calculated equity consideration of **$1,436,646,695.32**. It also identifies **$1 billion note principal**; the note subscription form prices the notes at 96% of principal. [TAE-S18, Item 1.01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125020967/ef20049966_8k.htm); [TAE-S20, preamble](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125020420/ef20049689_ex10-2.htm).

| FY2025 cash-flow reconstruction of the May package | USD million |
|---|---:|
| PIPE cash-flow proceeds, after fee offset | 1,395.3183 |
| Note cash proceeds, after original-issue discount | 960.0000 |
| Cash debt/equity offering costs | (34.3992) |
| Derived net new cash | **2,320.9191** |

The disclosed **75.7276** total offering costs consist of **41.3284** netted against subscriptions and **34.3992** paid in cash. Subtracting the whole fee amount again from the PIPE cash-flow line would double count the offset. Share-price gross minus the offset differs from reported PIPE cash by **−$4.68**, retained as a published-precision reconciliation residual. The annual report labels its 1,395.3183 figure gross even though the offset explains its difference from share-price consideration. Note face, discount, gross consideration, cash components and net cash overlap; they are not separate fundraising receipts. This is an annual-report reconstruction of the May package, not proof of a closing-day bank balance or December availability. [TAE-S05, F-7 and Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036126007174/ef20062365_10k.htm).

The funds should not all be described as unrestricted. The filed equity form limits working-capital/general-corporate use to **3% of equity gross proceeds**; the note form specifies Bitcoin strategy and collateral uses, less expenses. These filed forms do not include every investor's executed signature page. Written amendment/waiver provisions do not establish that a waiver occurred. The executed indenture names U.S. Bank Trust Company, N.A. as trustee/collateral agent and addresses collateral coverage and release; that role does not identify the economic source or TAE wire bank. No reviewed record allocates these proceeds to TAE or establishes compliance or breach. [TAE-S19, §§3(w), 8(o)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125020420/ef20049689_ex10-1.htm); [TAE-S20, §§1, 3(z), 6(b), 8(i)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125020420/ef20049689_ex10-2.htm); [TAE-S21, §§3.8, 3.10, 13.1–13.2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125020967/ef20049966_ex4-1.htm).

### Foris cash direction and remaining documentary gap

The signed Foris Holdings US Inc. agreement and annual Note 13 describe **TMTG transferring $50 million plus 2,797,985 shares in exchange for 684,427,004 CRO**. The agreement's $105 million CRO consideration is not $105 million incoming Crypto.com cash. The agreement is dated August 26, 2025, as is Note 13; the annual business summary instead says August 25. Preserve that date difference without treating either as an independently verified bank-settlement date. [TAE-S22, definitions, §§2.1–2.2 and signed schedule](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036125032615/ef20054552_ex10-1.htm); [TAE-S05, p.8 and Note 13/F-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000114036126007174/ef20062365_10k.htm).

Aggregate cash, crypto, collateral and receivables cannot substitute for those records. The later UKAEA investment remains separate. No Russian or sovereign source of the TMTG advance, governmental exchange or personal Trump receipt is established; historical financial totals are unchanged.

## Sources and completed process

**NOV4-DIP-S001:** The Ukrainian presidency's [November 4 original readout](https://www.president.gov.ua/news/volodimir-zelenskij-obgovoriv-zi-stivenom-vitkoffom-i-dzhare-107153), displayed **22:06** without an acquired timezone, and its [official English version](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/volodimir-zelenskij-obgovoriv-zi-stivenom-vitkoffom-i-dzhare-107153) report a **90-minute call from Madrid** between Zelenskyy and US presidential representatives Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. Zelenskyy says Ukraine supplied its proposals to the US side, discussed air defence and needed Patriot interceptors, and will continue bilateral work including Drone Deals. The article identifies preparation for a US–Ukraine–Russia team-level format in Abu Dhabi in less than a week. It does not specify exact meeting dates in the inspected text. Ukrainian and English versions are one official source family, not independent confirmation; a US-side readout and call memorandum were not acquired.

The same source also reports that **reconstruction consultations had taken place in Brussels on November 4**, with Ukrainian, US, EU, French, British, German, NATO, World Bank and global-business participants. It identifies **November 5 Brussels security-guarantee discussions as planned**, not already held or agreed. The future Brussels meeting is distinct from the prospective Abu Dhabi format.

Its **EUR 231.5 billion** is the Commission's cumulative EU-support account since the start of Russia's war, not money newly committed at this meeting. The [Commission original locator](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/STATEMENT_26_2348) returned only an application shell in raw retrieval; that shell is not an acquired statement body or independent corroboration of the reproduction.

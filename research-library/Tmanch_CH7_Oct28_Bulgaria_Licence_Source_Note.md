Their qualifying subsidiaries are nonindividual persons owned/controlled directly or indirectly by the Bulgarian entities under regulation 7. Preserve the instrument's literal drafting irregularity rather than silently expand the perimeter.

Paragraph 4 allows qualifying Persons to continue covered business, including payments under existing/new obligations and provision/receipt of economic resources. Relevant UK Institutions may process covered payments. “Person” excludes UK designated persons as defined; the instrument excludes other designated-person ownership/control from that definition except the named Bulgarian entities. Notification does not verify legality. Other UK sanctions regimes remain outside the permission. Treasury may vary/revoke/suspend.

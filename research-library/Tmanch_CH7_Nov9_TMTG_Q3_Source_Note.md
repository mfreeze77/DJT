## Gains, losses and cash are different measurements

MD&A p.25 decomposes the **Q3 $378.1343 million digital/pledged-digital gain** into **$200.7984 million pledged-asset unrealized gain**, **$107.4502 million digital-asset unrealized gain** and **$69.8857 million derecognition gain**. These components sum exactly. Derecognition does not identify a cash sale: Note 2 describes collateral and Bitcoin-denominated arrangements, while p.6 classifies disposition/reacquisition of pledged assets among noncash investing/financing activities.

Note 14, printed p.17, reports Q3 investment income of **$60.0097 million**: realized equity-sale gain **+$19.6775 million**, unrealized equity gain **+$98.2999 million**, realized derivative loss **−$46.0449 million**, unrealized derivative loss **−$7.9630 million**, dividends **+$0.0046 million** and investment expenses **−$3.9644 million**. These sum exactly.

The release's rounded **$496.1 million combined digital/equity gains** combines $378.1343 million, $19.6775 million and $98.2999 million. Do not add it again to those components or label the whole amount cash or unrealized. Its rounded **$54.0 million derivative losses** combine realized and unrealized components. A realized accounting label does not by itself prove a cash settlement: p.6 separately contains a nine-month noncash derivative-settlement adjustment of $46.0449 million. No quarter-only asset-sale cash-proceeds reconciliation was acquired.

The same cash-flow statement reports **nine-month equity-sale proceeds of $266.1713 million**, while Note 14 reports a **nine-month realized equity-sale loss of $100.5807 million**. Proceeds, gains/losses, periods and shareholder distributions must not be interchanged or summed as new receipts.

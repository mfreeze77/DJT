**OFAC-NOV5-S002.** The [original signed determination](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937326/download?inline=), effective November 5, applies section 2(a)(i)(A) of EO 14404 to the electronics, manufacturing, marine and technology sectors of Cuba's economy. It creates an applicable designation basis for foreign persons determined to operate or have operated in those sectors. It is not a statement that every sector participant has automatically been individually designated. The single-page instrument names Bradley T. Smith, OFAC Director; the signature/effective-date page was visually inspected.

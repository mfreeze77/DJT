# October 24 monitored satellite-speed restoration

**SAT-RESTORE-S001:** Ukraine's Ministry of Defence [reported on October 24 at 12:18 PM EEST](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/starlink-and-starshield-speed-restored-in-ukraine) that its monitoring recorded restored Starlink/Starshield operation, with stable connectivity during movement above 400 km/h. It acknowledged earlier restrictions and said monitoring continues. The [Ukrainian version](https://mod.gov.ua/news/sistemi-monitoringu-ministerstva-oboroni-ukrayini-oficzijno-fiksuyut-vidnovlennya-shvidkosti-roboti-starlink-starshield) is the same institutional evidence family, not independent corroboration. Original HTML from both versions was acquired and read.

This is affirmative official monitoring evidence of operational improvement, stronger than the October 22 assurance. **J20-2026-045 / CH7-SAT-RESTORE-001** records a later status change, not a correction erasing the contemporaneous October 23 continuing-limit account in **J20-2026-039 / CH7-SAT-STATUS-001**. Publication time is 09:18 UTC, not a verified moment of configuration change. Raw telemetry, affected-terminal population, geographic coverage, service plans and permanence remain unverified. The ministry credits high-level Ukrainian dialogue; this does not identify the original restriction's author or decision chain.

**Who benefits:** United States/allies, directly and near-term through restored Ukrainian high-speed communications supporting drones, logistics and defense. Those functions matter to resistance against Russia. The earlier restrictions' operational advantage to Russia is not erased by a subsequent reversal. The cumulative record now shows reported impairment, repeated warnings and oversight demands, an assurance followed by continuing-limit observations, and later monitoring-reported restoration. Restored operation is affirmative evidence of remediation; it is not affirmative evidence that the prior restriction was harmless or justified. Persistent dependence and the authority to restrict service remain unresolved. No new intentional falsehood, coordinated Russian action or casualty-specific causal claim follows from this update.

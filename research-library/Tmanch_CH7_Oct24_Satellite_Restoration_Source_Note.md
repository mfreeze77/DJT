# October 24 monitored satellite-speed restoration

**SAT-RESTORE-S001:** Ukraine's Ministry of Defence [reported on October 24 at 12:18 PM EEST](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/starlink-and-starshield-speed-restored-in-ukraine) that its monitoring recorded restored Starlink/Starshield operation, with stable connectivity during movement above 400 km/h. It acknowledged earlier restrictions and said monitoring continues. The [Ukrainian version](https://mod.gov.ua/news/sistemi-monitoringu-ministerstva-oboroni-ukrayini-oficzijno-fiksuyut-vidnovlennya-shvidkosti-roboti-starlink-starshield) is the same institutional evidence family, not independent corroboration. Original HTML from both versions was acquired and read.

This is affirmative official monitoring evidence of operational improvement, stronger than the October 22 assurance. **J20-2026-045 / CH7-SAT-RESTORE-001** records a later status change, not a correction erasing the contemporaneous October 23 continuing-limit account in **J20-2026-039 / CH7-SAT-STATUS-001**. Publication time is 09:18 UTC, not a verified moment of configuration change. Raw telemetry, affected-terminal population, geographic coverage, service plans and permanence remain unverified. The ministry credits high-level Ukrainian dialogue; this does not identify the original restriction's author or decision chain.

**Who benefits:** United States/allies, directly and near-term through restored Ukrainian high-speed communications supporting drones, logistics and defense. Those functions matter to resistance against Russia. The earlier restrictions' operational advantage to Russia is not erased by a subsequent reversal. The cumulative record now shows reported impairment, repeated warnings and oversight demands, an assurance followed by continuing-limit observations, and later monitoring-reported restoration. Restored operation is affirmative evidence of remediation; it is not affirmative evidence that the prior restriction was harmless or justified. Persistent dependence and the authority to restrict service remain unresolved. No new intentional falsehood, coordinated Russian action or casualty-specific causal claim follows from this update.

## October 24 presidential testing and delivery-status follow-up

He credited American and other partners, including members of Congress, senators, media, the State Department and the President. ## November 24 follow-up: interceptor approval reporting is separate

The [Ukrainian presidency's published November 18 Ramstein address](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/zvernennya-prezidenta-do-uchasnikiv-kontaktnoyi-grupi-z-pita-107373) urged the U.S. to avoid delivery delays and proposed an allied stock exchange, but did not identify a shipment count. On November 24, the [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/02db16f6-808f-44b4-98dc-fe47f6a04d33) attributed to people familiar with the matter an estimate of about 60 previously allocated interceptors still awaiting U.S. transfer approval. The report's two variant estimates are included in the total. The Pentagon's statement of unchanged general policy did not address the specific shipments, and [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-defense-chief-has-yet-approve-patriot-missiles-already-allocated-ukraine-ft-2026-11-24/) did not independently verify the claim. No shipment-specific approval, denial or receipt was recovered. The reporting does not establish a new satellite restriction, coordinated intent, or any change to the scores.

Do not join this to the $2.10 billion proposed sale (J20-2026-074), the German contract and acceleration discussion (077), Cabinet authorization to sign a NATO PURL framework (120), or the separately tracked $400 million appropriation. The [NATO advisory](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/media-advisories/2026/11/24/nato-secretary-general-to-visit-the-united-states-of-america) schedules a November 25 Trump–Rutte meeting but gives no agenda or result. Recheck authoritative statements for specific authorization or recipient confirmation; a meeting alone does not advance the shipment stage.

The [November 23 Ukrainian presidential address](https://www.president.gov.ua/en/news/raketi-dlya-petriotiv-potribni-tut-v-ukrayini-mi-duzhe-ochik-107457) independently describes Ukraine still awaiting the necessary U.S. decisions on Patriot missiles. It supplies no quantity or shipment identification. This corroborates the continuing approval dependency, not the approximately 60 figure, a decision to withhold, or a delivery outcome.

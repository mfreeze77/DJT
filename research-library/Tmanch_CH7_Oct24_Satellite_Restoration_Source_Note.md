# October 24 monitored satellite-speed restoration

**SAT-RESTORE-S001:** Ukraine's Ministry of Defence [reported on October 24 at 12:18 PM EEST](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/starlink-and-starshield-speed-restored-in-ukraine) that its monitoring recorded restored Starlink/Starshield operation, with stable connectivity during movement above 400 km/h. It acknowledged earlier restrictions and said monitoring continues. The [Ukrainian version](https://mod.gov.ua/news/sistemi-monitoringu-ministerstva-oboroni-ukrayini-oficzijno-fiksuyut-vidnovlennya-shvidkosti-roboti-starlink-starshield) is the same institutional evidence family, not independent corroboration. Publication time is 09:18 UTC, not a verified moment of configuration change. Raw telemetry, affected-terminal population, geographic coverage, service plans and permanence remain unverified. The ministry credits high-level Ukrainian dialogue; this does not identify the original restriction's author or decision chain.

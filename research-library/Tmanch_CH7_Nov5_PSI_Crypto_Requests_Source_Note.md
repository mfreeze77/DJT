# November 5 congressional cryptocurrency records-request renewals

**J20-2026-096 / CH7-PSI-CRYPTO-NOV5-001.** Unscored congressional oversight action. November 9 acquisition is not the issuance date. This record establishes signed requests and deadlines, without adopting the letters' allegations or adding a financial flow.

## Acquired action and recipients

**PSI-CRYPTO-NOV5-S001–005:** Senator Richard Blumenthal's [official release](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/newsroom/press/release/blumenthal-sends-warning-to-targets-of-investigations-into-crypto-corruption-immigration-abuses-big-tech-and-more) links four signed **November 5, 2026** cryptocurrency records-request renewals:

- [World Liberty Financial, Inc.](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-11-05_letter_from_sen_richard_blumenthal_to_world_liberty_financial.pdf), addressed to co-founder Zach Witkoff; renews the May 6, 2025 request.
- [Fight Fight Fight LLC](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-11-05_letter_from_sen_richard_blumenthal_to_fight_fight_fight.pdf), addressed to Bill Zanker; renews the May 6, 2025 request.
- [Binance Holdings Ltd.](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-11-05_letter_from_sen_richard_blumenthal_to_binance.pdf), addressed to Richard Teng; renews the February 24 and April 1, 2026 requests.
- [Tether and Tether US](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-11-05_letter_from_sen_richard_blumenthal_to_tether.pdf), addressed to Paolo Ardoino and Bo Hines; renews the June 6 and October 22, 2026 requests.

Each asks for **receipt acknowledgment by November 9**, production to **begin by November 30**, and voluntary production of the requested records/information **by January 4, 2027**, after which alternative compliance measures may be considered. The January deadline does not erase the November production-start request or the provision for additional records on a schedule agreed in advance with staff.

The cover letters identify Blumenthal as **Ranking Member of the Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations** and copy Chairman Ron Johnson. The release's description of an incoming chair does not change his capacity in the signed instruments. These letters establish neither actual receipt/acknowledgment nor a subpoena, court order, completed production, enforcement finding or recovered funds. No recipient response or later compulsory process was independently acquired in this review.

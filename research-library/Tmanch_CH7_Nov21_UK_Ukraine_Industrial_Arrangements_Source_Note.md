# November 21: UK–Ukraine industrial arrangements and existing winter-budget allocation

**J20-2026-126 / CH7-UK-BUDGET-001 follow-up.** The [Ukrainian Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/deep-strike-middle-strike-drone-production-uk) and the [UK Ministry of Defence](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/defence-secretary-meets-president-zelensky-and-minister-khmara-in-kyiv-as-uk-deepens-industrial-support-for-ukraine) report that Evgeniy Khmara and Wes Streeting signed project arrangements on November 21 for Ukrainian-designed drones to be produced in the UK with UK funding for Ukraine. The two governments corroborate the signing, not separate programs. The terms, facility activation, contracted quantities and delivery of co-produced units were not verified.

The UK release also explains how the previously announced **£500 million** October 28 winter envelope (**J20-2026-060**, [Budget source note](Tmanch_CH7_Oct28_UK_Ukraine_Budget_Source_Note.md)) was allocated: **£240 million** for an air-defence package and **£260 million** for drones and other capabilities, the latter announced by [Downing Street at the Berlin meeting](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/1000-long-range-strike-drones-announced-for-ukraine-as-prime-minister-co-chairs-coalition-of-the-willing-in-berlin). More than 80% of the £260 million package is expected to go to UK firms. These are components of the same £500 million, not three new flows. No separate signed project price, overlap between those procurement quantities and the industrial arrangements, supplier receipts or Ukrainian equipment receipt established.

**Who benefits:** Ukraine and allies prospectively gain expanded production capacity and less reliance on one country's facilities. UK firms are expected beneficiaries of the procurement allocation, not verified cash recipients. This is concrete allied mitigation through signed arrangements and spending allocations, not a U.S. decision or evidence that U.S. Patriot transfer approvals have cleared. Keep the funds distinct from **120/125** and the industrial program distinct from **075** training or a multinational-force deployment. No scored event, measured production, or delivered defensive effect is asserted.

# November 16 EU defence loan receipt and cumulative comparison

**J20-2026-153.** On November 16, the [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_2452/IP_26_2452_EN.pdf) reported disbursing **€1.71 billion**, the eighth defence payment under the Ukraine Support Loan. [Ukraine's Cabinet](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/news/serhii-koretskyi-obhovoryv-z-ursuloiu-fon-der-liaien-finansovu-stiikist-ukrainy-pidtrymku-ekonomiky-ta-ievrointehratsiiu) said **€1.7 billion** had arrived in the Treasury account that day; this is one payment reported at different precision. It is distinct from the November 5 seventh defence payment (part of [J20-2026-085](Tmanch_CH7_Nov5_EU_Disbursement_Source_Note.md)) and the ninth Ukraine Facility budget payment, [J20-2026-125](Tmanch_CH7_Nov20_EU_Ukraine_Facility_Source_Note.md).

**Cumulative follow-up.** The November 16 Commission cumulative defence figure was **€18.1 billion**; the Ukrainian prime minister cited **€14.6 billion**. The [November 26 Finance Ministry investor deck, p. 2 note 4](https://mof.gov.ua/storage/files/Financing_en_v26112026-(1).pdf#page=2) reports **€18.1 billion** channelled to Ukraine's special budget fund, matching the earlier payer figure at published precision. Preserve both earlier reports as dated observations; they do not establish a separate €3.5 billion transfer, bank value dates, or the reason for the earlier mismatch. Do not add any of these stocks to the individual tranche or to the deck's broader already-inclusive external financing headline.

Ukraine benefits through available financing for urgent defence procurement. The Commission described intended procurement from EU-based manufacturers for about 40% of this tranche, not contracts awarded, supplier receipts, deliveries, or measured effects. No U.S. transfer verified.

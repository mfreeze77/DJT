## Read the reporting convention first

**The report lists these amounts; this is not a reconstructed bank-cash ledger.** OGE allows net distributive share for partnership, LLC and S-corporation income, even without a distribution. Accordingly, an amount listed under a company is not automatically its gross receipts, a deposit into Donald Trump's personal account, or an amount to multiply again by the ownership percentage. Preserve the disclosed income category. [OGE-S003](https://www.oge.gov/web/278eGuide.nsf/Definitions)

Annual Part 2 normally covers the preceding calendar year; a filing extension does not extend that period. This note assigns the 2026-filed report to calendar 2025 and the 2025-filed report to calendar 2024. The extracted cover fields both display 2025, so that field alone is not used as the period rule. Filing context, OGE's reporting-period rule, and dated schedule content control the comparison. [OGE-S004](https://www.oge.gov/web/278eGuide.nsf/For_Ethics_Officials#_2.01:Reporting_Periods) [OGE-S001](https://extapps2.oge.gov/201/Presiden.nsf/PAS%2BIndex/69AEAA9D7455ACD585258E27002DDEE1/$FILE/Donald-J-Trump-2026-278ANNUAL.pdf) [OGE-S002](https://extapps2.oge.gov/201/Presiden.nsf/PAS%2BIndex/4EC9A8E6DD078F2985258CA9002C9377/$FILE/Trump%2C%20Donald%20J.%202025%20Annual%20278.pdf)

- Doha's $5.25 million intercompany receivable from Trump Acquisition LLC is an asset description alongside the fee, not another $5.25 million of income. Prior Dubai DG and KSA rows each describe a $4 million receivable, also excluded from income sums.
- DT Marks Dubai LLC without DG and DT Dubai Golf Manager LLC name DAMAC; they are outside the four-row Dar-named fee set. Other developers and management categories are not silently combined into an all-Gulf total.
- The Diriyah and Dubai DG hotel-manager rows use a below-$201 category. That category is not an exact zero and supplies no exact licensing addition.
- Asset-value ranges, project development values, company loan capacities and loan draws are not extra licensing income. No borrowing-to-fee trace is supplied here.

# Comparison method

## What this measures
One source communication × target, not every sentence and not every news article quoting a sentence. Each target receives separate positive and negative flags. Mixed communications count in both any-positive and any-negative totals; positive-only, negative-only, mixed and neutral partitions are mutually exclusive. All coding is AI semantic review, not a human-validated gold standard. Source excerpts and decisions are provided for inspection and correction.

The requested window begins at the January 20, 2025 inauguration. This snapshot was acquired October 12, 2026 UTC. DCPD records stop at September 11, 2026; social archive capture reaches October 11 locally. Present channel-specific results and an optional shared September 11 cutoff. Do not interpret missing recent transcripts or missing media transcription as silence.

## Attribution
Only Trump's own spoken words or text attributed to his account count in the main comparison. An account post is primary evidence that the account published it, but may contain another person's words. Reporter questions, foreign leaders' answers, reposted headlines, quotation-only posts, archive descriptions and other people's praise are not automatically his assertions. Screenshots and video speech require additional transcription and attribution before counting.

DCPD source typography distinguishes speaker labels from references to officials in a monologue. Text-only speaker heuristics initially produced false negatives; the source-based correction preserves paragraph indices and provides an audit log. Records containing joint statements or uncertain authorship remain identifiable and are not silently pooled into the direct-voice base.

## Positive categories
- Explicit competence, character or conduct praise
- Current personal affinity or stated good relationship
- Ceremonial welcome/thanks and memorial tribute, separately flagged
- Favorable intention attributed to the target, separately flagged
- Communication-quality or deal-outcome appraisal, separately flagged; a 'good call' is not by itself praise of the person

Use the same definition for every target. A strict sensitivity excludes ceremonial-only, affinity-only, favorable-intention-only and communication/deal-only cases. This is a narrower question than all favorable rhetoric and must not replace the broad results without explanation.

## Negative categories
Mild disagreement, substantive condemnation, insults and coercive threats are separately tagged. A neutral tariff announcement is not automatically criticism. An explicit punitive threat, refusal to defend, accusation or derogatory evaluation counts. Repeating a historical compliment in order to withdraw it does not establish current praise. An announced policy, sanction, tariff or weapons transfer is rhetoric evidence; implementation and consequences require separate records.

## Targets and denominator
Putin personally and Russia as a state are different targets. The personal Putin analysis does not include every criticism of Russia. NATO institution, NATO secretary general, member states, their leaders, EU and unspecified Europe remain separate. Ukraine is not classified as a NATO member. Canada is both a named comparator and a NATO member; prevent double-counting in any alliance-wide aggregate. Greenland is a territory associated with Denmark, with a separate sensitivity category. Known former leaders, opposition politicians, spouses and monarchs retain separate role labels where reviewed. Unresolved individual roles remain generic individuals; no all-people aggregate is labeled incumbent executive leaders.

Report raw positive/negative counts and proportions of target-mention communications. A many-country category has many more possible targets than Putin; its total is not an exposure-matched single-person comparison. Show individual leaders as well as country and institutional aggregates. A long press conference can discuss many targets. Per-target rows cannot simply be summed to get the number of communications criticizing 'allies'. Union them by communication ID first.

## Deduplication
Preserve all platform IDs in the archive. For analysis, distinguish archive mirrors, reposts, cross-platform copies, same-day corrections, repeated messages on different dates, and a social post later reproduced in DCPD. Candidate similarities are not confirmed identical events. Separate-channel tables avoid pretending unresolved DCPD/social overlaps are independent observations. Do not produce a pooled headline without verifying the overlap map.

## Interpretation
These results test rhetorical asymmetry. They do not, by themselves, prove motive, financial dependence, coordination or criminal conduct. Historical connections, actions, realized consequences and plausible competing explanations belong in a separately linked cumulative assessment. Neither occasional criticism of Putin nor praise of an ally automatically cancels a larger pattern; neither does a striking quotation establish frequency.

# Harmonized categories

A flag needs target-resolved, Trump-attributed evidence in the communication. Quotes by reporters/guests are not Trump statements. Preserve the full question and response when available. Personal leaders, states and institutions are distinct.

1. Character/competence praise: an affirmative appraisal of the target’s qualities or ability. Institutional adaptation describes the institution’s quality. Appearance-only or purely conditional praise is excluded from the main count.
2. Favorable conduct appraisal: approving or positively evaluating target conduct; mere description or acceptance of a proposal is insufficient without favorable appraisal.
3. Current affinity: an asserted current positive personal relationship. Past-only affinity is separate.
4. Explicit trust: a stated belief in the target’s honesty/reliability.
5. Defense/excuse: justification, minimization, rebuttal of criticism or protective defense of the target. These subtypes are consequentially different and not weighted as equivalent misconduct. Peace intention alone is insufficient.
6. Favorable peace intention: attributing a desire or intention for peace.
7. Communication quality: favorable description of a call/meeting, separated from personal praise and the broad-favorable union.
8. Clear target/conduct negative: explicit adverse evaluation of the resolved target or conduct, including mild disappointment and shared blame. It does not mean a harsh attack.
9. Contextual negative: an adverse evaluation dependent on ambiguity, softened attribution, or context; kept separate from clear negatives.

Praise union is categories 1–2. Broad favorable union is categories 1–6. Neither union includes category 7. Mixed communications can contain both positive and negative flags. Source-specific sensitivity decisions remain in the record files. Prompting and post-criticism softening are separate dimensions, not polarity categories or complete opportunity denominators.

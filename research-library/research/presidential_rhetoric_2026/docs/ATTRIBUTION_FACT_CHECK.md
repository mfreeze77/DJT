# Rhetoric is evidence of a statement, not its factual accuracy

Checked October 12, 2026.

The Canadian-leader annotations retain Trump's criticism assigning responsibility to Justin Trudeau for Russia's exclusion from the G8. That criticism is an observed utterance, even where its historical premise is wrong. The archive must not convert it into an established action by Trudeau.

The March 24, 2014 Hague Declaration states that the participating leaders suspended their participation in the G8 and would meet in G7 format. Canada's contemporary account identifies Prime Minister Stephen Harper as Canada's participant in that declaration. These primary records support the attribution correction in the Canadian-leader audit.

- [The Hague Declaration, March 24, 2014](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2014/03/24/hague-declaration/)
- [Government of Canada's response to the situation in Ukraine, historical chronology](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2014/06/government-canada-response-situation-ukraine.html)

This fact check concerns the historical premise, not whether Trump actually voiced the criticism. The source-linked transcript annotations establish the latter separately.

## January 4: described equipment/intangibles acquisition

The already indexed DTA 807303 decisions describe **Barrier Gas** buying **intangible assets and equipment from Century Resources Corporation for $155,000 cash on January 4, 1988**. The preceding corporate history identifies Barrier Gasoline Service, Inc. The decisions describe consideration; no underlying agreement, bill of sale, bank instrument, closing statement or allocated asset schedule was acquired. They do not say this acquisition conveyed Bridgeport land, a terminal, corporate shares or all Century assets. [CH2-P62, pp.2–3](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/807303.dec.pdf#page=2); [CH2-P63, p.2](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=2).

## February 1: recorded conveyance by a separately named corporation

Bridgeport land records **document 1695, Volume 2446, pp.116–118** identify **Century Resources Bridgeport Terminal, Inc.** as grantor and **Bridgeport Marine Terminals, Inc.** as grantee of land, buildings and improvements on Eagle's Nest Road. The deed was executed and acknowledged **February 1, 1988**, then received for record **February 2, 1988 at 11:58 A.M.** The execution date and recording stamp are different events, not competing sale dates. CH2-P65 identifies the official portal and retrieval locator below.

The official CT DEP Form III bundle independently supplies a signed filing describing **change in ownership by conveyance of real property**, with transfer date **February 1, 1988**, at **400 Eagle's Nest Road** and the same corporate parties. The transferor certification and transferee receipt are dated February 1. Its disclaimer says DEP does not certify the submitted information. The later agency acknowledgment establishes filing completeness, not independent verification of every submitted fact. [CH2-P64, physical PDF pp.9–11](https://filings.deep.ct.gov/DEEPDocumentSearchPortal/Home/DisplayPDF?DocID={B036CB84-0100-C42C-80D5-DB0453662158}#page=9).

The deed's good-and-valuable-consideration recital supplies **no actual land price**. No price is inferred from conveyance-tax markings, the later filing fee, or Barrier's $155,000 acquisition. Similar names do not establish that Century Resources Corporation and Century Resources Bridgeport Terminal, Inc. are the same corporation. No consideration allocation, bank trace or onward destination is established. Keep the accounts separately typed, not aggregated into proved nonoverlapping cash flows.

## Three distinct date functions, with the discrepancy retained

- **February 1, 1988:** signed Form III transfer date and deed execution/acknowledgment.
- **February 2, 1988:** deed recording stamp.
- **February 11, 1988:** agency receipt of Form III, explicitly identified by the April 28, 1992 fee notice. The November 7, 1988 complete-form acknowledgment refers to the February filing and August 17 resubmission. These administrative dates are not additional conveyances. [P64, PDF pp.6–7](https://filings.deep.ct.gov/DEEPDocumentSearchPortal/Home/DisplayPDF?DocID={B036CB84-0100-C42C-80D5-DB0453662158}#page=6).

The **May 5, 1992 fee form instead enters December 17, 1987 as the property-transfer date** and uses One Eagles Nest Road rather than the signed form's 400 Eagle's Nest Road. Those variants remain unresolved; they are not silently corrected or counted as a second transfer. The contemporaneous deed and signed form are stronger conveyance evidence than this later administrative entry. [P64, PDF p.3](https://filings.deep.ct.gov/DEEPDocumentSearchPortal/Home/DisplayPDF?DocID={B036CB84-0100-C42C-80D5-DB0453662158}#page=3).

The current **REACT case DCN-017205** displays **Date Of Transfer 2/11/1988**, observed November 14, 2026. That is lower-tier migrated metadata, not a signed closing record. The original agency letter independently identifies February 11 as receipt; no migration mapping was acquired, so the displayed label must not overwrite the signed deed/Form III dates. [CH2-P67, official case page](https://deep-react.service.ct.gov/s/case/500cs00001Q1xTN/detail); [P64, PDF p.6](https://filings.deep.ct.gov/DEEPDocumentSearchPortal/Home/DisplayPDF?DocID={B036CB84-0100-C42C-80D5-DB0453662158}#page=6).

## Filing fee is not consideration

The May 5, 1992 form lists Bridgeport Marine Terminals, Inc. as payor and **$900 initial payment**, with agency payment-receipt date **May 7, 1992**. The April 28 notice explains the fee relates to pre-July 1990 Form III filings, not a new 1992 purchase. This is an administrative fee record, not seller proceeds or bank-clearance proof. Do not add it to the $155,000 acquisition or treat it as land price. [P64, PDF pp.3,6](https://filings.deep.ct.gov/DEEPDocumentSearchPortal/Home/DisplayPDF?DocID={B036CB84-0100-C42C-80D5-DB0453662158}#page=3).

## Separately documented mortgage release

Bridgeport **document 1693, Volume 2446, pp.113–114**, executed **January 20, 1988** and recorded **February 2 at 11:56 A.M.**, records **Union Trust Company, trustee**, releasing an open-end mortgage from **Century Resources Bridgeport Terminal, Inc. to Connecticut Development Authority**. The release identifies the mortgage as dated **May 20, 1982, Volume 1669 p.1**, and an assignment from CDA to Union Trust as trustee dated the same day, **Volume 1669 p.17**. These are role-specific references: terminal company as mortgagor, CDA as mortgagee, Union Trust as assignee/trustee. Union Trust is not thereby established as the original direct lender. [CH2-P66, official search portal](https://i2o.uslandrecords.com/CT/Bridgeport/D/), search locator below.

The release is a title/finance observation with no established cash amount, **not proof of payoff cash**. It states no principal, payoff amount, payer, receipt or transfer medium. The underlying 1982 mortgage/assignment was not inspected: the portal's earlier mortgage index entry offered no image pages, consistent with its stated June 1983 image start. Do not infer its amount or terms, a common funding source, or allocation to Barrier's $155,000 or the deed's unstated consideration.

The downloaded official Form III PDF is retained with its byte count, acquisition timestamp and SHA-256. Screenshot hashes identify review captures, not hashes of original instruments. Exact image-page locators and the portal are in P65 provenance. For conveyance facts, original recorded deed images and signed Form III outrank later administrative metadata; dated agency letters identify receipt/completeness. No broader environmental-culpability, operator-succession or person-match inference is made.

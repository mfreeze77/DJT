# Barrier: acquisition accounts and reported financing balances

November 13, 2026. Separate, non-additive comparison of DTA 807303 and the already indexed DTA 813859 sources. [Typed observations](barrier_observations.csv); [four-source provenance](barrier_source_manifest_2026-11-13.json). This is not a new counted payment chain.

[CH2-P62, pp.2–3](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/807303.dec.pdf#page=2); [CH2-P63, p.2](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=2); reused [CH2-P60, p.3](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/813859.DEC.pdf#page=3) and [CH2-P61, p.4](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/813859.det.pdf#page=4).

Seller and buyer wording, date and asset scope differ. The accounts cannot be silently merged, nor counted as two proved nonoverlapping flows. The $650,000 cash/note split and note terms are absent. The later loss is not a receipt or purchase price. No original agreement, closing statement, note or bank instrument was acquired. General leasing activity does not add leases to the 1988 acquisition paragraph; the later account does not identify particular lease locations. Neither account proves a remittance to a person or WHPC.

[P63, pp.9–10](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=10). The five components sum to the source total, but parent and components must never be added together. These are reported balances, not independently reconciled lender accounts, original principal or five disbursements. Origination dates, collateral and use of proceeds are absent. The officer is unnamed. The $400,000 credit-line entry does not establish the amount drawn and is distinct from the statutory surety requirement bearing the same amount. The Tribunal does not reproduce this table; that omission is neither reversal nor independent confirmation.

## Two reported tax payments, dates not invented

- **$13,888.24 reported paid promptly:** audit of June 1985–January 1988; letter dated March 28, 1988 states **$10,337.20 tax** plus penalty/interest to the total. The tax is a component, not a second receipt. The letter date is not an exact payment date; no notice number or bank instrument is supplied. [P62, p.7](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/807303.dec.pdf#page=7); [P63, p.5](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=5).
- **$25,000 reported payment:** Article 13-A returns for 1988 and January–February 1989 were received **March 15, 1989 without remittance**. Payment followed and the balance was deferred. March 15 is the return-receipt date, not the payment date. The nonpayment ground was withdrawn when the agreement became known, not the debt forgiven or proved paid in full. Neither total liability nor installment schedule is supplied. [P62, p.13 n.2](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/807303.dec.pdf#page=13); [P63, p.8 n.2](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=8).

These payment descriptions do not establish bank clearance or an allocation to acquisition consideration, loan proceeds, Shore Line sale proceeds or WHPC collections. No recovery aggregate is calculated.

## Limited registration result

On January 4, 1991 the Tribunal affirmed the ALJ: motor-fuel registration refusal was sustained; diesel registration was directed subject to a **$50,000 surety bond**. This is a condition, not proof of bond posting, a cash tax payment or later registration. The separate motor-fuel bond issue was not properly raised and remained undecided for a future application. [P62, pp.21–22](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/807303.dec.pdf#page=21); [P63, p.16](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/807303.DET.pdf#page=16). This bounded disposition summary is not a complete account of the decision's reasoning, a blanket exoneration or a finding that judicial review was exhausted.

## Data contract and prior-record preservation

The 16-field CSV holds separate typed observations, not central financial-flow rows. `parent_observation` joins debt components to the source total and the tax component to its reported payment. Empty amounts mean unquantified/not applicable, not zero; empty payment dates mean not supplied. All aggregation/counting/clearance flags are false. Paired ALJ/Tribunal versions are not independent transactions. Four source records are used: new P62/P63 and reused P60/P61; review text/renders are not additional originals.

The [Westchester/Shore Line appendix](westchester_shoreline_note_2026-11-13.md), its separate 1993 Atlas/Lewis sales and WHPC collections remain unchanged. FIN056/FIN057, Tunyung, Meridian and all prior claims/corrections are untouched. No corporate-identity merge, common-fund inference, federal-liability attribution or Trump receipt is established by this comparison.

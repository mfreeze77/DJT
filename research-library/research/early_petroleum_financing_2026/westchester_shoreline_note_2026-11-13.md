## Shore Line sales, debt use and payment status

The earlier Tribunal decision reports **October 15, 1993** sale of the Bronx customer list and a Mack truck to **Atlas Fuel Oil Corp. for $446,108.50**, and **December 21, 1993** sale of a customer list covering certain towns in Westchester, Putnam and Dutchess Counties to **Lewis Oil Company for $285,943.45**. These are reported sale consideration, not independently verified bank receipts and not WHPC collections. Proceeds satisfied certain Shore Line tax warrants docketed April 22, 1993 and other debts; neither the amount allocated to taxes nor payment dates or instruments are given. Do not count the described debt use as a second quantified transfer. [P60, p.10](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/813859.DEC.pdf#page=10).

The later decision's general 1992/1993 description and shorter Atlas Fuel/Lewis Fuel labels are retained as a version discrepancy, not silently normalized or counted as additional sales. The exact dates and buyer labels above come from the earlier detailed account. [P58, pp.4–5](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/815413.dec.pdf#page=4).

Shore Line's January 23, 1995 deferred-payment agreement covered six assessments: **$86,845.94 tax**, **$125,021.94 including penalty/interest**, with **$4,997.61 minimum monthly payments**. These are obligation/account figures, not cash receipts. Substantial compliance at the hearing is not proof every installment cleared or full payoff then. [P60, p.9](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/813859.DEC.pdf#page=9). The later decision reports remaining taxes paid in early 1997; no new payment amount or bank-clearance date is supplied. This later status is not a contradiction of earlier liabilities. [P58, pp.4–5,13–14](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/815413.dec.pdf#page=4).

## Reliability and narrow disposition

The June 28, 1995 Shore Line CARTS printout still showed **$99,689.15** on L007951478-9 despite a stipulation reducing tax to **$70,000**, and incorrectly classified protest status. This is a specific reliability control, not a refund/payment or grounds for rejecting every CARTS record. [P60, pp.6–7](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/813859.DEC.pdf#page=6).

In DTA 813859, the Tribunal denied Shore Line's exception and petition, affirmed the ALJ and sustained the proposed refusal to register. [P60, pp.19–20](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/813859.DEC.pdf#page=19); [CH2-P61, pp.34–35](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/813859.det.pdf#page=34). In DTA 815413, it denied Standard Petroleum's exception and petition, affirmed the ALJ and sustained the January 5, 1996 proposed registration cancellation. Even after Shore Line's paid status removed its unpaid-tax ground, WHPC's final unpaid liabilities and Shore Line's continued unregistered operations remained independently sufficient grounds. [P58, pp.13–15](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/815413.dec.pdf#page=13). The ALJ expressly said the three corporations were not accused of fraudulent/deceptive acts in that proceeding. That is neither a fraud conviction nor blanket exoneration. [CH2-P59, p.15](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/determinations/815413.det.pdf#page=15). No exhausted judicial review is asserted.

## Data contract and preservation

The 16-field CSV is a standalone, non-additive observation appendix. `source_ids` join the four-entry manifest and indices; `related_observations` connect the two sale descriptions and their unquantified use, not additional payment edges. Empty amounts mean unquantified/not applicable, not zero. Every aggregation/counting/clearance flag is false. Receipt, debt, sale consideration, obligation and noncash record correction are different types; none may be summed here. Paired ALJ/Tribunal versions are not independent money events. PDFs are original sources; extracted text and page renders are review aids. Original CARTS, checks, bank records, sale instruments and underlying stipulation were not acquired.

Existing transactions, relationships, FIN056/FIN057, Tunyung payment, Meridian assessments, claims and correction history remain unchanged. This appendix establishes no Trump receipt or new corporate identity match.

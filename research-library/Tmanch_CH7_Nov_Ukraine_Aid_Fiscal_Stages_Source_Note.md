# Ukraine aid: September 30 fiscal snapshot and November PURL framework

## September 30 balances, reported in November

[Lead Inspector General quarterly report, Table 2 (PDF p. 11), printed pp. 16–17 and 28–29](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=11) separates received or appropriated funds, obligations and disbursements, as of September 30, 2026:

| Fiscal line | Appropriated / received | Obligated | Disbursed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| European Capacity Building, fully allocated to Ukraine | $400.00M | $399.98M | $37.56M |
| NATO Contributions Received | $4,891.25M | $3,202.96M | $423.30M |

These are successive stages of the same dollars, not additive. ECB is included in the report's Ukraine Security Assistance discussion but is a separate Table 2 line from pre-existing USAI appropriations; the $400 million in the oversight letter is not a second $400 million. The $362.42 million difference between obligations and disbursements is a dated fiscal difference, not a cash balance, undelivered equipment valuation or proof of a current backlog.

For [legacy J20-032](J20-Pres.md), the report separately attributes about $6.8 billion pledged or contributed for PURL to State; Table 2 is the U.S. account stage. The rounded allocation figures, about $2.32 billion for Ukraine procurement and $2.58 billion for U.S. stock replenishment, are not disbursement or delivery amounts. JUMPSTART is distinct: the same report gives $3.1 billion pledged and $2.8 billion transferred per DSCA. The [November 17 NATO transcript](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/events/transcripts/2026/11/17/joint-press-conference-at-the-udcg) uses a later, broader pledge figure across PURL and U.S. foreign military sales. Do not add these figures, compare them as if underlying stages and dates matched, or join them to a specific sale without case-level evidence.

The [November 9 Bennet/Costa letter, published Nov. 12](https://www.bennet.senate.gov/2026/11/12/bennet-costa-colleagues-press-administration-to-expedite-delivery-of-ukraine-aid/) asks OMB and DoD to explain the timing by November 30. Final delivery into FY2029 and reported exclusions are the lawmakers' characterization of the spend plan, not inspected delivery records. Check the public response and actual execution, not just the deadline. The [October 30 proposed Patriot sale](Tmanch_CH7_Oct30_Patriot_Sale_Source_Note.md) is not the ECB account.

## November 19 Cabinet decision: J20-2026-120

The [Ukrainian government readout](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/ukraina-zmozhe-oplachuvaty-amerykansku-zbroiu-cherez-purl-koshtamy-kredytu-ies) records Cabinet approval to sign a General Framework Arrangement with NATO for later use of Ukraine Support Loan funds through PURL. It describes package-specific letters, submission of a package list to the European Commission and NATO as financial intermediary, not any completed payment or executed framework. The [Commission's earlier eligibility decision](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/ukraine/commission-approves-eur61-billion-support-ukraines-air-and-missile-defence-and-other-defence-needs_en) concerned a broader package; do not attribute that total to the Cabinet step or equate this PURL mechanism with transmittal 26-113's JUMPSTART and national-funds scope.

Ukraine may gain an additional procurement route while U.S. industry and stock replenishment may benefit through separate mechanisms. Actual aggregate fiscal stages are documented above; specific vendor receipts, delivered materiel and measured effects do not follow. No Russian benefit or score added. Check a signed framework, package-specific confirmation and account outlays separately.

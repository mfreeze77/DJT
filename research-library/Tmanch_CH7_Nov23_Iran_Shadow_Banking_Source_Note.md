# November 23 Iran shadow-banking sanctions and FinCEN dataset

**J20-2026-130.** [OFAC’s November 23 legal listing](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261123) adds **8 individuals and 22 entities**, with no vessel, aircraft or unrelated administrative changes listed. Most use Iran E.O. 13902. Robust Trading is tagged E.O. 13871; H N B Trading carries SDGT and IFSR tags tied to the E.O. 13224 counterterrorism authority. [Treasury’s explanation](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0699) identifies the Bank Sarmayeh-associated Nesim Baharan, Mellat Exchange and other exchange-house or front-company networks. Legal blocked status and exposure are verified; Treasury's conduct descriptions are attributed, and no resulting blocked balance or interrupted payment has been verified.

**Separate from the designation amounts.** [FinCEN’s analysis, pp. 2–3](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/100/FTA-Iran-Shadow-Banking.pdf#page=3) reports approximately **$14.7 billion across 4,983 transactions** potentially linked to Iranian shadow banking from January 2025 to August 2026. The dataset used selected Bank Secrecy Act returns, including suspicious activity reports and certain correspondent banking reports, with a $500,000 minimum and screening for corroboration of links to Iran. It is an analyzed sample, not a new seizure, a proven illicit-profit total or the transaction roster for the 30 listed parties. Country tables that count both sides of transfers are not additive to the headline. FinCEN reports declining observed volumes and treats partial network disruption as possible, not proven by this designation.

**What it adds:** a concrete U.S. institutional restriction following the distinct November 4 and November 6 actions (J20-2026-081 and -090). It constrains named financial intermediaries through U.S. sanctions and exposes counterparties to potential penalties. This supports U.S. financial-security objectives and pressure on Iran, but neither public record identifies a specific Russia-linked flow or shows an effect on Kremlin revenue or support to Ukraine. It is not counted as Russia sanctions enforcement, a reversal of [Graham Act waivers in J20-2026-025](J20-Pres.md), or evidence of personal presidential direction. No score or cash total assigned.

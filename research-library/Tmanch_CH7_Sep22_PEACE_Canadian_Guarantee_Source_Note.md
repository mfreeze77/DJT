# PEACE project K060-UA: Canadian guarantee, IBRD cash and Ukraine's budget

**J20-2026-132 (September 2026 backfill reviewed November 25).** The World Bank's [September 4 Board decision](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099090826161534555) approved the tenth additional financing under the Public Expenditures for Administrative Capacity Endurance (PEACE) project, P178946, backed by Canada. Separate World Bank document records identify the [IBRD–Ukraine loan K060-UA](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099091126111519269), the [Canada–IBRD guarantee](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099091426154012919), and the [disbursement instructions](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099091126111513659). Ukraine's Finance Ministry [reported signing by September 14](https://www.mof.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine_to_receive_841_million_from_the_world_bank_under_canadas_guarantee_ministry_of_finance_and_world_bank_signed_agreement-5913).

The [September 22 recipient announcement](https://www.mof.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine_received_usd_839_million_from_the_world_bank_under_canadas_guarantee-5922) confirms **$841 million** received for reimbursing state-budget pension expenditures. Use the ministry's title and body, not the legacy “839” in its URL. Its [October budget readout](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/imf_and_world_bank_annual_meetings_imf_and_world_bank_leadership_welcome_ukraines_progress_in_implementing_reforms-5979) confirms receipt into the state budget. The lender's [Finances One data](https://financesone.worldbank.org/countries/ukraine) lists IBRDK0600 with $841 million principal and $841 million disbursed. The [World Bank's aggregate financing table](https://www.worldbank.org/en/brief/2026/05/31/world-bank-emergency-financing-package-for-ukraine) reflects the same amount; do not count the agreement, lender disbursement and recipient receipt as separate flows.

IBRD provided the loan cash and Ukraine's state budget received it; Canada provided credit backing. No guarantee call, Canadian cash payout, individual pension payment, private beneficiary or connection to the Vodafone transaction is established. This is a positive comparator for the separate Vodafone financing investigation: a named instrument and guarantee are not treated as realized draw until lender and recipient evidence supports one.

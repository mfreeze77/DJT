# October 23 Oleshky humanitarian announcement: conflicting dates, prospective outcomes

**J20-2026-043.** The [White House announcement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/10/first-lady-melania-trump-secures-ceasefire-between-russia-and-ukraine-localized-to-oleshky/) credits Melania Trump with arranging a localized ceasefire for aid and preparation for voluntary civilian evacuation, naming October 29. This is the administration's account, not independent observation of a ceasefire.

The [Ukrainian armed-forces notice](https://www.zsu.gov.ua/news/syly-oborony-ukrainy-zaprovadzhuiut-rezhym-prypynennia-vohniu-dlia-dopomohy-meshkantsiam-okupovanykh-oleshok), dated October 23 at 22:05, names 03:00 Kyiv time October 27 for a defined-area ceasefire. **The dates differ; the inspected evidence does not reconcile them.** Its full short text was inspected through the search index. Direct retrieval returned 403 and was stopped; no original-byte archive is claimed.

[ArmyInform's earlier 20:40 government report](https://armyinform.com.ua/2026/10/23/vpershe-uzgodzhena-gumanitarna-misiya-v-oleshkah-syly-oborony-zabezpechat-rezhym-tyshi-ta-vyhid-czyvilnyh/) confirms Ukrainian readiness and repeats the White House date. It predates the later military notice and cannot settle the discrepancy. White House and ArmyInform originals were acquired; hashes and source IDs **OLESHKY-S001–003** are in the [manifest](Tmanch_CH7_Oct23_Evening_Source_Manifest.json). Publication order is not proof of a revised agreement.

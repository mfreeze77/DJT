## Verified action and scope

Jamie Raskin, House Judiciary ranking member, signed an October 26 [four-page letter](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-10-26-raskin-to-sommers-api-thompson-afpm-clark-uscc-re-russian-diesel.pdf) to Mike Sommers of the American Petroleum Institute, Suzanne P. Clark of the U.S. Chamber of Commerce and Chet Thompson of American Fuel & Petrochemical Manufacturers. **DIESEL-INDUSTRY-S001.** The [same-day official announcement](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/ranking-member-raskin-puts-oil-industry-on-notice-anyone-helping-trump-and-putin-evade-america-s-ban-on-russian-oil-could-face-criminal-penalties), **DIESEL-INDUSTRY-S002**, links the original and is the same congressional source family.

The letter warns the associations and their members that Raskin considers Russian-diesel imports exposed to civil and criminal liability notwithstanding GL 135. It invokes the 2022 statutory import prohibition, its termination procedures and the October 16 order's savings clause. This records his legal position, not an adjudication, binding agency interpretation, prosecution or independently established statutory violation. The letter supplies no response deadline, subpoena or records-production demand. No recipient response or resulting commercial decision was inspected. Being an addressee or association member establishes no participation in a diesel transaction.

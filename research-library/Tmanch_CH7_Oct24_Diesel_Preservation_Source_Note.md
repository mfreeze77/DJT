## What the request does

The letter seeks preservation of transaction, shipping, customs, payment and intermediary records, including business communications on personal devices and relevant government communications from September 1, 2026. It requests written confirmation by October 28, 2026 of preservation measures and disclosure of involvement or knowledge. It is not a subpoena, court ruling, received response or proof that preservation occurred. The signers' legal objections remain attributed positions, not findings of liability or proof of statutory noncompliance. No new legal accusation is adopted here.

# November 3 notice: continuation of the 1979 Iran national emergency

**J20-2026-083 / CH7-IRAN-EMERGENCY-2026-001.** Unscored presidential legal-continuity action, signed November 3 and published November 5. This is separate from the November 4 OFAC designation additions in **081**.

**IRAN-12170-S001:** The [final Federal Register notice, 91 FR 69947, document 2026-22685](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-05/pdf/2026-22685.pdf), titled “Continuation of the National Emergency With Respect to Iran,” states that the emergency declared on **November 14, 1979 by Executive Order 12170** and related measures must continue beyond **November 14, 2026**. Invoking section 202(d) of the National Emergencies Act, **50 U.S.C. 1622(d)**, Trump continues that emergency **for one year**. The signed notice is dated **November 3**; November 5 is the publication date, not a second presidential action. **IRAN-12170-S001-PI:** The [public-inspection PDF](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-22685.pdf) is the same notice at an earlier publication stage, not independent corroboration or another renewal.

The notice gives the administration's stated reason: relations with Iran have not normalized and implementation of the **January 19, 1981 agreements** remains ongoing. It expressly distinguishes this emergency from **Executive Order 12957 of March 15, 1995**, and this renewal from the **March 2, 2026** renewal. That distinction prevents treating every Iran emergency or sanctions announcement as the same legal action. The notice orders publication and transmission to Congress; the final publication is verified, but congressional receipt is not.

## Materiality, beneficiaries and limits

The completed act preserves an existing emergency framework and its related measures rather than allowing that authority to lapse. Its specific, prospective US institutional benefit is continuity for the legal framework associated with implementing the 1981 agreements. This is analytical interpretation of the notice's stated purpose, not evidence of a new settlement, collection, recovered asset or measurable national gain. The instrument identifies no new sanctioned person, blocked-property amount, cargo restriction or expanded sanctions programme. Net economic and national effects remain unmeasured; no Russia-specific benefit or financial flow is established.

Because this is a signed presidential notice, presidential authorship is direct, unlike an inference from an agency action. Its materiality is **maintenance of existing authority**, not a new punitive package. No motive beyond the stated rationale or connection to private financial interests is inferred.

**Existing joins:** **J20-2026-033** is an earlier Iran-sanctions/licensing action; **068 / CH7-IRAN-FF1-001** is a bank-specific wind-down permission; **081 / CH7-IRAN-NOV4-DESIGNATIONS-001** adds named entries under other Iran authorities. This notice neither creates their targets nor supersedes their specific transaction rules. No OFAC licence-matrix or money-flow row is added, and no event score is changed.

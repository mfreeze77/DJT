# October 26 outbound-investment proposal

**J20-2026-053 / CH7-OISP-PROPOSAL-001**. Inspected October 26, 2026. **OISP-S001**: [authenticated Federal Register proposal, 2026-21786](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/pdf/2026-21786.pdf), 91 FR 67396–67433; Docket TREAS–DO–2026–0595; RIN 1505–AC94.

## Documented scope and status

On October 26 Treasury's Office of Investment Security published proposed regulations implementing Subtitle C of the Comprehensive Outbound Investment National Security Act, codified as Title VIII of the Defense Production Act. The preamble identifies the December 18, 2025 enactment in the FY2026 NDAA; this is not the separately tracked Graham Act. The proposal would impose notification requirements or prohibitions on specified US-person investments in sensitive technologies. Proposed §851.207 includes Russia and Iran among countries of concern. It is not a blanket ban on all investment or all financial services. Sector definitions, knowledge standards, covered-person tests and exceptions matter; the preamble describes exclusions for ordinary bank lending, payment processing/clearing and other specified services (PDF pages 1–4; proposed definitions on pages 25–26).

Written comments are due December 7, 2026. This is a **proposed rule, not a final regulation or newly operative prohibition**. Proposed §851.102 retains a placeholder for the final rule's effective date. The preamble says the proposal will not operate retroactively; existing Part 850 obligations continue for activities in the applicable period. No submitted comments, final rule, blocked investment or realized enforcement outcome is established here. Publication is an agency implementation step, not proof of personal presidential direction.

## Cumulative record and who benefits

**United States/allies — prospective.** Restricting covered investment and its associated capabilities could protect US/allied national security and constrain adversary military or intelligence technology development. That is the mechanism; no Russia-specific investment denied, revenue lost or capability reduction has been measured. Conditions and exceptions limit reach.

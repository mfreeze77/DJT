# Burgas refinery: shareholder facility and group trade setoff

Evidence supplement for **J20-2026-019** (Bulgaria operations) and **J20-2026-114** ([GL 131L](Tmanch_CH7_Nov19_LIG_Berlin_Source_Note.md#gl-131l), contingent international divestment). This is not a new U.S. policy action or evidence that a sale closed.

## Shareholder credit

Lukoil Neftochim Burgas AD (Bulgarian UIC 812114069) reports **EUR 565.8 million drawn as of December 31, 2025**, under a **EUR 600 million unsecured facility** from Swiss Litasco SA. [FY2025 accounts, Note 23, printed pp. 33–34 (PDF pp. 35–36)](https://portal.registryagency.bg/CR/api/Documents/7033eb3df48c48139819c4c1c9f823a6#page=35). The reported drawn principal translates to BGN 1.106609 billion; the table separately presents BGN 1.112826 billion as the nominal and carrying amount. These are not additive. The Bulgarian text labels LF-A August 30, 2023, gives December 31, 2025 as the contractual deadline as extended by Addendum No. 2 (November 1, 2024), and says that draws and principal payments stopped following U.S. and UK sanctions. Litasco sent an early full repayment demand on November 7, 2025. The accounts do not establish the current payoff amount or collection.

Management describes deferral, refinancing or other resolution of the shareholder loan as a condition affecting going concern. It did not assume a Bulgarian direct-financing commitment. [Note 2(a), printed pp. 8–9 (PDF pp. 10–11)](https://portal.registryagency.bg/CR/api/Documents/7033eb3df48c48139819c4c1c9f823a6#page=10). Under [Note 31, printed p. 57 (PDF p. 59)](https://portal.registryagency.bg/CR/api/Documents/7033eb3df48c48139819c4c1c9f823a6#page=59), the company separately reports BGN 22.224 million in interest paid on Litasco's unsecured facility during FY2025. The annual total does not establish dates, payment to Russia, payments after the November demand, or effects of the November 2026 license.

## Separate realized trade netting

[Note 32(c), printed p. 58 (PDF p. 60)](https://portal.registryagency.bg/CR/api/Documents/7033eb3df48c48139819c4c1c9f823a6#page=60) reports a March 31, 2026 intragroup setoff. Burgas used USD 179.041 million of its own trade claims and USD 17.199 million of claims assigned by Lukoil Bulgaria EOOD against its trade obligations to Litasco. The USD 196.240 million sum is claims applied, not a third amount. Burgas reported USD 21.552 million remaining payable to Litasco on trade accounts and a new payable to Lukoil Bulgaria. This reflects actual accounting settlement of trade balances, not proof of a bank transfer, parent dividend, shareholder-loan repayment or Russian state receipt.

## Procurement relief, license limits and who benefits

The [Bulgarian Council of Ministers' June 30 account](https://www.government.bg/bg/prestsentar/novini/ot-utre-%E2%80%9Elukoyl-neftohim-burgas%E2%80%9C-shte-mozhe-da-zakupuva-neft-ot-vsichki-kontragenti-registrirani-v-shveytsaria) links the 2023 loan dispute to restrictions on procuring crude from Swiss-registered counterparties and says those restrictions would lift July 1. It describes a wider out-of-court resolution as a goal, not an executed discharge. [Litasco's February 25 statement](https://litasco.com/api/presscenter/exportpressrelease?id=723754) reports a February 19 notice of investment dispute; that is separate from evidence of an arbitration filing, judgment or financial settlement.

The procurement change removes an operating constraint for Burgas and its customers, while operating continuity may preserve the value of the group's existing equity and creditor interests. [U.S. GL 130B](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/937241/download) and [UK INT/2025/7895596](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ofsi-general-licence-int20257895596) provide separate, conditional Bulgaria operations permissions; they are not GL 131L closing authority or proof of authorization for these specific transactions. No realized Russia benefit is assigned from procurement relief or a buyer announcement. At a documented closing or refinancing, distinguish repayment, rollover, waiver, assignment and ongoing amounts owed, and verify any blocked-funds conditions, beneficial recipient and actual disposition before attributing proceeds. Do not add historical interest or the USD trade setoff to the EUR principal, sale proceeds, or sanctions scores.

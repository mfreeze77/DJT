## Inspected originals

**UK-BUDGET-S001:** The [October 28 Budget](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-document/budget-2026-html), Ukraine discussion and policy-decisions section, announces support from 2026–27: £500 million additional winter air defence; £7 billion over three years, including up to £5.5 billion World Bank loan guarantees and £1.5 billion mobilized through the IDA crisis facility; and £3 billion additional UKEF credit capacity, bringing total cover to £6.5 billion. These are different instruments, not £10 billion cash delivered. The already-committed £750 million ERA component and existing £3 billion annual military baseline must not be counted again as new support. The Budget separately confirms a £17 million ODA grant for Chornobyl's New Safe Confinement repairs and commits £3 million in 2027–28 for deported children's tracing, return and reintegration, bringing that work's total to £7 million. This records commitments; it does not establish executed guarantees, drawdowns, deliveries, repairs or completed returns.

**UK-BUDGET-S002:** The [October 28 Downing Street call readout](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/prime-minister-and-chancellors-call-with-the-president-of-ukraine-28-october-2026) dates the call to that morning and repeats the three principal components. It is another UK government account of the same package, not a second financial flow or independent delivery confirmation. No operative funding contracts or additional parliamentary implementation instruments were acquired.

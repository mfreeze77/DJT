The separately signed €10.7 million EU grant supports implementation and compensation capacity; no grant payment was verified. Do not include it in the housing-loan cash. The separate €80 million displaced-person housing-voucher facility is not the second HOME III tranche.

The [November 20 Ministry of Finance review](https://mof.gov.ua/en/news/olga_zykova_and_the_ceb_discuss_results_of_joint_projects_and_cooperation_prospects_for_2027-6030) reports €650 million in CEB loan agreements signed and over €560 million received across Ukraine's portfolio since it joined in 2023, as of early November. Its separate €200 million received for HOME I and II is earlier financing, not part of HOME III; the October 29 ministry update attributes 6,174 issued certificates and more than 15,700 people in purchased homes to that earlier funding. Do not add the first HOME III tranche or the earlier phases to the portfolio receipt figure. The intended HOME III beneficiaries are households whose homes were destroyed; this record shows Ukrainian sovereign receipt and initial reservations, not end-beneficiary settlement.

## Separate EIB–EU social-rental housing pilot: signed, not verified paid

This is not HOME III. [EIB project 20230998](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20230998) lists two signatures on June 25, 2026: €50 million of EIB loan financing and a €50 million EU investment grant for the pilot. The €400 million approximate full-project cost and €250 million approximate proposed EIB financing for the wider program are not additional signed or paid amounts.

[Ukraine's housing ministry](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/lviv-zhytomyr-ta-kropyvnytskyi-zbuduiut-nove-sotsialne-zhytlo-za-pidtrymky-ieib-ta-ies-minrehion-rekomenduvav-hromady) reported November 24 that its commission recommended **Lviv, Zhytomyr and Kropyvnytskyi** from five assessed cities, with a target of housing for more than 1,500 families in the 2027 pilot, including displaced and other priority households. Design and cost documentation remain to be developed. The [Rada’s bill 0403 record](https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/71549) shows passage on November 11 and dispatch to the President on November 16; the record checked November 26 did not show a later presidential signature or effective date. No pilot draw, cash allocation to an individual city, contractor payment or occupancy was verified. The €50 million HOME III Ukrainian receipt above must not be confused with either signature here.

The [Finance Ministry’s November 1 EIB portfolio snapshot](https://mof.gov.ua/en/eib) separately reports €2.6096 billion disbursed for its specified public-sector project portfolio, including €49.12 million in 2026 through the cutoff. It does not allocate a payment to this pilot or reconcile the portfolio perimeter to the separate €50 million Naftogaz draw (J20-2026-136); do not add them.

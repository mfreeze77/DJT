# October 15–16, 2026: reported Patriot interceptor shortage

## Evidence and source boundaries

[CNN's October 15 report by Victoria Butenko and Lauren Kent, syndicated by ABC17](https://abc17news.com/news/national-world/cnn-world/2026/10/15/exclusive-ukraine-runs-out-of-patriot-defense-missiles-leaving-it-more-vulnerable-to-russian-attacks-source-tells-cnn/), attributes exhausted Patriot stocks and missed ballistic interceptions during the October 13–14 attack to an unnamed source. This is attributed reporting, not an authenticated stock ledger. Interception totals cannot establish remaining ammunition. CNN syndications and broadcasts belong to one reporting family, not independent confirmations.

[The Post's sources differed on remaining inventory; a NATO source said deliveries continued.](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/10/15/ukraine-critically-low-patriot-interceptors-russian-strikes-intensify/)

[European Pravda's October 16 Brussels briefing report](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/news/2026/10/16/7247886/) attributes to Zelenskyy severe PAC-2/PAC-3 scarcity, priority allocation to the Middle East, and efforts to assemble small batches from partners. This supports an attributed shortage assessment, not a verified receipt or exact stock count.

Reviewed October 16: indexed CNN syndication and European Pravda text; accessible Post article. Direct requests for the first two initially failed. No unretrieved presidency statement or CNN transcript is treated as independently inspected evidence.

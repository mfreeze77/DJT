## Later closing clarifies transaction types

The February 5, 1986 assignment, effective February 1, transfers **49.9% of the entire JV** from Equitable to Trump for **$44,491,050 in non-negotiable promissory-note consideration** (PX-632 pp.199–203). This is seller financing, not that amount of cash paid. The note's full terms and repayment history are absent.

The executed closing agreement recites **$2,792,223 already distributed**, half to each partner (**$1,396,111.50 each**, pp.188/195), and **$477,793.50 paid by Trump to Equitable** as a real-estate-tax adjustment (p.189). These are executed-instrument recitals, not cleared checks; adjustment provisions remain applicable. Do not add the recipient halves to the total again. The $167,486.33 Chase interest is paid-or-payable language, while obligations and a reserve are prospective. The $717,020.61 obligations figure conflicts by ten cents with Schedule I's $717,020.71; neither is treated as a demonstrated completed payment.

## Earlier operating-business money: separate evidentiary chain

**CH2-P27:** The [February 27, 1997 NY Tax Appeals Tribunal decision, DTA 801735/801744/807078](https://www.dta.ny.gov/pdf/archive/decisions/801735.dec.pdf), pp.3–5, describes SMI's April 1982 formation, a July Republic National Bank account authorization, and a December 1, 1982 agreement pricing ten shares at **$130,000**, transferred February 21, 1983. ## Cody/Hixon: primary qualification of an unresolved claim

**CH2-P30:** [Manufacturers Hanover Trust Co. v. Hixon, November 13, 1986](https://openjurist.org/124/ad2d/488/manufacturers-hanover-trust-co-v-hixon), records a January 4, 1984 note/mortgage on Hixon's Tower condos and later missed payments. It supplies neither principal nor a Cody funding link.

**CH2-P31:** The [June 24, 1992 NJ DGE investigation, case 92-107](https://a.fastcompany.net/asset_files/-/2016/03/04/Trump1992MOB.pdf#page=23), pp.23–25, describes Trump-Equitable's 1984 alterations suit and a March 1, 1985 settlement specifying $500,000, including $68,251.59 payable to the condo board. Those are terms, not cleared disbursements. DGE says reviewed litigation records disclosed no evidence of Cody's apartment interest; an FBI agent characterized the information as weak and Trump as a possible coercion victim. DGE nevertheless recommended sworn questioning. This materially qualifies CH2-CODY-021; it does not resolve the reported Swiss-loan story.

## Cumulative assessment and next records

These records distinguish family refinancing, institutional project debt, seller financing, partnership distributions, purchaser encumbrances and operating-business acquisitions. They sharpen known funding routes without changing CH2-SOVFIN-033's insufficient-evidence conclusion. Neither proximity nor shared counsel supplies proof of illicit funds reaching Trump or Soviet financing.

Needed next: original ACRIS instruments and closing statements; identification of the remaining purchasers; actual equity/draw/payoff records; original 1980 costs schedule; 1986 seller note and repayment ledger; Hixon note, settlement performance and any source-of-funds evidence. No inference of criminal participation follows from being a lender, attorney or institutional counterparty.

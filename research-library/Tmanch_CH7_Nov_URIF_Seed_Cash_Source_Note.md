# U.S.–Ukraine Reconstruction Investment Fund: seed commitment, cash and projects

**J20-2026-124; September 30, 2026 cutoff.** [DFC's FY2026 audited financial statements, Note 19 p. 67](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/USIDFC_FY2026_AMR.pdf#page=67) distinguish **$75 million committed**, **$5 million disbursed to the partnership during FY2026**, and **$3.6 million carrying value** at September 30. The [FY2025 financial statements](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/DFC%20Annual%20Management%20Report%20-%20FY%202025_508.pdf) disclose no disbursement against that commitment by their September 30 cutoff. Thus $5 million is the audited cumulative DFC-to-fund cash through September 2026, not a measure of fund cash on hand or payments to any project. DFC describes authorized seed uses as fees, expenses and initial investments. There is no basis to attribute a specific project payment or to treat the carrying value change as a cash loss.

The [Operation Atlantic Resolve quarterly report, PDF p. 12 and endnote 28](https://www.stateoig.gov/uploads/report/report_pdf_file/oar_q4_sep2026.pdf#page=12) describes the $75 million U.S. capitalization as a disbursement. The **$70 million difference** between that wording and the transaction-specific audited cash outflow is unresolved; the record does not establish a second payment, a missing-funds event, or when any later contribution was made. Do not add $5 million to $75 million or adjust the oversight report's whole-program totals on this evidence alone. This civilian investment fund is distinct from military aid and DFC's direct Vodafone Ukraine facility.

Note 19 records equal U.S. and Ukrainian representation on the six-person board of the general partner, no federal guarantee of partnership obligations and no obligation to cover losses beyond committed capital. DFC's disclosed ownership of the general partner does not give it unilateral board control. Distributions of project earnings to the U.S. are not payable before the tenth anniversary under the agreement; those future distributions are not today’s cash flows. This adds an implementation record to the existing Chapter 7 discussion of the fund's creation; it is not a second creation or a fresh award.

## DTEK and the October project approvals

- [DFC Board Resolution BDR(26)17, September 16](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/BDR%2826%2917%20DTEK%20BESS_508.pdf) authorizes **up to $185 million in principal plus interest** for DTEK battery storage, naming six BESS UA B.V. subsidiaries: DTEK TILIGULSKA WEP-2 LLC; SOLAR FARM - 4, - 6, - 7 and - 8 LLCs; and DTEK BERDYANSKA WIND FARM LLC. This sets a ceiling, not a per-entity allocation or evidence of a draw.
- [DTEK's October 2 statement](https://dtek.com/en/media-center/news/us-backed-investment-fund-introduces-financing-for-dteks-landmark-battery-storage-proje/) puts URIF inside the **€85 million** announced financing structure, without stating the lender split. The same six-site 200 MW/400 MWh system was [energized in September 2025 with Fluence](https://dtek.com/en/media-center/news/dtek-and-fluence-energise-the-largest-battery-storage-facility-in-ukraine-with-a-total-capacity-of-200-mw/), when DTEK reported **€125 million** invested. That is prior project investment, not new DFC or URIF funding, or a known payment to Fluence. Do not add the three figures.
- [Treasury's October 2 announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0648/) and [Ukraine's Economy Ministry](https://www.me.gov.ua/News/Detail/4effb053-bd14-4b24-803a-d380986ebdab?lang=uk-UA) distinguish approvals and third-party financing targets from cash disbursements. [BGV](https://bgv.com.ua/en/urif-and-bgv-create-joint-investment-platform-for-critical-minerals-projects-in-ukraine/) expects up to $30 million for the separate minerals platform, with individual project financing to be structured separately; no project-level receipt established.

Ukraine and project companies may benefit if projects are financed and executed. No assignment of the U.S. seed cash to specific companies or vendor receipts. Check subsequent DFC or fund capital calls with proof of payment, fund-level accounts and signed project-level documents or draw notices. No change to Russia benefit scoring.

## Legal instrument and dates

**CAN-RUS-NOV9-S001–004:** Global Affairs Canada's [November 9 Paris announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/11/minister-anand-announces-additional-sanctions-to-reduce-russias-missile-capabilities-and-raise-economic-costs.html) describes a package adding **24 individuals and 38 entities** under the Special Economic Measures (Russia) Regulations. The [official country chronology](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/russia-russie.aspx?lang=eng) identifies **SOR/2026-233 as entered into force November 5, 2026**, separately from its November 9 announcement, and links the full [English](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/russia_regulations-reglement_russie5.aspx?lang=eng) and [French](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/russia_regulations-reglement_russie5.aspx?lang=fra) amendment.

Sections 3–4 contain the 24-person and 38-entity Schedule 1 additions. Section 21 provides entry into force on the day of registration. The **November 5 date is sourced to Global Affairs Canada's explicit entry-in-force record**; no separate Gazette or registration record was acquired in the bounded search. It is not inferred from the later announcement or an assumed publication clock time.

The Schedule 1 framework imposes an asset freeze and dealings prohibition on designated persons, applying to persons in Canada and Canadians abroad, subject to applicable exceptions and permits. This establishes a legal restriction, not that particular assets were located, frozen, forfeited or measured. Government descriptions of the targets' activities are the government's rationale, not independent findings of each designee's conduct. This action-only record does not adopt new misconduct allegations.

## The amendment is not uniformly restrictive

Section 1 adds **3.04(3)**, a narrow exception from the listed-vessel financial-services prohibition for financial services required for listed ships' owners, operators or managers to obtain Canadian legal services concerning these regulations' prohibitions. The package also includes language and tariff-description/code corrections. Those provisions should not be summarized as new sanction tightening or a general shipping exemption.

**CAN-RUS-NOV9-S005:** The acquired [Justice Canada consolidation](https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-58/FullText.html) explicitly says it is current to **October 19** and last amended **September 4, 2026**. It supplies baseline context but does not yet incorporate this amendment. The newer official instrument and chronology control the later addition; absence of the new names from the lagging consolidation is not contrary evidence.

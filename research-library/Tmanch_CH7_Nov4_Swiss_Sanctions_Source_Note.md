# November 4 Swiss sanctions adoption and commencement

**J20-2026-082 / CH7-SWISS-NOV4-001.** Unscored contextual European sanctions action. Swiss legal action is not a US presidential action, implemented confiscation or measured economic outcome.

## Official announcement and list boundaries

**CH-SAN-S001:** The [November 4 Federal Council release](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/KsmEWk4E-bHM) reports adoption of the EU's 21st-package Russia/Belarus measures and other listings. EAER list decisions were made November 3, effective November 4 at 23:00; no timezone is expressly supplied. The release reports 254 additional asset-freeze/resource-prohibition entries, 41 export-control companies, 41 vessels and separate transaction-ban groups. These categories are not additive or a reconciled unique-person total. Six individuals and one company were removed. Two Russian nationals were not included in the sanctions list for now, while fourteen third-country companies were not made subject to enhanced export controls. The release separately says existing 20th-package operational measures address circumvention; that is an official assurance, not an independently measured outcome. This is selective alignment, not identical adoption of every EU restriction. Counts remain the government's aggregate account, not an independently reconciled annex census. No listed person's underlying conduct is newly adopted as a finding.

## Operative text and dates

**CH-SAN-S002:** [RO 2026 601](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/oc/2026/601/fr), the November 4 amendment to the Ukraine ordinance, was inspected in the original browser-rendered text and acquired as original [Fedlex XML](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2026/601/fr/xml/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-oc-2026-601-fr-xml-1.xml). Section III sets general commencement on **November 5, 2026**, but **new Article 12h's LNG-terminal-service prohibition starts January 1, 2027**. Thus not every announced restriction is already operative. The XML's document/publication dates are November 4 and general entry date November 5. The observed Fedlex index/sidebar instead displayed November 3 decision/publication and November 4 entry; this inconsistent presentation is retained, not silently reconciled. The operative clause and XML metadata agree with the government release.

Article 12e(4) requires prompt reporting to SECO of LNG-tanker sales/ownership transfers to third countries, including counterparties and vessel identifiers. Article 20(2bis)(a) expands acquisition restrictions to cryptoasset-service entities established/incorporated under Swiss or EEA law. The control and management clauses were already present and also refer to the entities covered by letter (a).

**CH-SAN-S003:** The [official consolidated ordinance](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/151/fr), Article 20(3), exempts Swiss, EEA and UK nationals and persons holding temporary/permanent residence permits in Switzerland, the EEA or UK from paragraphs 1–2bis. The acquisition provision is therefore not a blanket prohibition on every Russian national. The original rendered Article 20 text was inspected and retained as a derived browser extraction; consolidated raw XML was not acquired.

## Exceptions and mixed mechanisms

RO 2026 601 Annex 29 extends the exception for specified Sakhalin-2-associated crude/condensate shipments to Japan through **March 31, 2028**. Annex 29a permits specified Sakhalin-2 LNG to Japan and South Korea from **November 5, 2026 through March 31, 2028**. Article 12g(4–6) provides a conditioned exception for qualifying pre-February 24, 2022 LNG contracts serving destinations outside Switzerland/EEA: duration above one year, no significant later modification, an annual ceiling based on 2025 volumes, and reporting beginning by December 7, 2026. These are defined permissions, not evidence of shipments or revenue. Extended wind-down authorizations and specified humanitarian, civilian and account-closure exceptions also qualify the restrictions; no individual authorization was acquired.

**CH-SAN-S004:** The original [RO 2026 600 XML](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2026/600/fr/xml/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-oc-2026-600-fr-xml-1.xml) supplies the separate November 4 Belarus sectoral amendment, with general commencement on November 5 under section III. Its date and commencement must not be collapsed into an additional Russia-list event or counted as a second package.

The prospective Ukrainian/European benefit mechanism is tighter restrictions on access to finance, relevant goods and maritime services, plus anti-circumvention reporting and provisions for orderly business wind-down. The energy exceptions preserve bounded lawful operating opportunities for Russian commercial participants and supply options for Japan/South Korea. These mixed channels do not establish net national gain, actual resource denial, recovered funds, lost Russian revenue or a Trump-directed bargain. No benefit score, financial-flow row or new monetary total is warranted.

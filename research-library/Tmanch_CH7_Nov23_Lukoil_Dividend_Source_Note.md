# Lukoil: declared dividend, historical outflow and foreign control

**J20-2026-123. Checked November 24, 2026.** The November 23 [shareholder-vote disclosure](https://disclosure.1prime.ru/news/-12/%7B8390C7DF-C405-4952-8B45-D8DA893716E2%7D.uif) and separate [dividend material-event filing, items 2.6–2.10](https://disclosure.1prime.ru/news/-12/%7B39042955-8B1C-47A1-9A91-DDE74C8C0D87%7D.uif) are issuer records published by a disclosure agent. Shareholders approved cash from the Russian parent's account on November 20: **RUB 535 per ordinary share**, with a **RUB 370,683,182,670 declared aggregate**, matching all 692,865,762 issued shares. Record date is December 9; payment deadlines are December 22, 2026 for registered nominees and professional trust managers and January 21, 2027 for other registered holders. The declared aggregate is not a cash disbursement. June's 585.884 million shares in circulation would imply roughly RUB 313.45 billion at the declared rate, but uses the wrong date for a confirmed payout; public records reviewed do not reconcile the two bases or identify actual payees, amounts received or blocked-account credits.

The [issuer's unaudited H1 2026 consolidated statements, cash-flow statement and Notes 5, 18 and 22](https://ai.finam.ru/report/download/r/MOEX%3ALKOH/) ([filing index, August 28](https://www.e-disclosure.ru/portal/files.aspx?id=17&type=4)) report **RUB 432.491 billion dividends paid in H1** and **RUB 259.669 billion dividend liabilities at June 30**. The first is a historical aggregate outflow; the second is a balance, not a second payment. Neither is the November declaration or a tracing of a specific receipt. Note 5 says the parent retained legal title for possible divestment but lost control of Lukoil International GmbH (LIG) and deconsolidated it and certain other foreign companies effective November 21, 2025. The issuer reports no LIG-to-Russian-group cash transfers after that date within the filing's scope and no visibility into LIG's internal cash and banking information. It reports a **RUB 1.667011 trillion impairment recognized in 2025**, not a cash outflow. No newer consolidated IFRS confirmation found; the parent's September standalone reporting is not comparable. No inference that foreign assets funded any dividend.

Separate [GL 131L track, J20-2026-114](Tmanch_CH7_Nov19_LIG_Berlin_Source_Note.md): November 3 [Reuters reporting on a revised Boehly consortium proposal](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/us-billionaire-leads-swap-plan-buy-lukoils-overseas-assets-sources-say-2026-11-03/) says an unidentified bank acting for the consortium bought frozen Lukoil securities at roughly 30 cents on the dollar from Western holders, including funds and depositary banks, and the proposed swap would escrow the securities rather than deliver them directly to Lukoil. The outlet cites unnamed people. Seller receipts, trade amounts, escrow terms, any DFC role and authorization remain unverified; the $22 billion cited was an early asset valuation, not a verified bid. Western sellers would be earlier potential recipients if those purchases are substantiated. [OFAC's November 19 FAQ](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1224) says closing needs separate authorization and sets out expectations of full severance, U.S.-jurisdiction blocking for sums owed, and no up-front windfall including via swaps. Escrow is not an approval or evidence of any proceeds reaching Lukoil. Check actual December/January dividend reports, depositary notices, signed transaction or closing authorization, and documentary settlement before booking new receipts.

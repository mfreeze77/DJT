## Original sources and legal stage

**UK-NOV12-S001:** The [FCDO November 12 notice](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6af5ad18cc0dbfec767a509f/Sanctions_Notice__Russia__12_November_2026.pdf) says twenty entries were added on November 12 and are now subject to their listed restrictions; one existing entry was varied. The additions are **nine entities and eleven ships**, not twenty-one new designations. New identifiers are RUS3792–RUS3811. This establishes implemented legal restrictions, not independent proof of the government's underlying conduct allegations, observed blocking, prevented shipments or lost revenue.

Entity entries: JSC NIPIGAZPERERABOTKA (RUS3792); PJSC SIBUR HOLDING (RUS3793); FORTEZA TRADING DMCC (RUS3794); PJSC OIL AND GAS COMPANY SLAVNEFT (RUS3800); JOINT STOCK COMPANY UGOLNAYA KOMPANIYA KUZBASSRAZREZUGOL (RUS3801); JOINT-STOCK COMPANY "SIBERIAN COAL ENERGY COMPANY" (RUS3802); PATERA FZCO (RUS3803); NOVUS ENERGY DMCC (RUS3804); LLC Vostok Oil (RUS3809). Their sanction headings specify asset freeze, trust services, director disqualification and transport sanctions with entry-specific detail. Seven vessel headings say **Shipping & Trade Sanctions**; RUS3805–RUS3808 say **Shipping sanctions**. Those four entries' Other Information also describes conditional trade prohibitions, so the heading distinction is not a finding that they are categorically exempt from trade restrictions. Do not call all eleven asset-frozen, silently harmonize labels or ignore exceptions/licences. The [structured legal-list reference](Tmanch_CH7_Nov12_UK_List_Reference.json) preserves exact headings and identifiers without copying statements of reasons.

**UK-NOV12-S002:** The [November 12 government release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukraine-set-to-be-focus-of-epc-summit-as-uk-sanctions-russias-top-ten-oil-producers) describes energy and transport pressure and says Slavneft completes UK coverage of Russia's top ten oil producers. The top-ten/first-country characterization is government-reported, not an independently audited market census. No quantified loss or financial receipt follows from it.

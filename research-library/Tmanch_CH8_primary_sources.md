# Chapter 8 Primary Sources and Source Controls

## Tier A — official and primary records

### CH8-SRC-001 — 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment
- Institution: ODNI / CIA / FBI / NSA
- Supports: Putin ordered an influence campaign; Russian goals included undermining faith in U.S. democracy, harming Clinton, and helping Trump where possible.
- Limit: did not assess electoral outcome.

### CH8-SRC-002 — Senate Intelligence Committee Volume 5
- Institution: U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence
- URL: https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/sites/default/files/documents/report_volume5.pdf
- Supports: Manafort/Kilimnik counterintelligence findings; possible Kilimnik connection to GRU hack-and-leak; June 9 findings; kompromat risk section; limitations and redactions.
- Limit: significant passages remain redacted; Committee did not establish every alleged relationship.

### CH8-SRC-003 — Senate Intelligence Committee Volume 5 release / Rubio statement
- URL: https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/2020/08/18/press-rubio-statement-senate-intel-release-volume-5-bipartisan-russia-report/
- Supports: bipartisan finding of aggressive multi-faceted Russian interference; Manafort proximity created opportunities for Russian intelligence; June 9 participants had significant Russian-government/intelligence connections.
- Limit: Rubio's statement also states the Committee found no evidence of a Trump-campaign agreement with the Russian government to meddle; that counterevidence is preserved.

### CH8-SRC-004 — Treasury election-interference sanctions, April 15, 2021
- URL: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy0126
- Supports: Kilimnik is a known Russian Intelligence Services agent; during the 2016 campaign he provided Russian intelligence with sensitive polling and campaign-strategy information.
- Limit: does not publicly disclose downstream operational use.

### CH8-SRC-005 — DOJ OIG 2018 Midyear report
- URL: https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/2019-12/2018-06-14.pdf
- Supports: classified information mentioning Lynch and fear of a leak affected Comey's decision process; OIG found the July 5 unilateral statement inconsistent with DOJ policy and practice and found Comey's justifications unpersuasive.
- Limit: public report protects important classified details.

### CH8-SRC-006 — Senate Intelligence active-measures report landing page

## Tier C — investigative lead generator

### CH8-SRC-010 — David Corn, *How Russia Won* (2026)
- Site: https://howrussiawon.com/
- Role: lead generator and secondary synthesis.
- Supports: identifies underweighted public records and reports additional details.
- Control: no new Corn claim is treated as established without independent corroboration.

## Source-control rules

1. The Senate report and Treasury finding control the Manafort–Kilimnik chain.
2. The specific Facebook/IRA/GRU use of a named campaign file remains unresolved unless a routing record is produced.
3. The Comey episode is described as institutional effect from dubious Russian intelligence, not as proved Russian control of Comey.
4. Kompromat allegations remain allegations unless independently proved.
5. Real FBI/FISA errors are preserved as counterevidence; they do not erase independently established Russian operations.

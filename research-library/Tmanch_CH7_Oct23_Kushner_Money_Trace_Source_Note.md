### 2. Watani and Dherar existed in the alleged period, but legal form must be dated

**KMT-S002:** [Dubai Protocol Department, June 12, 2017](https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/hamdan-bin-rashid-honors-the-winners-of-watani-al-emarat-humanitarian-work-award/) names Dherar Belhoul Al Falasi as Director-General of Watani Al Emarat Foundation at its humanitarian awards ceremony. This establishes the role and public use of the foundation name during 2017. It does not establish an account, signatory mandate or covert activity.

**KMT-S003:** [Dubai Law No. 7 of 2018](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2018/Law%20No.%20(7)%20of%202018%20Establishing%20Watani%20Al%20Emarat%20Foundation.html), issued and effective May 26, 2018. Article 3 establishes the public-benefit legal person; Article 14 transfers the Watani Programme's rights, assets, obligations, responsibilities, powers and employees to it. Articles 9 and 13 provide for board-approved financial statements/accounting rules, auditor appointment and reports; Article 9(b) requires specified written delegations. Article 11 addresses executive representation/contracts. The [Arabic original](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Ar%20Reference/2018/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20(7)%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202018.html) was inspected and agrees on the date and succession provision; Arabic controls legally.

The 2018 incorporation does **not** prove that a 2017 account under the public-facing Watani name was impossible: the contemporary 2017 record and statutory predecessor are affirmative evidence of earlier activity. The actual holder could only be settled from account-opening and succession documents. Do not silently substitute the later legal person for its predecessor.

### 3. A later law identifies where to look for assets and successor records

**KMT-S004:** [Dubai Law No. 27 of 2024](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Law%20No.%20(27)%20of%202024%20Dissolving%20Watani%20Al%20Emarat%20Foundation.pdf), issued October 22, 2024, pp.1–3. Article 1 dissolves the 2018 foundation; Article 2 assigns a steering committee to determine successor entities, property/asset positions and budget treatment. Article 3 sets a settlement period, subject to extension. Article 5 makes publication, not issuance alone, the commencement trigger. The exact Gazette publication date and completion of settlement were not established here.

**KMT-S005:** [Executive Council Resolution No. 85 of 2024](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2024/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(85)%20of%202024%20Forming%20the%20Steering%20for%20Watani%20Al%20Emarat%20Foundation.pdf), issued October 30, 2024, pp.2–5. Article 2 names officeholder roles on the steering committee, chaired by the Executive Council Secretary General. Article 3 covers foundation and affiliate asset/obligation transfers and implementation plans; Article 6 requires government cooperation/documents; Article 7 requires a final report through the General Secretariat. This identifies records and responsible institutions to search, not publicly available account balances or proved destruction/concealment.

The dissolution PDF visibly has a **2024 title/body/issuance date but a 2025 running footer**. Preserve the discrepancy rather than silently converting the law date. The separate committee resolution expressly cites Law 27/2024. Both English translations say Arabic controls. A dissolution instrument must not be treated as evidence that all brand activity stopped, that assets were illicit, or that an alleged account was closed.

### 4. Bank of Israel: narrow the legal question, do not overstate the source

**KMT-S006:** [Bank of Israel Law, official-hosted unofficial English translation](https://www.boi.org.il/media/s2tb3h2w/bank-of-israel-law.pdf), Sections 48 and 50, printed pp.35–37. Section 48 addresses government banking; Section 50 also permits accounts for banking corporations and other financial entities, and approved foreign central banks, monetary authorities and foreign banks. The [Bank's own government-banking explanation](https://www.boi.org.il/en/bank-of-israel/about-the-bank-of-israel/the-bank-of-israels-functions/acting-as-the-banker-of-the-government/) describes transfers, daily statements and balance confirmations for government ministries.

Thus a categorical statement that the central bank holds **only government funds** is too narrow. The permitted institutional-account categories do not establish an ordinary private individual account or the reported Beta mechanism. No primary dual-key mandate, Netanyahu instruction or actual transfer was acquired. Required precision: legal account holder, authorized signatories, beneficiary, account class and applicable contemporaneous rules. This qualification does not establish or disprove the reported funds.

## What remains untraced, and the next records that would change the finding

No Azerbaijani bank is named in the inspected excerpt, and no independent public record reviewed here identifies that missing bank or a corresponding debit/credit leg. Do not substitute a bank from a separate laundering case simply because the country matches.

1. **Institution identity and succession:** pre-May-2018 Watani Programme holder records, 2018 transfer schedules, board appointment/delegation minutes, later steering committee inventory/transfer plan/final report and identified successors.
2. **Account authority:** original opening/KYC documents, beneficial-owner declarations, board-approved account/signatory mandates, amendments and closure/migration instructions. A signatory, legal owner and ultimate beneficiary are different roles.
3. **Money movement:** paired bank debit/credit entries with value dates, currencies, reference identifiers, intermediary/correspondent banks, exchange rates and fees. Reconcile a purported upstream deposit with downstream disbursement rather than adding both as separate funding.
4. **Control checkpoints:** who approved a transaction, who reconciled it, whether oversight bodies/auditors received it, and the first documented warning and response. The statutory existence of controls is not proof they operated; missing public minutes are not proof the controls were bypassed.
5. **Disclosure and official authority:** inspect actual applicable disclosures and authorization records before making a personal nondisclosure or illegality finding. Bank accounts, public ethics forms, confidential filings and foreign-account reporting are not interchangeable datasets.

No records request, third-party contact, purchase, account creation or private-bank access was attempted.

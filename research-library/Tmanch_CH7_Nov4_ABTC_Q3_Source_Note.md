# November 4 American Bitcoin Q3 disclosure

**CH7-ABTC-Q3-001** records bounded family-company context. It is not a presidential action, a Russian-linked transaction or an additional financial-flow total. No score is assigned.

## Reported corporate results and asset boundaries

**ABTC-Q3-S001:** The [November 4 original issuer release](https://abtc.com/news-and-insights/american-bitcoin-reports-third-quarter-2026-results) identifies Eric Trump as co-founder and chief strategy officer. It reports that American Bitcoin issued no shares through its ATM program and purchased no Bitcoin during the quarter. These statements concern that quarter and program, not all financing or earlier purchases.

**ABTC-Q3-S002:** The [September 30 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755953/000119312526447413/abtc-20260930.htm), unaudited statements of operations, printed page 4, reports Q3 **revenue $63.355 million**, **net income $125.987 million**, digital-asset gain $197.340 million and derivatives loss $64.276 million. These convert its USD-thousands table. The separate nine-month net loss is **$12.956 million**.

Note 6 (pages 17–18) reports **8,427 Bitcoin** at September 30, including **3,090 pledged to Bitmain**. MD&A, “Liquidity and Capital Resources” (page 39), reports approximately **465 Bitcoin sold in Q3 for $36.3 million** for operating expenses. This sale is sourced to the filing, not the release.

Corporate assets, income, gains and proceeds are different, non-additive measures, not family distributions. Pledged Bitcoin are a reserve subset. This review does not classify the entire digital-asset gain as cash or unrealized.

## Dated ownership bridge

**ABTC-Q3-S003:** The [definitive proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1755953/000119312526178754/abtc-20260424.htm), pages 21–22, lists Eric Trump with **285,000 Class A and 68,147,664 Class B shares** as of **April 10, 2026**. Its **9.3%** represents Class B ownership and total voting power, not total equity. Footnote 9 associates the same position with his revocable trust, for which he is trustee and a beneficiary. These are not additive holdings.

The figures precede the **July 2 one-for-15 reverse split** disclosed in S001. No current stake value is calculated. Proxy page 27 says no compensation **under the Advisory Agreement**; this does not establish no compensation from every role or no other receipts.

## Interpretation and provenance

The records establish issuer disclosures and a dated ownership connection. They do not establish a governmental exchange, foreign-government funding, a Russian benefit, a family cash receipt or a policy-linked transfer. Company operating and reserve changes may affect shareholder exposure, but attributable personal gains and national effects are unestablished.

The issuer release, quarterly report and proxy are one issuer source family, not independent corroboration. The quarterly statements are unaudited. Missing records include current beneficial ownership, any later advisory amendments, transaction-level settlement evidence and identifiable personal distributions.
